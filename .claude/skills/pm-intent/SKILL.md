# PM intent

Use this while a batch is being agreed or when its purpose has become unclear.

1. Read the originating conversation or decision, issue, existing scope, plan row and any completion evidence. Separate Kyle's actual words from later technical interpretation.
2. Explain plainly to Kyle what problem the batch addresses, why it matters to profitable and risk-contained trading, what changes at the system-behaviour level, and what happens if it is skipped. Name any uncertainty.
3. Continue until Kyle's intended outcome and meaning of done are unambiguous. Technical implementation choices remain with the owner and reviewers; placement requires `pm-slotting`.
4. Add one entry to `1-system-manual/pm/INTENT_REGISTER.md` with batch id, owner, date, source, Kyle's own words, a clearly labelled plain-language explanation, what done means to him, and links to the scope and completion report.

Quote only words Kyle actually used. A paraphrase is labelled `PM explanation`, never presented as his quote. If Kyle delegates the intent to an agreed group recommendation, cite both his delegation and that recommendation.

When intent changes, keep the earlier entry, mark it superseded by the dated replacement, and link both. Do not silently rewrite history.
