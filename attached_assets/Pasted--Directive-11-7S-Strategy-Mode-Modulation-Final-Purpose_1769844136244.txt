4.2 Stop Distance Design Rationale (MANDATORY COMMENT)

Important:
Stop distance multipliers > 1.0 intentionally widen stops in DEFENSIVE and SURVIVAL modes.

This is a professional volatility response:

Risk is controlled via position size reduction

Wider stops prevent whipsaw stop-outs in unstable markets

Do not tighten stops in volatile regimes — that would increase loss frequency.

This comment must exist in code near the overlay logic.
