Objective

To remove all unnecessary components introduced in or after Directive 8.8.4-C.14.D, including the C15A validation and reset APIs, redundant health monitors, and auxiliary middleware that have destabilized the trading system.

This rollback returns the application to the stable, proven architecture that existed after Directive 8.8.4-C.14.C — ensuring the Ready-to-Buy (RTB) table, trading engine, and FX5 scanner operate in a synchronized, reliable state.
