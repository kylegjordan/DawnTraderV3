1 | Objectives

This directive introduces two major upgrades:

Decision Confidence Engine (DCE) — a unified model combining all major confidence signals (CWQI, NGC, ML Confidence, Regime Score, MACO Consensus) into one composite Decision Index (DI) used to prioritize trades and exposures.

Liquidity Trap Strategy Agent (Agent 9) — completes the MACO ensemble by adding the missing reinforcement learner specialized in illiquidity exploitation.
