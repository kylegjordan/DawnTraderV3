5A. Global Metric Definitions
Metric	Simple Explanation
Global Regime	This tells you what kind of overall market environment we’re in — for example, trending upward (bull), downward (bear), flat, or changing. It helps you understand how most markets are behaving.
Global Friction	This shows how easy or difficult it is to trade across the markets. A low score means trades can happen quickly and cheaply. A high score means trading is slower or more expensive.
5B. Regime Types
Regime	Meaning in Simple Terms
Bull Stable	Markets are trending upward steadily. Buyers are in control, and momentum strategies work best.
Bear Volatile	Markets are dropping sharply or swinging wildly. It’s better to be cautious or trade short setups.
Low Vol Chop	Prices move sideways without clear direction. Range trading works best here.
High Vol Impulse	Prices move very fast and sharply. Short-term breakout and scalp trades work well.
Transition	The market is changing direction or finding a new trend. Keep trades small and flexible.
5C. Friction Types
Friction Type	Meaning in Simple Terms
High Liquidity / Low Cost (0–20)	Trades are cheap and fast. Ideal environment.
Moderate Liquidity (21–50)	Trading is normal and smooth.
Limited Liquidity (51–80)	Fewer buyers and sellers; trades take longer or move prices.
High Cost / Frozen (81–100)	Markets are expensive or paused. Best to wait.
