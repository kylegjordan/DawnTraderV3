🧮 Guardrail Dynamic Slot Calculation

Replace hard-coded slot constant (MAX_SLOTS = 15) in
server/core/guardrails.ts with dynamic computation:

this.maxSlots = Math.floor(this.maxExposure / this.maxPosition);


Example:
If maxExposure = 100% and maxPosition = 12%, then maxSlots = 8.

This ensures accurate capacity limits for every portfolio size and exposure profile.
