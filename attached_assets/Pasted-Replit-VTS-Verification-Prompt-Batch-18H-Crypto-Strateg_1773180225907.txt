Check 3: Skip Reason Analysis
# What are the skip reasons post-18H?
# Fewer strategy-gate rejections = thresholds working
cat logs/vts_skipped_signals/2026-03-11.json 2>/dev/null | python3 -c "
import json, sys
from collections import Counter
data = json.load(sys.stdin)
reasons = Counter()
for s in data:
    for r in s.get('reason','').split('|'):
        if r.strip():
            reasons[r.strip()] += 1
for reason, count in reasons.most_common():
    print(f'  {reason}: {count}')
" 2>/dev/null || echo "No data"

Report: Skip reason breakdown. Are signals now getting further through the pipeline (failing at SQE/governance rather than at strategy-level gates)?
