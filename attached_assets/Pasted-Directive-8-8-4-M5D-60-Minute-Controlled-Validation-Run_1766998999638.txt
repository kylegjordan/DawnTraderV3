### **⚙️ Execution Procedure**

#### **Phase A — VTS Simulation**
1. **Start Simulation**


POST /api/vts/validation/run-vts?duration=60

2. System runs in autonomous mode for 60 minutes:
- 1 cycle / 60 seconds × 20 pairs ≈ 1 200 simulated signals potential.
- Each trade saved to `/data/vts_trades_<timestamp>.json`.
3. Metrics logged every 15 seconds:
- CWQI, NGC, DI, GSI, adaptive relevance, feed latency.

#### **Phase B — Paper Trading**
1. Toggle paper trading **ON** (`tradingActive = true`).
2. Run 60 minutes under identical portfolio/risk configuration:


POST /api/vts/validation/run-paper?duration=60

3. Paper trades recorded to `/data/paper_trades_<timestamp>.json` through `recordPaperTrade()` hook.

#### **Phase C — Auto Comparison & Report Generation**
1. At session end, system automatically triggers:


/api/vts/validation/compare-vts-live

2. Generates consolidated report:
`/reports/VTS_Paper_Comparison_<timestamp>.json`
3. Extract summary with:


GET /api/vts/validation/latest-comparison
