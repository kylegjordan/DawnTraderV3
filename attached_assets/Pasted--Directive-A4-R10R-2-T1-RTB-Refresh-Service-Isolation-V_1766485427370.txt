🧩 Directive A4.R10R-2.T1 – RTB Refresh Service Isolation Validation
Objective

To verify that the Ready-To-Buy (RTB) Refresh Service, operating in its temporary isolated state (with an internal 15-second timer and no Central Clock dependency), performs all expected refresh, reconfirmation, and ranking operations correctly.

This test validates that:

The service runs autonomously on its own timer loop.

Every micro-cycle completes within expected time bounds (<1 s typical).

All signals in the RTB pool are refreshed and re-ranked within 120 s total coverage.

PriceCache and FX5 remain unaffected during RTB refresh cycles.

No drift, overlap, or resource contention occurs between RTB cycles and FX5 scan cycles.

Test Scope

This is an investigative validation only — no code changes are to be made.

Step-by-Step Instructions for Replit
1️⃣ Initialize Paper Trading Engine
npm run start:paper


Ensure the workflow fully starts. Wait for confirmation of:

[A4.R10R-1][PriceCache][HEALTH]
[A4.R10R-2][RTBRefresh][CYCLE_START]
[A4.R10R-2][RTBRefresh][CYCLE_COMPLETE]

2️⃣ Confirm RTB Refresh Autonomy

Over a 5-minute observation window:

Verify that [A4.R10R-2][RTBRefresh][CYCLE_START] appears every ≈ 15 seconds.

Verify that [CYCLE_COMPLETE] logs always follow [CYCLE_START] within ≤ 1000 ms.

Confirm that no CentralClock tick or FX5 scan event precedes these logs (proving timer independence).

3️⃣ Verify Refresh Coverage

Use RTB service diagnostics or database queries:

readyToBuyService.count() → Number of active signals

For each signal, check the lastRefreshed timestamp

Confirm that no signal remains unrefreshed > 120 seconds

4️⃣ Validate Price Cache Interaction

Inspect logs for:

[A4.R10R-1][PriceCache][BATCH_FETCH] source=readyToBuy


Confirm:

Batches correspond to each RTB refresh cycle

weightedRequests per second never exceed 10

source=readyToBuy indicates all refreshes are via the centralized cache (no Kraken API direct calls)

5️⃣ Observe FX5 Scanner Independence

Ensure [FX5Scanner][COMPLETE] continues every 30 s while RTB cycles run.
Confirm that:

FX5 logs do not show waiting or blocking between cycles.

No shared log timestamp overlaps that would indicate sequencing dependency.

6️⃣ Runtime Stability Checks

CPU load: < 60 % average

Memory: steady (no leak > 10 MB across 5 min window)

Timer drift: < 500 ms after 5 minutes (compare CYCLE_START deltas)
