Directive 8.8.4-C.8 — Validation Trading Execution & System Performance Audit
Objective

To perform a controlled validation trading session to confirm that the unified signal flow, CWQI-based ranking, RTB lifecycle, and TCL promotion logic are all operating correctly and efficiently under live simulation conditions.

This directive verifies full operational readiness before transitioning to semi-live or live trading states.

Verify Pre-Session Conditions

Phase 2 — Validation Trading Session

Run the Engine for 10–15 Minutes

Allow the FX5 scanner to perform at least 15 cycles (≈7 minutes 30 s).

Continue until at least 100 SQE-qualified signals populate the RTB pool.

Observe and Record the Following:

Signal Flow Integrity

Verify that every new SQE-qualified signal enters the unified RTB table.

Confirm CWQI and Confidence fields populate correctly.

Ranking and Re-confirmation

Observe CWQI re-ranking every 30 seconds.

Verify that expired or unqualified signals are removed on schedule.

TCL Promotion Logic

Once ≥ 100 signals OR 5 minutes elapsed, TCL should trigger:

[8.8.4-C.6][TCL_ACTIVATE]   or
[8.8.4-C.6][TCL_FALLBACK_ACTIVATE]


Confirm promotions to Active Trades occur automatically based on highest CWQI.

Trade Lifecycle

RTB → Active Trades → Closed → Trade History progression should occur cleanly.

Closed trades should persist correctly in trade_history.

Performance Monitoring

Capture engine performance metrics:

CPU < 70 %

Refresh latency < 5 s

No skipped FX5 cycles

Record [RTB_REFRESH] log timestamps to confirm consistent 30 s cadence.

Phase 3 — Data Verification

Database Snapshot

Run the following SQL checks and include results in the report:

SELECT COUNT(*), MIN(queued_at), MAX(queued_at) FROM rtb_signals;
SELECT COUNT(*), MIN(created_at), MAX(created_at) FROM paper_trades;
SELECT COUNT(*), MIN(closed_at), MAX(closed_at) FROM trade_history;


Expected:

Non-zero results for all three.

Timestamps advancing in correct chronological order.

Log Verification

Confirm presence of log tags:

[8.8.4-C.5][RTB_INSERT]
[8.8.4-C.5][RTB_REFRESH]
[8.8.4-C.6][TCL_ACTIVATE] or [TCL_FALLBACK_ACTIVATE]
[8.8.4-C.8][VALIDATION_SUMMARY]


Ensure no duplicate refresh or overlapping TCL activation logs.

UI Validation

The “Ready to Buy” tab must show a single unified table, continuously updating CWQI values.

Active Trades and Trade History tabs must populate appropriately as promotions and closures occur.
