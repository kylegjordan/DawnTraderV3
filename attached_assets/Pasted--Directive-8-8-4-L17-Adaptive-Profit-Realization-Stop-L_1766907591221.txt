1 | Purpose

Directive L17 adds dynamic, intelligent trade exit optimization.
It enables the system to adapt profit-taking and stop-loss thresholds in real time based on the Decision Index, volatility conditions, and regime-specific confidence levels.

This module closes the intelligence feedback loop — linking predictive learning, regime awareness, and execution discipline.

4 | Dynamic Re-Optimization Logic

Executed at each signal evaluation and order refresh:

if (ΔDI > 0.1 && trendStable)
    widenTP()
else if (ΔDI < -0.1 || driftDetected)
    tightenSL()


The APR-SLE maintains rolling averages of DI slope (ΔDI) to capture momentum.
