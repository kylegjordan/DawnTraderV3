✅ **Directive: Phase 8.8.3 — AJ16

RTB Cooling Diagnostic (Why Ready-To-Buy Stops After 10 Minutes)**

Objective:
Identify and fix the exact cause(s) of the observed behavior where:

RTB signals initially appear

Several trades open

RTB quickly dries up to zero

Some RTBs appear briefly but disappear before trading engine processes them

Meanwhile 20–70 active pool pairs remain eligible but produce no RTB signals

This behavior indicates a systemic flow issue, not just strategy randomness.

🟦 AJ16.1 — Instrument Strategy Outputs (Signal Emit Logging)

Add detailed logging inside each strategy module immediately before a signal is returned.

Log includes:

[AJ16][STRATEGY_SIGNAL]
pair=BTC/USD
strategy=VWAP_PULLBACK
price=42103.11
cooldownOk=true
riskOk=true
signalValue=1.0
reason="met all criteria"
timestamp=...


If no signal is returned, log:

[AJ16][STRATEGY_NO_SIGNAL]
pair=ETH/EUR
strategy=DHMA
reason="failed trend strength"
trendStrength=0.14
cooldownOk=true
stopRuleOk=true
timestamp=...


Every strategy must emit one or the other every cycle.

🟦 AJ16.2 — Instrument Cooldowns (Internal + Guardrail)

Every symbol needs:

[AJ16][COOLDOWN_CHECK]
symbol=ADA/USD
internalCooldown=true|false
guardrailCooldown=true|false
cooldownRemaining=180sec


This reveals:

✓ internal strategy cooldowns (hidden from Goals Engine!)
✓ guardrail-enforced cooldowns
✓ mismatches between UI cooldown (5 min) and strategy cooldown logic

This is a required step.

🟦 AJ16.3 — Active-Position Exclusion Logging

Log whenever a symbol is skipped because an open position exists:

[AJ16][ACTIVE_POSITION_EXCLUDE]
symbol=XRP/USD
reason="symbol has active trade"


This tells us whether:

32 open trades eliminate 32 symbols automatically

this is starving RTB flow

this needs to be redesigned later
(we can allow multiple sequential trades per symbol in a later phase — Phase 9 or 10)

🟦 AJ16.4 — Strategy Input Sanity Logging (Indicators)

At least once per cycle, log:

[AJ16][INDICATOR_STATUS]
pair=LINK/USD
rsi=54.1
atr=0.0143
vwap=12.24
ma50=12.19
ma200=11.92
dataAge=0.23s


Why?

Because many historical “no signals” bugs came from:

an indicator not updating

stale data

missing bars

mismatched TFs

NaN values creating silent failures

This is required to verify the strategy inputs are valid.

🟦 AJ16.5 — RTB Generation Logging (Final Gate)

Add logging at the exact point where a filtered pair becomes RTB:

[AJ16][BECAME_RTB]
pair=ATOM/USD
strategy=VWAP_BOUNCE
confidence=78
reason="Met all criteria"


And when the pair is rejected at the last stage:

[AJ16][RTB_REJECT]
pair=SUSHI/USD
reason="Stop too tight" | "Cooldown" | "Confidence < threshold"


These are critical signals for diagnosing flow.

🟦 AJ16.6 — Snapshot Export Every 5 Minutes

Every 5 minutes, dump:

of active filtered pairs
of pairs producing signals
of pairs failing cooldown
of pairs failing strategy
of pairs failing guardrails
of pairs becoming RTB

This creates a heatmap of system behavior over time.

🟦 AJ16.7 — Ensure Logs Tag CycleId

Everything must be tied to:

cycleId = scanTickId or tradingTickId


This allows replay analysis.

🟦 AJ16.8 — Produce the AJ16 Diagnostic Report

Replit must deliver a full markdown report showing:

1. Which strategies are producing signals reliably
2. Which strategies are failing criteria most often
3. Whether cooldowns (internal or guardrail) are suppressing signals
4. Whether Active Position Exclusion is suppressing signals
5. Whether indicator feeds are stable
6. Why RTB flow declines after ~10 minutes

The report must be placed in:

docs/audits/phase_8.8.3-AJ16_RTB_cooling_diagnostic.md

🟩 Expected Output / Success Criteria

RTB should never go to 0 unless:

market conditions are truly flat

all signals are failing due to real data

cooldown suppresses temporarily

If RTB constantly = zero:

A bug exists

A strategy misfire exists

A data flow issue exists

A guardrail is overfiring

Or Active Position Exclusion is choking the market

AJ16 pinpoints which one.
