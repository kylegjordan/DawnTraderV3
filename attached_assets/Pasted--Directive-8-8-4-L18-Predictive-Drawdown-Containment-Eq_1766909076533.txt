1 | Purpose

Directive L18 adds an adaptive drawdown-control system and an equity-curve stabilizer that anticipate risk deterioration before it materializes.

The Predictive Drawdown Containment (PDC) module learns patterns that historically precede equity declines, while the Equity Curve Smoothing (ECS) system dynamically moderates exposure to prevent volatile swings and protect capital during uncertain regimes.

This directive makes the trading system self-regulating — learning from performance fluctuations to prevent extended loss streaks.
