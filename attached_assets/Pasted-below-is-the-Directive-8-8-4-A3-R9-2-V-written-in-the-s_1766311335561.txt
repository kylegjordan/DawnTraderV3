Phase: 8.8.4 — Signal Integrity & Quality Normalization
Directive ID: A3.R9.2.V
Classification: Validation / Observational
Objective: Confirm that all R9.2 Integrity Rebuild fixes are functioning as intended, that the SQE correctly rejects poor-quality signals, and that NGC/CWQI normalization is now producing realistic variance.

🧩 1. Objectives

Validate the full signal pipeline post-R9.2:
Signal Orchestrator → SQE → RTB → TCL → Diagnostics

Verify that:

SQE rejection rate is within 40–55 %

NGC and CWQI averages fall between 0.63 – 0.69

Reconfirmation events appear in RTB logs

No duplicates exist between RTB and Open Trades

Atomic promotion removes RTB entries only after confirmed trade creation

Diagnostic trace remains non-blocking (< 1 ms flush latency)

🧠 2. Procedure
Step 1 – Prepare System

Ensure trading engine is in idle state.

Clear any residual signal cache (if applicable).

Confirm diagnostics are ready:

GET /api/diagnostics/trace/status


Should return "enabled": false.

Step 2 – Start Diagnostic Tracing
POST /api/diagnostics/trace/start
{
  "durationMinutes": 15,
  "maxSizeMB": 2,
  "phase": "8.8.4-A3.R9.2.V",
  "autoDisable": true
}


Starts non-blocking trace with 2-second async flush interval.

Target output: /logs/diagnostic/trace_A3R9_V.log

Step 3 – Activate Paper Trading Session
POST /api/paper-sim/start


Runs full trading cycle in paper mode.

Duration: 15 minutes (auto-stop optional, manual stop acceptable).

All Central Clock subscribers (FX5Scanner, RTB, TCL) should become active.

Step 4 – Observe & Log

During the run, monitor console output for the following diagnostic tags:

Tag	Meaning
[A3.R9.2][DECAY_ORDER_FIX]	Decay applied before normalization

[A3.R9.2][SQE_REVALIDATION_FAIL]	Signal rejected during revalidation
[A3.R9.2][PROMOTION_ATOMIC]	Trade created & RTB removed atomically
[A3.R9.2][METRICS]	Periodic performance snapshot

No synchronous or blocking events should appear.

Step 5 – Stop Tracing and Trading

After 15 minutes (or auto-disable trigger):

POST /api/diagnostics/trace/stop
POST /api/paper-sim/stop

Step 6 – Collect Artifacts

Retrieve the following files from the server or repo:
