Directive 8.8.4-C.5 — SQE→RTB Integration and TCL Warm-Up Control
Context

Following completion of Directive 8.8.4-C.4 (RTB Queue & Metrics Integrity Audit), it was confirmed that:

The SQE (Signal Quality Evaluator) is functioning correctly and identifying high-quality, strategy-filtered signals.

The Ready-to-Buy (RTB) queue service is fully implemented but not currently wired into the signal pipeline.

The Ready-to-Buy tab in the UI contains two tables (“Ready-to-Buy Signals” and “RTB Queue”), but only one unified pool is needed.

The TCL (Trade Capacity Limit) currently activates immediately after the engine starts, which can cause premature trades before sufficient market data has been gathered.

This directive establishes a single, unified Ready-to-Buy pool, connects SQE-qualified signals into that pool, and introduces a TCL warm-up delay to ensure sufficient market data density before trades are initiated.

Objective

To complete the Ready-to-Buy and TCL integration flow so that:

All SQE-qualified signals are inserted into one unified RTB pool.

The RTB pool maintains dynamic CWQI-based ranking and continuous requalification.

TCL only activates once sufficient data (≥100 SQE-qualified signals) is present to ensure optimal trade selection.

Tasks for Replit Implementation
1. Remove Redundant RTB Table

Remove the lower “RTB Queue” table from the Ready-to-Buy tab in the UI.

Retain only the original upper “Ready-to-Buy Signals” table as the single unified pool.

Ensure this table displays:

Symbol

Strategy

Confidence

NGC

CWQI

Expected Return

Risk

Profit Rate

Timestamp (last refreshed)

2. Connect SQE → RTB Pool

In server/core/filters/signal_quality_evaluator.ts or equivalent orchestration file:

After a signal passes SQE, immediately forward it to the ReadyToBuyService.queueSignal() method.

Include all relevant metrics (CWQI, NGC, Confidence, ExpectedReturn, ProfitRate, etc.).

Confirm the RTB pool persists signals correctly to the rtb_signals table.

Ensure signals remain in the pool even if all trade slots are filled.

3. Enable Continuous RTB Maintenance

RTB should re-evaluate each signal every 30 seconds (aligned with FX5 scanner cadence):

Re-calculate CWQI and risk metrics.

Remove any expired or invalid signals.

Maintain continuous CWQI-based ranking.

4. Introduce TCL Warm-Up Delay (100-Signal Threshold)

In server/core/rtb/ready_to_buy_service.ts or paper-execution-engine.ts, implement a TCL activation guard:

Condition: TCL will remain inactive until at least 100 SQE-qualified signals exist in the Ready-to-Buy pool.

Logic:

if (readyToBuyPool.size < 100) {
  logger.info("[8.8.4-C.5][TCL_WARMUP] TCL delayed — insufficient RTB population (" + readyToBuyPool.size + " signals)");
  return;
}


Once ≥100 signals exist, TCL automatically transitions to “active” and begins promoting top-ranked signals into trades.

Add logging tag:
[8.8.4-C.5][TCL_WARMUP] for visibility in the logs.

5. Ensure Proper Promotion and Capacity Handling

Once TCL is active:

Continuously check available trade slots.

Promote the top CWQI-ranked signals from the RTB pool to the Active Trades list.

Update trade creation to record source = "RTB_POOL".

When a trade slot frees up, promote the next best signal automatically.

6. Runtime Diagnostics & Logging

Add logging for traceability:

[8.8.4-C.5][RTB_INSERT] when SQE adds a new signal to RTB.

[8.8.4-C.5][RTB_REFRESH] when signals are re-evaluated or dropped.

[8.8.4-C.5][TCL_ACTIVATE] when TCL becomes active.

[8.8.4-C.5][TCL_PROMOTE] when a signal is promoted to an active trade.

Expected Outcome

Once implemented and verified:

The Ready-to-Buy tab will display all SQE-qualified signals in one table.

The RTB pool will continuously refresh and rank signals by CWQI.

The TCL will activate only after ≥100 SQE-qualified signals exist, ensuring optimal selection quality.

Promotions to Active Trades will occur automatically as trade capacity becomes available.

Logging tags will confirm every stage of the flow is operational.
