🧩 Directive 8.8.4-C.4 — RTB Queue & Metrics Integrity Audit
Objective

Perform a comprehensive diagnostic audit of the Ready-to-Buy (RTB) subsystem — including its queue, signal promotion logic, and statistical tracking — to verify accuracy and integrity following the removal of legacy trade-creation paths under Directive 8.8.4-C.3.

⚠️ Important:
This directive is analysis-only.
Do not modify, refactor, or remove any code, configuration, or database entries at this stage.
Your role is to investigate, analyze, and report your findings and recommendations.

Background Context

Following completion of Directive 8.8.4-C.3, all legacy trade-creation paths (scanForSignals, checkSymbolForSignal, and injectForcedTrade) have been deprecated and archived.
Now, every trade in the system should originate from the Signal Orchestrator → SQE → RTB → TCL → Trade Execution flow.

However, several operational anomalies remain:

RTB Statistics Mismatch

The Ready2BuyMetrics panel shows:

~60 trades opened

~3,000 signals rejected

~2,655 rejected specifically by “Max Trades”

These rejected counts do not align with the actual contents of Ready2BuySignals or Ready2BuyQueue.

Empty Ready2BuyQueue Table

The Ready2BuyQueue table (visible in UI and database) remains empty even though signals are being processed.

Historically, this queue was used to hold pending signals prioritized by CWQI decay, awaiting TCL promotion.

Missing Ready2BuySignals Entries

Rejected and promoted signals are not consistently logged in Ready2BuySignals.

The discrepancy suggests potential issues in:

ready_to_buy_service.ts (enqueue/dequeue logic)

signal_quality_evaluator.ts (filter rejection handling)

or a stats aggregation layer (RTB metrics counters).

Ghost Trade Correlation

Earlier “ghost trades” may have originated from legacy paths that bypassed RTB completely.

With those paths now disabled, this audit ensures no remnants or internal miscounts persist.

Audit Scope

You are to perform a non-destructive analysis across the following modules:

Module	Purpose	Key Audit Targets
server/core/rtb/ready_to_buy_service.ts	RTB queue management	Enqueue / dequeue operations, CWQI decay handling, queue persistence
server/core/filters/signal_quality_evaluator.ts	Signal filtering and rejection logging	Rejection counts, max-trades enforcement
server/services/signal-orchestrator.ts	Signal routing	Correct passage of CWQI / NGC into RTB
server/core/metrics/quality_index.ts	Metric propagation	CWQI recalculation and decay functions
RTB-related database tables	Persistent tracking	Ready2BuyQueue, Ready2BuySignals, Metrics counters

Task 3 — Perform Source Analysis

Review the following files for logic consistency and document observations:

ready_to_buy_service.ts

signal_quality_evaluator.ts

signal-orchestrator.ts

Specifically:

Confirm that enqueueSignal() is actually called after SQE filtering.

Confirm that queue prioritization by CWQI decay (λ = 0.03) is active.

Confirm that “Max Trades” rejection is triggered by the correct source and not counting stale signals.

Identify any race conditions or conditions where the RTB queue could be overwritten or cleared prematurely.

Task 4 — Provide Recommendations

Identify any logic, threshold, or counting errors.

Propose non-destructive fixes or improvements, if necessary.
