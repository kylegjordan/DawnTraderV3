## 🧮 Mathematical Specification (CWQI v4)

We evaluate each candidate trade in two stages — **The Gate** (Pass/Fail) and **The Score** (Ranking).

### 1️⃣ The Gate — Net Expectancy (EV)
Does the trade actually make money?

\[
EV = (P_{win} × Dist_{Target}) - (P_{loss} × Dist_{Stop}) - Cost_{Total}
\]

- \(P_{win} = 0.40 + (DI / 200)\) → Capped at 0.60  
- \(P_{loss} = 1 − P_{win}\)  
- \(Cost_{Total} = 0.5\%\) of price (0.4% fees + 0.1% slippage)  
- **If EV ≤ 0 ⇒ Reject**

**Distance Inputs:** Use Kalman-smoothed price series (Directive 9.3) to derive `Dist_Target` and `Dist_Stop`. This ensures trend-aware expectancy estimation.

### 2️⃣ The Score — CWQI (0–100)
Ranks trades that pass the Gate.

\[
Score = \left(\frac{Reward}{Risk}\right) × DI × (1 − VolNoise) × (1 − \bar{ρ})
\]

Where \(\bar{ρ}\) is the mean correlation from the Covariance Engine.
