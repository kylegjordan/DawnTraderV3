Filter Category	Description
Passed All Filters	Pairs that successfully passed all filtering criteria and are eligible for trading.
Min Price	Excludes very low-priced pairs that may have penny-stock characteristics.
Min Volume	Excludes pairs with very low daily volume that may create liquidity issues or high slippage risk.
Max Spread	Excludes pairs with wide bid-ask spreads that increase trading costs.
Min Daily Range	Removes pairs with insufficient daily price movement for day-trading strategies.
Exclude Stablecoins	Filters out stablecoins, which have minimal price volatility.
Valid Quote Currency	Filters out pairs that do not use an approved quote currency (e.g., USD, EUR).
Already Active	Filters out pairs that already have an active position to avoid duplicate exposure.
History	Filters out pairs lacking the required minimum number of historical trading days.
