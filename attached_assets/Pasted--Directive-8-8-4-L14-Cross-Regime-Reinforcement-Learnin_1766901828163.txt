1 | Objective

Establish a reinforcement learning loop that continually adjusts strategy allocation and exposure bias based on long-term cumulative returns, across all market regimes.

Where L13 predicts what regime is coming, L14 teaches the system how best to act in each regime to maximize portfolio growth over time.

2 | Components
Component	Purpose
Reward Evaluator (RE)	Computes per-strategy, per-regime cumulative rewards (PnL, Sharpe ratio, drawdown).
Policy Learner (PL)	Reinforcement model learning an optimal action policy: allocate exposure across strategies for given regime state.
Experience Buffer (EB)	Stores recent (state, action, reward, next_state) tuples for offline learning.
Action Executor (AE)	Applies updated allocation policy to orchestrator weighting tables.
3 | Implementation Tasks
3.1 Reward Evaluator (RE)

📁 server/services/reward-evaluator.ts

Inputs:

Regime data from L12 MCP

Strategy returns from RPT/VTS

Outputs cumulative reward per (strategy, regime):

R_{s,r} = α₁ × \text{profit_rate} + α₂ × \text{win_rate} − α₃ × \text{drawdown}

with α₁=0.6, α₂=0.3, α₃=0.1.

Stores rolling averages for last 1000 trades in /logs/rewards/.
