🧭 Directive 8.8.4-L10
Title: Adaptive Strategy Biasing & Exposure Optimization
Phase: 8.8.4 (L-series)
Status: Ready for Implementation
1 | Objective

Integrate strategy-weighted adaptive exposure control into the Signal Orchestrator, Risk Engine, and Adaptive Risk Advisor (ARA) so that strategies with higher real-world reliability and calibration confidence are automatically allocated proportionally larger portions of the trading budget — all while remaining inside guardrail constraints.

This closes the learning loop:

VTS ➜ ML Calibration ➜ Strategy Confidence Weighting ➜ Adaptive Risk & Exposure Control.

2 | Design Goals
Goal	Description
1. Adaptive Exposure Scaling	Dynamically distribute total risk exposure based on per-strategy reliability weights.
2. Guardrail Compliance	Always respect global max exposure, per-trade risk, and stop-loss thresholds.
3. Transparency	Display how exposure and bias decisions are made in both ARA and the Health UI.
4. Modularity	Biasing logic remains an optional layer (can be toggled or tuned).
