Phase 8.2 — Strategy Analytics & Performance Aggregation
🎯 Objective

Expand Dawn Trader’s intelligence layer by computing and caching derived performance analytics for every active strategy.
The goal is to transform raw data from Bob modules into actionable insights — so Walter and InsightBob can reason about comparative performance, risk balance, and trend strength in real time.

🧩 Action Steps
1. Verify Phase 8.1 Integrity Baseline

Confirm that the Cortex still syncs every 30 seconds and reports modulesActive = 7.

Check logs for [CortexCore] 📸 Snapshots synced successfully and [InsightBob] ✅ Insight summary generated.

2. Create Performance Aggregation Cycle

Introduce a scheduled analytics pass (every 15 minutes) that compiles strategy-level and portfolio-level performance summaries.

Each pass should compute:

Cumulative P/L % and absolute value

Rolling Sharpe Ratio (7 day window)

Max Drawdown %

Win Rate and Average Return per Trade

Trade Frequency per hour and per day

Results stored in Cortex under strategy_analytics.

3. Add Portfolio Aggregation

Combine all strategies to produce:

Portfolio-level Volatility and Sharpe

Capital Allocation Weightings

Diversification Index (variance correlation metric)

Cache this under portfolio_summary.
