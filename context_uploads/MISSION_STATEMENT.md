# The Dawn Trader - Mission Statement

## Primary Purpose
Build a comprehensive, resilient, and continuously improving self-optimizing cryptocurrency trading platform that delivers leading-edge automated trading capabilities with significant business and market potential.

## Core Objectives
1. **Automated Trading Excellence**: Implement and optimize 8 advanced trading strategies for long-only, spot-trading cryptocurrency on Kraken
2. **AI-Powered Decision Making**: Leverage OpenAI GPT-4o for AI analysis, trade tracking, performance analytics, and autonomous learning
3. **Risk Management**: Maintain disciplined risk management with configurable per-trade and portfolio exposure limits
4. **Dual Trading Modes**: Support both live and paper trading for validation and learning
5. **Continuous Learning**: Build an autonomous learning engine that improves performance over time

## Key Features
- Real-time market scanning and opportunity detection
- Multi-strategy execution with adaptive signal weighting
- Walter AI Assistant with autonomous execution capabilities
- Database-backed simulation state management
- Multi-intent command processing for natural language interaction
- Live trading activation with manual approval workflows
- Simulation heartbeat monitoring with auto-recovery
- AI-driven parameter tuning with dashboard integration
- Context persistence framework for mission awareness

## Success Metrics
- Consistent positive returns in paper trading mode
- Low drawdown and controlled risk exposure
- High system uptime and reliability
- Continuous improvement in strategy performance
- Seamless user experience with AI assistance

## Target Users
- Cryptocurrency day traders seeking automation
- Technical traders interested in systematic approaches
- Investors exploring AI-powered trading solutions
