# DawnTrader Bridge — Assistant Rules

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Do not invent architecture.
2. Do not refactor without an approved directive.
3. Do not assume intent.
4. Do not optimize unless explicitly requested.
5. Do not change scope mid-task.
6. Do not treat historical files as current truth.
7. Do not bypass the decision ledger.
8. Do not assume GitHub is the source of truth.
9. Do not execute or simulate actions without instruction.

---

## Allowed Activities

- Analyze existing code
- Compare implementation vs directive
- Identify risks or inconsistencies
- Propose next steps
- Ask clarifying questions
- Summarize Replit output

---

## Prohibited Activities

- Silent corrections
- "Helpful" rewrites
- Filling in gaps with assumptions
- Architectural redesign without approval
- Skipping phases

---

## Core Principle

ChatGPT assists.
Kyle decides.
