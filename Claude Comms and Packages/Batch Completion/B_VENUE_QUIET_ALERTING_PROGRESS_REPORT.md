### 3a. ➕ AMENDMENT 2026-10-10 ~22:40Z — STILL BEFORE ANY WEEKDAY DATA (Langston's review of §3; §3 above is left as written)
**Why:** Langston showed criterion 1's clock is wrong by construction and that criterion 1 and FINDING-1 were one gate read as two observations. The standing record resolves only on `verdict === 'not_quiet' && stillOut.length === 0` (`venue-quiet-alerting.ts` sweep), and `not_quiet` needs `T >= quiet_ticking_min` (346) — at the 00:00Z reopen the venue is in extended hours, so T will not reach 346 there. And `stillOut` empties only when every member has been priced, which a book-state member under a persisting refusal never is: **11 of the 16 members carry `book_state`** (ORCL, GEV, CRWD, KKR, UNH, AMAT, COPX, MCD, BKR, RBLX, STZ; the other five are pure `quiet_market`).

**The criteria that now decide (they replace §3's PASS 1-3 and FAIL clauses; §3's text stays for the record):**
1. **The record — clocked on the object that decides it.** The deciding event is **the first sweep whose verdict reads `not_quiet`**; the earliest possible time is the 00:00Z reopen and the **expected time is the 13:30Z regular open** (Thursday 10-08, all-frames: regular-session p05 461, off-session median 159 — Langston). **PASS-1:** by 14:30Z (one hour into the regular session) either the record has resolved by `active-exit-monitor`/`engine`, or **every member the sweep names as holding it** (`held=` on the sweep line, r6) is (a) a `book_state` member — attributed to FINDING-1, not to the quiet rule — or (b) a `quiet_market` member for which a duration page has minted. **FAIL-1:** the record unresolved after a `not_quiet` sweep reporting `held=0`; or a `quiet_market` member held past `escalate_after_ms` after the first `not_quiet` sweep with no duration page.
2. **The count at the reopen — one read site decides.** The **sweep verdict line** (`[VENUE_QUIET][SWEEP] T=… verdict=…`) is what the rule acts on and decides; the archiver heartbeat (`symbols_updated_60s`, the same function over the same window) is its control; page-time T is a third read time and is not used. **00:00-00:15Z is the resubscribe warm-up and is not graded.** **FAIL-2 (decided now, not after the data):** from 00:15Z to 04:00Z, three or more consecutive sweeps reading `thin` while the heartbeat on the same minutes shows `connected=true` and `update` frames > 0 ⇒ the 50 floor, calibrated on all-frame counts, is too high for the update-only count and pages a healthy feed — a knob FAIL. A `thin` reading with the heartbeat at `update:0` or `connected=false` is the feed genuinely silent and is a **correct page**, not a FAIL.
3. **The close — with its control.** At and after the 20:00Z close, zero quiet-family `price-skip` mints counts **only over positions priced at least once in the regular session** (the in-memory escalation guard clears only on a successful pricing, so a never-priced position's zero is the default, not the rule working). The denominator is reported.
4. **FINDING-1 rests on the MECHANISM, established in code, not on Monday's observation.** A book-state-basis row or member clears only when the engine notes a price, and a book-state refusal never reaches that note; so a held key from an off-session row can suppress the regular-session page the rule promises. **Disposition 1: fixed in this batch before close, designed at Step 9 and reviewed by Langston, whatever Monday shows.** Monday corroborates. **Resolving the eight by hand does not discharge it** — Langston is deliberately leaving them; they are the instrument.
5. **A restart inside the window VOIDS the reading from the previous anchor and re-anchors at the new `pm_uptime`** — it is not a FAIL (10-10: the 11:59:18Z and 17:05:11Z restarts each produced a 16-position burst within ~64 s; the 21:20:58Z restart, after r4, did not).
6. **The instrument (r6, `held=`):** before r6 the sweep computed which members held the record open and discarded the list unless a duration page fired, so a record that did not resolve could not name its blocker. r6 logs it on every sweep line. **It must be deployed before 13:30Z Monday** for criterion 1 to be readable; if it is not, criterion 1's attribution is reported as unreadable, not as a pass.
