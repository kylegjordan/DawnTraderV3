## What it was for
The crypto recorder (the passive archive) keeps a durable price history for every crypto pair over two Kraken connections. On 2026-10-21 10:19:55Z one of them (shard0, 196 of 408 coins) was cut by the venue, reconnected, took in its opening data, then received nothing for 14 days while reporting `connected=true`. It recovered at the 2026-11-04 12:13:26Z restart (the `e1becf801` deploy). The recorders reconnected only after a close, never checked a subscription, and printed their own silence every minute without acting on it; the feed-health alarm graded a class by its freshest symbol, so one live connection kept it healthy. And the recorder had quietly become an input to a close decision: the not-warm close gate grades a walked fill against the recorder's "witness" price, and 14 closes in the dark window carried a witness frozen days earlier.
