## 2c. THE FRESHNESS RE-BASE, MEASURED — AND THE ANSWER IS **DO NOT MOVE THE NUMBER** (2026-09-03 05:3xZ)
**Why it was measured:** Kyle's (D) ruling carries one number that the audit said had lost its basis — the 15 s fill limit was derived from a **2026-06-12 measurement at ~1.8 s capture cadence: "RTH p99 inter-tick gap 8.75 s → freshness floor 15 s"** (`fill-safety-config.ts:19-22`), i.e. the rule was *p99 × ≈1.71 headroom*, and the archive has been throttled to 4 s since 2026-07-08. I told Kyle the number would move. **Measured, it should not.**

**Object + population:** consecutive-frame gaps per symbol in `xstock_spot_ticker_snap`, 2026-08-20 → 2026-09-03. RTH read: 20,887,631 gaps / 479 symbols, weekdays 13:30–20:00 UTC. **RTH p99 is now 15.07 s** (p50 4.44, p90 6.85, p99.9 76.06) — the SAME RULE (p99 × 1.71) would give ≈ 26 s.
⛔ **AND THE FRAME-COUNT VIEW IS THE WRONG OBJECT FOR THIS DECISION — I nearly reported it and it points the opposite way.** The gate compares `NOW() − MAX(captured_at)` at the instant an entry is attempted, so what decides a refusal is the share of **TIME** the newest row is older than the limit, not the share of **GAPS** longer than it. Long gaps occupy disproportionate clock (length-biased sampling): by gap count, off-hours looks 19–27 % refused; **time-weighted it is 69–80 %.** Per gap of length `g` the time spent staler than `L` is `max(0, g − L)`; summed over gaps and divided by elapsed time, that IS the probability a uniformly-timed attempt is refused.

**Time-weighted refusal share of the clock, by ET session** (gaps > 6 h excluded as session/weekend boundaries, stated):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| regular hours | 34,454 | **14.0 %** | 13.0 % | 11.8 % | 9.9 % |
| after-hours | 18,787 | **69.0 %** | 59.2 % | 37.7 % | 17.9 % |
| pre-market | 29,344 | **78.6 %** | 72.9 % | 62.7 % | 38.0 % |
| overnight | 36,774 | **80.4 %** | 76.3 % | 68.8 % | 49.7 % |

**BOTH ESTIMANDS, SIDE BY SIDE, WITH THE ESTIMAND NAMED (Langston Step-4 CONDITION-3) — a later reader who recomputes by gap count must not conclude I was wrong:**
| session | **share of GAPS longer than 15 s** (per-frame view) | **share of CLOCK spent staler than 15 s** (the decision object) |
|---|---|---|
| regular hours | 1.02 % | **14.0 %** |
| after-hours | 27.17 % | **69.0 %** |
| pre-market | 25.36 % | **78.6 %** |
| overnight | 19.18 % | **80.4 %** |
**The estimand that decides a refusal is the second one:** an entry attempt lands at an arbitrary instant, and the gate compares `NOW() − MAX(captured_at)` at that instant, so the denominator is elapsed CLOCK, not the number of gaps. The two differ by ~3× off-hours because long gaps occupy disproportionate time (length-biased sampling). Both are true of the same data; only the second answers "will an entry be refused".

⇒ **RECOMMENDATION, AND IT FOLLOWS FROM KYLE'S OWN RULING RATHER THAN FROM THE ARITHMETIC: LEAVE `active_fill_max_age_ms` AT 15,000 ms.** Re-basing to ≈26 s is defensible as *restoring the original rule* — but its ONLY effect is to let more entries through, ~10 points more off-hours (69 → 59 % after-hours, 80 → 76 % overnight) against ~1 point in regular hours, and Kyle's ruling is explicit: *"I just don't want to relax the rules so that we can get more trades in those off hours."* The measuring-stick argument justifies a change; he has told me which way to err.
⚠️ **THE DRIFT IS RECORDED RATHER THAN CORRECTED, so nobody later reads 15 s as still meaning what it meant:** at 1.8 s capture the limit sat at **1.71 × RTH p99**; at 4 s capture it sits at **1.00 ×**. **The bar has silently TIGHTENED by ~1.7× since it was written** — we are keeping the stricter number deliberately, not by inattention. If the capture throttle changes again, this ratio is the thing to re-read.

## 3. The semantics that came out of review (the reader needs these more than the diff)
- **A value is a look that happened; a NULL is re-cuttable.** `exit_book_state` is written only when the guard assessed a live frame. Crypto, a disabled guard, cold knobs, no tick, a flatten with no decision verdict, the maker leg's fill instant — all NULL, never `unknown`. `unknown` means exactly one thing: the guard LOOKED and had no comparator yet (labelled, actionable — constraint 1 at the post-restart path).
