### A3. Existing identity, writers and restart behavior

`active_open_positions` is re-read by the monitor. It carries the pending state, limit, placement and deadline while present. The entry row has a linked `closed_trades` ID in position metadata. Durable opened time is placement, closed time is a trade close or typed never-filled cancellation. Trade metadata and entry source/observed time/decision price do not by themselves establish the evaluator's local receipt, decision-now or a preceding refusal span. See `shared/schema.ts:1757-1784,1831-1832,2077-2125` and `active-execution-engine.ts:1931-2006,2254-2299,7235`.

The normal pending transition writes filled state and the linked trade's provenance. In crypto the accepted fill touch can be a different object from the outer monitor mark that currently supplies entry_observed_at_ms. Capture the actual successful side and receipt. Never reconstruct the fill time from opened_at plus an observed-age stamp. The single never-filled helper is reached by deadline, missing legacy provenance and engine-stop flatten. Reset/Start New, orphan reconciliation, and storage cleanup can terminate/remove the position through different boundaries. The normal #961 candidate cancel path keys the same trade and pending position in a transaction. At the shared cut, the paper start route clears active positions; legacy and C14 reset paths additionally call deleteAllClosedTrades, and duplicate-promotion compensation can delete its individual trade. See routes.ts:11368-11436,11698-11701, c14-validation-service.ts:162-163, active-engine-service.ts:1189, active-execution-engine.ts:7030 and storage.ts:3956-3986. A deleted trade is outside the assumed retention of this carrier. Require a reset/history continuity check and treat a destructive purge or missing reconciliation as coverage lost. Don't infer zero from survivors. Grade only an exact linked committed writer result for the candidate rest and classify other termination paths or missing authority as ungraded. A same-symbol replacement, historical entry price source, exit-rest history, or a missing-row no-op cannot be a pending-entry terminal result. Benchmark #961 at exact `5ce00e63038660d7e6128e30168395774304516a` changes xStock paper pending fill/cancel to `transitionPaperXstockPendingMaker` in storage. This exact ref is a review dependency, not an assumed current shared writer.
