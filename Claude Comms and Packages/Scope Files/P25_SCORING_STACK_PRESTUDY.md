# PART II — THE SCORING-SET REDESIGN (the Kyle-directive major rev, 2026-07-13; CC-A; for crew debate → Kyle ratification)

## 6. Field-research synthesis (4 parallel tracks, all cited in the track reports; strongest-consensus findings)
1. **Rank by after-cost expected return per unit of risk = documented practice** (Grinold "Implementation Efficiency" FAJ 2005; Grinold & Kahn; Gârleanu-Pedersen JF 2013; Tharp's expectancy/R-multiple tradition; Frazzini-Israel-Moskowitz + Novy-Marx-Velikov net-of-cost literature). Our B7.1 expected-R ranker is the documented standard. Breadth-1 note: one-trade-per-cycle RAISES the per-trade calibration burden (fundamental law).
2. **Gate on after-cost EV, never on a composite score** — no documented institutional practice gates admission on a composite quality score in place of a net-EV check; composites feed gates, never replace them. Best-documented refinements: **stricter entry-than-hold** (Novy-Marx-Velikov buy/hold spread — their empirically best cost mitigation) and an explicit **edge-to-cost budget** (Carver's "spend ≤⅓ of expected pre-cost return on costs"). Liquidity screens (ADV-participation ceilings) standard.
3. **Confidence = a calibrated probability, or it isn't a forecast.** Hand-built weighted "confidence" blends are **undocumented in serious literature as decision inputs** (retail-tool level only). The documented norm: express conviction AS a probability and CALIBRATE it against outcomes (reliability diagrams; **Platt scaling at small samples <~1000; isotonic only at 1000+** — Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana ICML 2005, scikit-learn §1.16; finance precedent = the meta-labeling line, López de Prado AFML 2018 + Meyer/Barziy/Joubert JFDS 2023: **the score is a FEATURE, the calibrated probability is the OUTPUT**). Dawes 1979 gives hand blends cover ONLY as *rankings* with correctly-signed, individually-predictive inputs — never as probabilities entering EV arithmetic.
4. **Combining weak signals: equal/rank-weight standardized inputs is the small-sample default.** Hand-tuned weights are the documented WORST option (Dawes; DeMiguel-Garlappi-Uppal RFS 2009 — optimizing weights need ~thousands of observations to beat 1/N; the 50-year forecast-combination puzzle); IC-weighted-with-shrinkage only when measured ICs are decisive (Grinold-Kahn; Qian-Hua-Sorensen). Directly indicts every hand-set blend in our stack.
5. **Shrink the edge before you size it** — four independent lines converge (Baker-McHale shrinkage under parameter uncertainty; Thorp/MacLean-Ziemba fractional-Kelly because overbetting is asymmetrically fatal; Carver haircut-Sharpe-then-half-Kelly-then-halve-for-skew; McLean-Pontiff: backtested edges ~26-58% overstated). Half-Kelly-or-less on a haircut edge; small samples keep the haircut large for a long time.
