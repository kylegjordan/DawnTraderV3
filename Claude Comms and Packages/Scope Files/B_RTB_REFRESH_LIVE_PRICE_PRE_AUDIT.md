## 0. PRE-REGISTRATION — committed BEFORE any measurement below is run
**Read sites and times:** every query runs on the staging database; each states its UTC read time and the deployed sha. The live ask for a queued signal is approximated from the durable ticker archives (`crypto_spot_ticker_snap`, `xstock_spot_ticker_snap`: `bid`, `bid_qty`, `ask`, `ask_qty`, captured time) — the newest row per symbol, with its age stated. **This is the archive, not the refresh's eventual read site** (crypto `selectCryptoTouch` in memory; xStock `getDepthSnapshot`, which reads this same xStock table); the crypto figures are therefore a proxy, labelled so.
**Population:** every `rtb_signals` row, both modes as they occur, split by `asset_class`, at each read time. **Three read times:** one now (xStock overnight session), one in the US regular session (13:30-20:00Z), one after it.

**M1 — would-retire (OBJ-2's blast radius), and C1's grounding.** Per row: the newest ticker row for its symbol no older than **15 s** (the open's `active_fill_max_age_ms`); classify `ask <= stop_price`, `ask >= target_price`, strictly inside, or no admissible row. **Every would-retire row is listed** with bid, ask, the spread as a share of the mid, both quantities and the row's age. **Decision rule, fixed now:** a would-retire row whose book the exit side would refuse to decide on (no quantity on the ask, a one-sided book, or a spread wider than the exit's book-state bound — the bound is read from the code at the audit, and cited) is counted as **"the contract must exclude"**; **if there is even one, OBJ-1's contract must exclude that shape before OBJ-2 may act** (Langston C1). Counts are not otherwise a gate — the retire rule is the existing primitive.

**M2 — `price_unavailable`.** At the same reads, the share of rows per class with no admissible ask. No decision rides on it; it is the denominator for C2 and the Step-2 question on unpriced signals. If xStock is unpriced on more than half its rows off-session, the plan states that the refresh check is in practice a regular-session check for xStock.

**M3 — is a retirement reversible? (Langston C4).** Code: the queue-insert path's dedupe, cooldown and re-entry rules for a `symbol × strategy` whose row was deleted, cited. Data: over the last 72 h, per class, the time between consecutive orchestrator `admitted` rows (`signal_eval_archive`, `source` = the signal orchestrator, `reject_stage = 'admitted'`) for the same `symbol × strategy` — median and p90. **Decision rule, fixed now:** if the median re-admission gap is at most **10 minutes**, a false retirement costs about one cycle and OBJ-1's contract may be the open's own; **if it is longer, or the code blocks re-entry, the contract is stricter than the open's** (a retirement then needs the ask to have passed the level on two consecutive passes, or equivalent) — chosen in the plan, not after the data.

**M4 — volume.** Rows per class per read; passes per hour from the refresh's own log line (`[A4.R10R-3][RTBRefresh] … signals=… priced=…`), sized to say whether OBJ-3's shadow line needs sampling (threshold fixed now: more than **2,000 lines an hour** ⇒ sample).

**M5 — the selection-to-open gap.** From the code: the refresh cadence per bucket and the promotion loop's interval, cited. No live measurement is planned (the promoted row is deleted at promotion, so the last refresh time is not retained); stated as **UNMEASURED** with that reason.

**L/USD vs row 2a (the plan's open question):** L's 1-hour bars at its 2026-10-06 open run through `B-ATR-BAD-PRINT`'s filter; result reported, or `UNMEASURED` with the reason if the bars are no longer in the archive.

### 1.3 M3 — is a retirement reversible? (Langston C4)
- **Code:** exactly ONE insert path — the signal orchestrator (`signal-orchestrator.ts:~1495`) → `queueSQESignal` (`ready_to_buy_service.ts:2096`) → `storage.upsertRtbSignal` (`storage.ts:4401-4433`), `ON CONFLICT (mode, symbol, strategy) DO UPDATE` setting **entry, stop, target, status and `queuedAt = new Date()`**. The only cooldown is `post_stop_reentry_cooldown_minutes`, keyed on a `closed_trades` `stop_hit` row (`:2277-2320`); a queue deletion writes no such row and there is no blocklist. **A retired symbol × strategy is re-queued, with fresh levels, the next time the orchestrator issues it.**
- **Data (72 h, `signal_eval_archive`, `source='signal-orchestrator'`, `reject_stage='admitted'`, `mode='paper_sim'`; positive control: 19,461 crypto rows over 116 pairs, 188 xStock over 177):** the gap between consecutive admissions of the same symbol × strategy — **crypto median 29.9 s (p90 32.1 s), n = 19,346; xStock median 1 h 47 m (p90 3 h 10 m), n = 11.**
- **By the pre-registered rule:** crypto ≤ 10 min ⇒ a false retirement costs about one cycle, and the contract may be the open's own. **xStock > 10 min ⇒ the contract is stricter: an xStock retirement needs the ask past the level on two consecutive passes** (plan item 3).
- ⚠️ **What the re-admission gap really shows (§1.4):** a pair that keeps firing is re-written every ~30 s with fresh levels, so its levels are never stale. The stale rows are the pairs that STOPPED firing — DOT has had zero orchestrator admissions in the last 6 h. **Retirement removes exactly those, and re-queues them fresh if they fire again.**

## 2. PLAN — each item ← its finding
1. **The ask contract, one function** ← 1.6, C1. `server/core/rtb/rtb-price-check.ts` (new) `readRetirementAsk(signal, now): { ok: true, ask, bid, ageMs, basis } | { ok: false, reason }`, built ONLY from the exit's pieces: **crypto** — `selectCryptoTouch` with the EXIT policy (`EXIT_TRIGGER_MAX_AGE_MS`, `EXIT_TRIGGER_MAX_SPREAD_FRACTION`, exported from where they live, not copied), the readers moved to one shared export (the engine's and the VTS's `CRYPTO_TOUCH_READERS` become one), `transactableSide(sel, 'buy')`; **xStock** — `getLatestEquityTick(symbol)`, refused on: no tick; `xstockTransactableSides` failing; `ask_qty` not > 0 (the hollow arm); age above `computeStalenessCeiling(price, stop, σ)` (σ absent ⇒ its floor, 15 s); the spread failing `judgeEntrySpread` against `resolveEntryYardstick`. **No fallback to a midpoint, the cache mark or the archive witness.** A refusal is a reason token, counted.
2. **The read site and the switch** ← 1.7, C2. RRS builds `asks: Map<signalKey, AskVerdict>` for the bucket's signals and passes it to `refreshAndRank(mode, bucketKeys, asks)` — the directive's original shape. **The switch becomes "the bucket has signals"** (`bucketSignals.length > 0`): the mark-cache gate no longer decides whether a signal is price-checked. `validPrices` stays only for its log line, renamed for what it is.
3. **The retirement** ← 1.1, 1.3, OBJ-2. In `refreshAndRank`, before the SQE: `refuseTakerBooking(ask, stop, target)`. **Crypto:** retire on the first pass. **xStock:** retire on the **second consecutive** pass that refuses on the same level with an admissible ask (a per-signal counter in the refresh state, reset by any pass that does not refuse) — §1.3's pre-registered rule. A retirement joins `bulkDeletes`, is counted (`rejectedInRefresh`, reason `price_past_stop` / `price_past_target`), and writes one `signal_eval_archive` row: `source = 'rtb-refresh'`, `reject_stage = 'rtb_price'`, `gateDecision { reason, ask, bid, askAgeMs, basis, entry, stop, target, passes }`. A signal with no admissible ask is neither retired nor reconfirmed on price — counted under its refusal reason and left to the SQE pass as today.
4. **`price_unavailable` is a counter, per class per reason** ← 1.2, M2 — exposed beside the refresh's existing outcome counts, so Step 7 reads its rate directly.
5. **The shadow measurement, sampled and durable** ← 1.5, 1.9, OBJ-3.
