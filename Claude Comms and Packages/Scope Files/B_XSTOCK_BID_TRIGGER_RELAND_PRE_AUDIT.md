## 2. PLAN (each item points at its finding)
- **P1 ← A1, A2 (condition 1: the frame line and the 1:1 fence).** Immediately after `invoked++` (`:3085`), for `_evalCls === 'xstock'` only: increment `this._xsFramesEmitted` and `console.warn` one line:
  `[3n.q7][XS_FRAME] <symbol> pos=<id> frame=<ok|none> <basis=raw_guarded|raw_unguarded | reason=<code>> mark= sl= tp= bid= ask= spread= thr= bidWouldFire=<stop|target|no> markExit=<y|n>`
  `EVAL_EXIT` gains `xsFrames=<emitted>/<xstock invoked>`, reset with the other per-cycle counters. **The fence is in code:** if `emitted !== invoked` for the cycle, `console.error('[3n.q7][XS_FRAME_RECONCILE_BROKEN] emitted=… invoked=…')`. Both numbers are printed every cycle, so the reconciliation is an assertion, not an argument about placement.
- **P2 ← A3 (condition 2: `frame=none` with its reason).** A new `xsFrameReason` beside `xsBid` at the caller, set on each of the four arms in A3: `unguarded_not_two_sided`, `unguarded_crossed`, `guarded_crossed`, `guarded_non_finite`. The `xsFrame` object gains `basis` and `reason`. A null object on an xStock invocation (the class resolvers disagree) prints `frame=none reason=no_frame_object`. **A hollow frame is never silently identical to no frame, and never dropped.**
- **P3 ← A4 (condition 3: the write-rate budget, as numbers).** One line ≈ 250 B (to be measured on the first real line and restated). The exit loop runs about every 1.5 s, so one line per held xStock position per tick is **≈ 2,400 lines/h ≈ 14 MB per held position per 24 h of evaluation**. Evaluation runs 24/5 while the venue quotes; over a weekend the mark goes stale and the row `continue`s at the freshness ceiling (`:1916-1931`) before the evaluator, so weekends add nothing (an upper bound, not an estimate). **Against today:** `error.log` is 64-120 MB/day (the 14 daily files 09-14..28). **At 5 held xStock positions:** +72 MB/day (~1 GB over the 14-day retention). **At 20, the ceiling after Kyle's $3,000 reset:** +288 MB/day (~4 GB retained). **Neither reaches the 1 GB `max_size`, so `error.log` keeps DAILY rotation and its 14-day reach** — the instrument does not cannibalise X3 or any other `error.log` instrument. Disk: 75 GB, 21 GB free (72%), `/var/log/dawntrader` 16 GB; the worst case uses about a fifth of the free space. **A fence:** if an `error.log` day exceeds 700 MB after the deploy, the frame line is the first suspect and the read is re-budgeted before the window continues.
- **P4 ← condition 4 (n-floor and EXTEND, pre-registered, with the expected n).** The unit is the **held xStock position-episode**, not the frame (frames within one episode are not independent). **Window:** 21 days from the deploy that lands P1. **Floor, all three:** ≥ 20 distinct held positions over ≥ 15 symbols · ≥ 60 held hours inside RTH (13:30-20:00Z, weekdays) · ≥ 10 held positions spanning the 13:30Z open. **Measured baseline (14 days to 09-29):** 40 positions over 37 symbols, 459 held hours, 135.6 in RTH, 16 open crossings, but almost all from the 09-14..19 burst; since 09-18 xStock opened about one trade a day (`#1079`). **Expected n:** at the post-`#1079` rate (~5 opens a week) about 15 positions in 21 days, BELOW the floor; after Kyle's reset (about 20 slots, provisional 2026-10-05) the burst-week rate (~39 a week) would reach it inside a week. **EXTEND:** below the floor at day 21 ⇒ one extension of 14 days, stated at day 21, not at the read. Still below ⇒ **INCONCLUSIVE**, reported to Kyle with the reset/`#1079` dependency named, and the re-land waits.
- **P5 ← A5 (proxy (a), orientation only).** A replay from `xstock_spot_ticker_snap` over held positions' intervals: the share of snapshot frames where the bid crosses the static stop or target while the mark does not, by RTH/off-hours. **Labelled `SNAPSHOT proxy`** (the 09-17 method). **Published with the measured frame-count ratio:** snapshot rows in an interval ÷ evaluated xStock ticks in the same interval (from `EVAL_EXIT`). It may size a threshold's neighbourhood; it may never set, pass or refuse one.
- **P6 — tests.** (a) One frame line per xStock invocation and none for crypto. (b) `frame=none reason=<code>` on each of the four arms and on a null object. (c) The fence prints `RECONCILE_BROKEN` when the counts diverge (driven by a test hook) and stays silent when they match. **Mutations:** emit removed, emit for all classes, reason dropped, fence removed. Each must fail a test.
