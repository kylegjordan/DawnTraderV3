**A0-1 — the mechanism (re-derived at `1c4a776c5`).** `equity-spot-archiver.ts` sets the in-memory tick's age (`tsMs`) and `observed: true` ONLY on an `update` frame (`:302-314`); a `snapshot` (the venue replaying a book on every subscribe) or an unrecognised frame keeps the prior `tsMs` and `observed` (`:322-325`), and with no prior entry is born `observed: false` (`:323-325`); a frame with NO mark keeps both too (`:327-331`, Langston F-1).
