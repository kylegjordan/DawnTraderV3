## PART 0 — INCREMENT 0: THE xSTOCK DISPATCH FRESHNESS GATE READS THE IN-MEMORY TICK

### Audit
**A0-1 — the mechanism (re-derived at `1c4a776c5`).** `equity-spot-archiver.ts` sets the in-memory tick's age (`tsMs`) and `observed: true` ONLY on an `update` frame (`:302-314`); a `snapshot` (the venue replaying a book on every subscribe) or an unrecognised frame keeps the prior `tsMs` and `observed` (`:322-325`), and with no prior entry is born `observed: false` (`:323-325`); a frame with NO mark keeps both too (`:327-331`, Langston F-1). But `bufferTickerSnap({ … capturedAt: new Date() … })` (`:334-338`) is OUTSIDE those branches and runs on EVERY frame. The DB row has no frame type and no `observed` column (`shared/schema.ts:5110-5137`; `metadata` jsonb with `schema_version` exists, `:5136`).
**A0-2 — the dispatch gate (census #1).** `getLatestTickAgeMs` is `NOW() − MAX(captured_at)` per symbol (`active-dispatch.ts:74-85`); the admission predicate is `if (ageMs === null || ageMs > safety.activeFillMaxAgeMs)` → skip (`:184`), with `activeFillMaxAgeMs` = 15,000 ms (`xstock_fill_safety`, Kyle-settled, unchanged). **Measured by Langston 2026-11-07:** 13 replay bursts in the retained window, each `snapshot:467` with `rows_persisted_60s=467`, 11 of 13 with `update:0`; `AAPL/USD` 9 byte-identical rows 13:20:58Z→20:09:21Z while its last genuine change was 05:14:29Z (15.68 h). ⇒ each burst reads every symbol's age ≈ 0 on a book up to ~15.7 h old — **false-fresh and fail-OPEN**. Magnitude ~15 s × 13 ≈ 195 s/day fleet-wide; **no realised harm observed** (4 xStock opens in 30 h, none inside a burst) — exposure, unexercised. **Extended by CC-B 2026-11-07 ~22:10Z (Langston asked; confirmatory, not a blocker):** the archiver heartbeat (`equity-spot-archiver.ts:500-508`, one line per minute, `ticker_snaps_60s=update:…,snapshot:…,other:…`) over every retained stdout file, 2026-11-04 15:43:20Z → 11-07 22:06:15Z — **4,692 heartbeat lines** (~78.4 h at one a minute ≈ 4,704, so near-complete coverage): **40 windows with `snapshot` > 0, every one `snapshot:467`, 0 windows with `other` > 0** — ≈ 12 replay bursts a day. They are NOT only restarts: they fall in regular hours too (11-04 15:48Z alongside `update:121088`; 11-05 17:48Z `update:71203`; 11-06 18:31Z `update:73428`), so a replay can land while the market is live, on whichever names are quiet at that moment. The 13 in Langston's window are the subset since 11-07 00:00Z with app restarts among them; why the archiver resubscribes ~12×/day on its own is the question he surfaced and did not place (owner's).
**A0-3 — the open depth gate (census #2/#3), assessed (r2, corrected by Langston's PART-0 review, Discord 21:5xZ).** `getDepthSnapshot` for `xstock_spot` reads the NEWEST `xstock_spot_ticker_snap` row and its age `NOW() − captured_at` (`depth-source.ts:96-104`); BOTH capital-gating age checks read that one age — `assessWarmth` at the open gate (`engine.ts:622`) and `assessConsumptionFreshness` at the last synchronous check before `openOrder` (`engine.ts:6413-6423`) — and the entry fill walks that row's ask. Same false-fresh shape on a replay. **Withdrawn (r2, Langston FINDING-4):** r1 called `const ageMs = ageReadValid ? rawAgeMs : 0` (`depth-source.ts:110`) a second fail-open. The clamp is deliberate (`:41-43`), and the consuming seam fails CLOSED on it — `assessConsumptionFreshness` refuses `consumption_clock_invalid` when `ageReadValid !== true` (`:272`, tested `b-entry-consumption-freshness.test.ts:68`); `:110` is also the shared producer the close reads (`engine.ts:4662`). It is an `assessWarmth`-local property, not a path property, and is NOT changed. **Corrected (r2, Langston FINDING-5):** r1 said the depth gate might be "the only age check between a replay and a fill".
