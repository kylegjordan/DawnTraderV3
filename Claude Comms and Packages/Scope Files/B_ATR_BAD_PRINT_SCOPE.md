(currentPrice * 0.02)` (`signal-orchestrator.ts:2279`) and sets **stop = 1.5 × ATR, target = 2.5 × ATR** (`pattern-recognizer.ts:585-586`). It also sets `strong_bull_trend`'s 3×/6× ATR (`strong-bull-trend.ts:79`), `vwap_pullback` (`strategy-engine.ts:236,1243`), ORB (`orb.ts:239`), the reach gate's `atrsToTarget` (`signal-orchestrator.ts:1916-1921`, `signal-target-normalizer.ts:109,121`), the RTB geometry floor (`ready_to_buy_service.ts:1696`) and the VTS's `atrAtOpen` (`vts-runner.ts:1563,2529`).
- **The cases:** **GBP/USD** opened 2026-09-23 21:12Z one hour after a Kraken 60-min candle printed **high 1.70000** against ~1.324 (+28 %; every other bar that day ranged 0.06-0.28 %) ⇒ ATR 2.17 % where the 13 clean bars give ~0.17 % ⇒ stop 3.25 %, target 5.42 % (1.5× / 2.5× exactly). **LIGHTER** opened 09-30 06:14Z two hours after a bar with **low 0.110** against ~3.7 ⇒ ATR 11.95 % ⇒ stop 17.7 %, target 30.1 %. Our recorded `atr_at_open` matches Kraken's own hourly ATR14 within 1.0-1.2× on all six crypto positions checked.
- **The population (crypto paper opens 2026-09-06 → 10-05, n=163, every one with Kraken history):** **6 (3.7 %)** opened with one bar carrying ≥ 40 % of the 14-bar true-range sum and ≥ 4× the median of the rest; **base rate over every hour of the same coins 671 / 53,827 (1.2 %)**. Those 6: **0 targets, 4 stops, 2 still open**; median hold 21.9 h vs 7.7 h. n=6 — the over-representation is a lead (a wider ATR makes a wider target, which clears the fee-aware gates more easily), not established.

## 2. Objectives
1. **ONE ATR implementation for every decision, robust to a single off-market bar.** Replace the six plain-mean copies that feed live decisions (MCE, `strategy-engine`, `strategy-helpers`, and the three DBS copies) with **one shared function**, so a fix cannot land in one copy and miss the others (rule 7). **The estimator is chosen at Step 2 from measurement, not here** — candidates: a median-based or trimmed true range, or capping each bar's true range against the pair's own recent typical range. Constraint: it must judge a bar only against the pair's own history.
   *Verify:* a unit fence that every ATR call site resolves to the shared function (census at the ref); and on the 163-trade population, recomputing the ATR at each open shows GBP/USD ≈ 0.17-0.2 % (not 2.17 %) and LIGHTER near its clean-bar value, while the 157 un-spiked opens move by less than a stated tolerance.
2. **A genuine volatility jump still raises the ATR (positive control).** A real high-volatility hour (several consecutive wide bars, or a sustained move) must still widen the reading — the fix must not mute real volatility. *Verify:* fixtures for (a) one isolated off-market bar → suppressed, (b) three consecutive wide bars → passes through, (c) a gap-and-hold → passes through; plus a measured check on historical spike-free trend days.
3. **Remove the hardcoded fallback** `currentPrice * 0.02` at `signal-orchestrator.ts:2279` (and any sibling found in the census): a missing ATR fails closed (no signal), never a silent 2 % (Kyle: no hard-coded fallbacks for governed values). *Verify:* census shows no literal ATR fallback on a geometry path; a test that a missing ATR produces no signal.
4. **Correct the labels:** the false *"14-period Wilder"* (`market-scanner.ts:18`, `LEVER_INVENTORY.md:168`) and the stale SIM line saying the DBS ATR comes from the MCE (`SYSTEM_IMPACT_MAP.md` ~`:579`). *Verify:* text at the ref.
5. **Document the one ATR** in the System Manual (estimator, window, bar source, what it guards against, what it deliberately does not) and the System Impact Map (its consumers). *Verify:* sections exist and match the code.
