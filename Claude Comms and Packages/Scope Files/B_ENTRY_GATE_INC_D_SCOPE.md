## Objectives and proof

1. **P6 — measure the whole Ready-to-Buy queue at each eligible refresh cycle.** For paper mode, record `crypto_spot` and `xstock_spot` separately at cycle start. Each count covers rows in `active` or `reconfirmed` across all refresh buckets, with a row counted once. Log the cycle identity and both class counts even for an empty queue. On a scheduled tick where the refresh is busy or the snapshot fails, record that outcome separately. Live mode and inactive classes are outside this prospective read.
   - **Proof:** Reconcile the cycle identities against the Central Clock refresh schedule and compute the per-class medians over all started cycles including zeros. Report observed busy ticks outside that denominator with their explicit skip records. Reconcile nonempty and empty fixture snapshots with the actual stored rows, including a status change and two classes in one cycle. An omitted zero sample, missing or duplicate cycle, or a class or mode leak must fail the check. A failed snapshot or unexplained gap makes the sample incomplete and requires a registered successor. A current median establishes a current population only.
2. **P8 — follow one evaluation from opportunity to its terminal result.** Cover paper-mode crypto evaluations for `inside_bar_reversal`, `reverse_impulse`, and positive-control `strong_bull_trend` at the eligible strategy dispatch. Count eligible strategy opportunities independent of candidate formation. Carry one ID from dispatch through no-signal or upstream refusal, target gate, and the Ready-to-Buy write call. Capture the durable write result after the storage upsert returns, including failures, separately from the admission marker and pre-write trace. Distinguish new queue entries and reissues where storage can prove the distinction. A durable upsert may replace a prior entry.
   - **Proof:** Every eligible opportunity has one terminal result. A target-gate refusal has zero write calls, an admitted candidate has one call and one durable success or failure, and an earlier refusal is named. Reconcile by strategy, not pooled totals. Local controls exercise no signal, gate refusal, committed new write, committed reissue and failed write. Remove an eligible event or suppress or misorder a required write and the verifier must fail. A live pass requires a positive control with a committed write and at least one actual candidate for each watched strategy. A strategy that never reaches the candidate seam is **insufficient for the gate-through-write leg**, with its measured upstream population and terminal reasons retained.

## Window and scope boundaries

It audits all enabled strategy entrances and exits so one lane cannot silently disappear. Keep evidence volume bounded for the three selected strategies and the fixed window without sampling eligible events inside that window. No changes to gates, thresholds, strategy enablement, mode, stored trade outcomes or the established P7 schedule. Any required authorization for a protected component is reviewed before Step 3.
