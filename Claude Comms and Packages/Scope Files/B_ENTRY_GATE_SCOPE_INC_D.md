## Objectives and proof

1. **P6 — measure the whole Ready-to-Buy queue at each eligible refresh cycle.** For paper mode, record `crypto_spot` and `xstock_spot` separately at cycle start. Each count covers rows in `active` or `reconfirmed` across all refresh buckets, with a row counted once. Log the cycle identity and both class counts even for an empty queue. On a scheduled tick where the refresh is busy or the snapshot fails, record that outcome separately. Live mode and inactive classes are outside this prospective read.
   - **Proof:** Reconcile every eligible emitted Central Clock tick ID, within the serving process, to exactly one started cycle or explicit busy skip. Use the emitted IDs even when the timing drifts. Compute per-class medians over all started cycles. A zero requires a successful snapshot explicitly reporting zero for that class. Busy skips are outside that denominator. Reconcile nonempty and empty fixture snapshots with stored rows, including a status change and two classes in one cycle. Omitted zeros, missing or duplicate cycles, and class or mode leaks must fail. A failed snapshot or unexplained gap makes the window incomplete and requires a registered successor. A current median establishes a current population only.
2. **P8 — follow one evaluation from opportunity to its terminal result.** Cover paper-mode crypto evaluations for `inside_bar_reversal`, `reverse_impulse`, and positive-control `strong_bull_trend`. Freeze dispatch eligibility before the window, independent of candidate formation. Carry one evaluation ID from eligible dispatch through no signal or earlier refusal, target-gate verdict, Ready-to-Buy service call, storage upsert attempt, and confirmed commit or failure. Record a Ready-to-Buy refusal or error before storage separately from an upsert failure. An error in later service work leaves an already confirmed commit intact. Distinguish new queue entries and reissues where storage proves that distinction.
   - **Proof:** For each strategy reconcile both ways against an independent census of eligible dispatch IDs. Each ID has one legal terminal outcome. Target refusals have zero Ready-to-Buy calls and zero upserts. A pre-upsert Ready-to-Buy refusal or error has one service call and zero upserts. An upsert attempt has a confirmed commit or a failure. Local controls cover no signal, target refusal, pre-upsert refusal or error, failed upsert, committed new entry, committed reissue, and a commit followed by a service error. Removing a whole evaluation trace or suppressing or misordering a required event must fail. The active control must commit a same-ID write in staging. A watched strategy with no target-gate opportunities is insufficient for its gate evidence. If all its candidates fail the gate, report the verified zero calls for those refusals. Claim a successful write for a watched strategy only with its own same-ID commit.

## Window and scope boundaries

Before deploy, register the UTC bounds for four complete eligible xStock regular sessions and use those same bounds for the paper crypto comparison. Pin the serving ref, modes, class set, strategy universe and comparison controls. Register the complete planned cycle population for P6 and eligible evaluation population for P8, the log-retention plan and the terminal-outcome reconciliation cutoff. A restart or unrelated deploy within the fixed window means incomplete; preserve that evidence and pre-register a successor. A missing record must be distinguishable from an observed zero.

Keep observation off the trading decision path. Reuse existing reads, identities and bounded evidence surfaces where they satisfy the proof. Step 2 compares a single consistent snapshot against the existing separate active/reconfirmed reads, and checks read cost before any extra per-cycle database access is proposed. It audits all enabled strategy entrances and exits so one lane cannot silently disappear. Keep evidence volume bounded for the three selected strategies and the fixed window without sampling eligible events inside that window. No changes to gates, thresholds, strategy enablement, mode, stored trade outcomes or the established P7 schedule. Any required authorization for a protected component is reviewed before Step 3.
