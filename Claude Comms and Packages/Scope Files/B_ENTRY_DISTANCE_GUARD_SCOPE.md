## 2. Objectives
1. **LIVE — a taker open at or through its stop or target is refused**, on both classes, by calling `refuseTakerBooking(fillPrice, stopPrice, targetPrice)` on the active engine's taker path after the fill is computed and before anything is written. No second copy of the logic.
   *Verify:* unit tests replay STZ, CEG, INTC, CRCL (xStock, fill below stop) and an at-target crypto case → refused with the primitive's reason; a fill strictly between passes. A source fence: the active booking calls the shared primitive. On staging after deploy: the refusal's counter and log line exist (positive control: the unit test); `#1168`'s four would be refused.
2. **LIVE — reward-to-risk at the fill must clear the strategy's live `min_rr`**, read through `getPerClassTargetGate(class, strategy)` — the same value the strategy's own guard used at birth, no new constant. `rr_fill = (target − fill) / (fill − stop)`.
   ⚠️ **This is NEW BEHAVIOUR ON AN EXISTING NUMBER, AND IT HAS A KNIFE EDGE (`ADJUSTMENT_FRAMEWORK` clauses 1-2).** Strategies whose RR is constant by construction sit just above their floor (e.g. a 2.0 RR against a 1.95 floor): an adverse fill of about **1.7 % of the stop distance** refuses them. **So OBJ-2 ships LIVE only if Step 2's pre-registered blast-radius read passes; otherwise it ships in SHADOW with the band** and the gap goes to Langston. *Verify:* Step 2 measures, per (strategy × class), how many post-reset paper opens would have been refused at their recorded fill (`intended_entry_price` vs `entry_price`), with the criterion committed before the query.
3. **SHADOW — the distance band.** Log, never refuse: adverse distance `fill − planned` against `max(spread, k·ATR)`, one `k` (a `module_constants` row, shadow-only), the favourable side in the same unit. **ATR read site and time named:** `signal.metadata.atr`, stamped at the signal's birth (`sizingContext.atr`, per symbol since `#581`), not re-read at the open. Spread: the open's own depth-gate snapshot. Each open logs one line with the would-refuse verdict. *Verify:* unit tests on both sides; on staging, the line on every taker open.
4. **No fabricated target at promotion.** `executePromotedSignal` refuses a row with no target instead of inventing `entry × 1.02`. *Verify:* unit test; Step 2 counts `rtb_signals` rows with a null target (expected 0 — then the fallback was dead, and goes under rule 18).
5. **Every refusal is visible and durable** — a new `OpenFailStage` (`ENTRY_GEOMETRY`) through `rtbMetricsService.recordOpenFailed` (and its zero-init list), plus a durable `archiveSignalEval` row (gate `entry_fill`), and visible in Filter Diagnostics. *Verify:* tests; on staging the stage appears in `/api/diagnostics/rtb-metrics` once one fires.
6. **Documented:** System Manual (the open path's fill-time checks and what they do not do) and System Impact Map (the active booking, the shared primitive's new caller, the new stage).
