## 4. What this means for the decision
1. **Five of the ten have no per-strategy reason at all** (strong_bull_trend, adaptive_flow, reverse_impulse, volatility_edge, support_bounce) — they were left off as part of a conservative first ship, with a promise to revisit after a few weeks of shadow data that was never kept. **Two have real reasons that still hold** (liquidity_trap: short-only; defensive_hedge: a crypto-only idea). **ORB has its own path** (15-minute validation, roadmap 25-14). **dhma and abcd_long** have only a group-level concern that was never tested.
2. **The nine that are on were never audited either.** The locked 2026-05-15 plan promised a per-strategy audit of the nine (Phase D.1) with pivot_shift, mean_reversion and range_trade on a watchlist; it has no completion record. breakout and inside_bar_reversal were on for three weeks with zero opens; vwap_pullback and morning_star made about 80 % of trades (`B_XSTOCK_CALIB_BASELINE_REPORT.md:18,140`).
3. **The standing intent (June, Langston- and Kyle-approved) is "a small obviously-stock-suited subset, never all at once."** That intent still stands and has no home past ORB — row 52 is now it.
