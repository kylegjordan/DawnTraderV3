## 4. Objectives — each with its verification
1. **TWO SEPARATE PREDICATES, never one (r2, Coltrane 1): `sourceHealthy` and `priceAdmissible`.** `sourceHealthy` = (a) the transport/shard is alive NOW, proved by recent provider heartbeat or other inbound control traffic that does not depend on any symbol moving; (b) this symbol's subscription is acknowledged in the CURRENT connection generation; (c) this book is trusted (checksum breaker) in the current generation. `priceAdmissible` = OBJ-2's age rule on the price's own observation/receipt age. A quiet symbol on a healthy source is `sourceHealthy` and may still be refused by `priceAdmissible`; ack + trust latched on a half-open connection is NOT `sourceHealthy`. Per feed: crypto WebSocket (all of a-c), crypto REST (the read's own observation time; no generation), xStock WebSocket (receipt of the last changed quote; replay-only = cold). **Verify:** mutation test — acknowledge and trust, then suppress all inbound traffic without a close event ⇒ `sourceHealthy` turns red; control — suppress only the symbol's price changes while the heartbeat continues ⇒ `sourceHealthy` stays green (the age rule may still refuse). Both definitions in the System Manual.
2. **THE MEASURED STANDARD — ONE METHOD, PARAMETERISED BY THE DISTANCE THE PRICE MUST NOT HAVE CROSSED (r3, Langston answer 4) — reviewed by Langston and Coltrane BEFORE any code reads it.** The rule, one for every symbol and both classes: *a price of age `a` may decide only if the one-sided upper confidence bound on the probability that the relevant executable side moved adversely by more than `D` within `a` is at most `p*`.* **Entry** instantiates `D = Δ = φ × 0.25 × |planEntry − planStop|` on the worse side — **`φ` is the AGE BUDGET, a pre-registered FRACTION of the window (r5, Langston C-1)**: a price admitted at its age limit must not have spent the whole window on staleness alone, because spread, depth walk and decision-to-fill latency (the terms OBJ-3 names) land on top; the remainder `(1 − φ)` is reserved for them. `φ` is pre-registered beside `p*` and `1 − δ`; without it OBJ-3 would be biased to FAIL by construction (and a FAIL resolves as OBJ-3 states, per checkpoint). **Exit** is a SEPARATE estimand under the same inferential framework (r4, Coltrane): *the probability that the executable exit side crossed EITHER contemporaneous boundary during age `a`* — direction-specific distances (current executable side to the stop; to the target; to any trailing boundary, each side-specific), its own sample and its own validation; missing boundary or path coverage ⇒ UNASSESSABLE ⇒ refuse. It is NOT one scalar distance: a stop and a target lie in opposite directions at different distances. Census #18's `budgetK × remaining room to the stop` (`mark-staleness.ts:15,197-198`) is a risk-consumption rule on the stop side only and says nothing about the target, so it is a precedent in kind, not the exit estimand (and its zero-room branch returns `floorMs` with `clamped: true`, `mark-staleness.ts:202-205` — the caller refuses separately; the exit derivation must not read "tightest" as "unassessable", r9); the crypto 2 s exit bound (#11/#12) is RE-DERIVED under the exit estimand rather than kept by assertion. Until the exit instantiation is derived and reviewed, the exit rows keep their current measured contracts. **`a` IS THE AGE OF THE EXACT PRICE AVAILABLE TO THE DECISION, ON THE OBSERVATION CLOCK WHERE ONE EXISTS (r3 Langston B2; r4 Coltrane).** Where a checkpoint has only receipt age (the xStock DB-snapshot rows #1, #2, #3, #5, #23 read `NOW() − MAX(captured_at)`): `capturedAt` is stamped when the frame is received, before buffering (`equity-spot-archiver.ts:334-338`), and the gate reads elapsed time since that persisted row (`active-dispatch.ts:74`, called `:183`). **Nothing is subtracted, because the delay is exactly the risk being bounded** (r8, Langston C-5). Receipt age lower-bounds observation age ONLY on a frame recognised as a venue update; on a replayed or unrecognised frame it can UNDERSTATE age (below). The 4 s sampling and ≤ 5 s flush (`ticker-batch-writer.ts:30`; Langston's F2, `1-system-manual/pm/decisions/2026-11-05_xstock-freshness-condition.md:16`, `:49`) are not a fixed floor (and are two read-sites, not a constant: the 5 s flush is code, `BATCH_FLUSH_INTERVAL_MS`; the 4 s is the live `passive_archive.b74_ticker_snapshot_min_interval_ms`, code default 1,000 — so any bound that uses them reads the LIVE constant in the central reader, never a hardcoded 9 s; r7 Langston condition 1), and market movement during that delay is exactly the risk being bounded; subtracting it would make an old price read younger. The remedy for that delay is coverage (reading the in-memory tick, OBJ-4/OBJ-6), not arithmetic. Where an upstream observation time is measurable it is used; otherwise the reader fails conservatively. The clock rule lives in OBJ-6's central reader, never at a call site. **⚠️ RECEIPT AGE CAN ALSO UNDERSTATE AGE, NOT ONLY LOWER-BOUND IT (r6, Coltrane; re-derived by CC-B at the ref):** the in-memory tick correctly keeps the OLD `tsMs` on a replayed or unrecognised frame (`equity-spot-archiver.ts:315-326`, the PLTR 20.8-hour replay lesson), but the same frame is still buffered as a DB row stamped `capturedAt: new Date()` (`:338`) — so every DB-snapshot reader (#1, #2, #3, #5, #23) reads a subscribe-replay of an hours-old book as fresh. **Binding on OBJ-2 and OBJ-6:** every one of those readers takes its age from the cold-preserving in-memory tick, or from a trustworthy upstream observation time; where neither exists, it applies a pre-registered conservative uncertainty bound or the price is UNASSESSABLE ⇒ refuse. Same-clock movement sampling does not cure the reset. **A lower bound licenses a REFUSAL, never an ADMISSION (r9, Langston):** `active-dispatch.ts:184` admits on one today (`if (ageMs === null || ageMs > safety.activeFillMaxAgeMs) { … return; }`), and `tickerSnapColumns` (`shared/schema.ts:5110-5137`) carries no frame type and no `observed` flag — so the DB path is ~9 s pessimistic on an update frame and ARBITRARILY OPTIMISTIC on a replay. **Census rows #1, #2, #3, #5, #23 are UNASSESSABLE on a non-`update` frame until OBJ-6's central reader carries the `observed` discriminator** (which the in-memory tick already supplies). *(Finding, §9.4 disposition 1 — folded here: these four entry gates and the birth quote are OBJ-6's own rows, so this batch is the fix; it is today's live exposure of the 10-20 audit's unresolved item 5.)* **AT SIGNAL BIRTH THE GATE IS POST-PLAN, ON THE SAME PRICE (r5, Langston C-2).** At birth the plan's entry and stop are derived from the very price under test (crypto `signal-orchestrator.ts:2582-2583` at `47b9360f7`, `:2452` at `c8dfa3db1`; xStock `scanner.ts:922`), so Δ cannot exist before the read.
