## 1. The defect, in one paragraph
"Fresh" has no single meaning. **46 places** check a price's age; **22 change a trading action**, with bounds of 2, 5, 8, 15, 90 and 300 seconds, 5 and 30 minutes, and a per-symbol formula — chosen batch by batch, mostly hardcoded, none derived from how far prices actually move. **And the one checkpoint that matters most has no bound at all:** crypto signal birth reads whatever price the cache holds (`signal-orchestrator.ts:2451-2458`, re-derived), and for ~94 % of births (10-20 audit) that is a 30-60 s REST poll, because only symbols with an open position or a queued RTB row are on the trading WebSocket. A reconnect then drops even the queued ones (`kraken-websocket-adapter.ts:776-782`, re-derived). xStock signal birth prices off the last 15-minute bar's close, whose own age nothing bounds (census #46).

## 4. Objectives — each with its verification
1. **ONE DEFINITION OF "CURRENT", per feed, written down before any number.** Crypto WebSocket (socket alive + subscription acknowledged since the last reconnect + book trusted by the checksum breaker), crypto REST (the read's own observation time), xStock WebSocket (receipt of the last changed quote; replay-only = cold). A quiet market on a live, acknowledged, trusted feed is current; a dead or unacknowledged subscription is not. **Verify:** the definition is in the System Manual; a test per feed shows a quiet-but-live symbol admitted and a silent-dead one refused.
2. **THE MEASURED STANDARD — the rule, the method and the numbers, reviewed by Langston and Coltrane BEFORE any code reads it.** One rule for every symbol and both classes: *a price of age `a` may decide only if, on that symbol's recorded movement, the chance the price moved against the entry by more than the plan's 25 % window within `a` is below a stated level.* Derived per symbol (and per session for xStock) from the recorded archives; the window comes from the plan at the moment of decision, so the admissible age is per decision, not a fixed table. **Verify:** a design document with the method, the confidence level and the per-symbol outputs; Langston + Coltrane PROCEED recorded; the numbers are reproducible by a committed query.
3. **CHECKED AGAINST PLANNED-VS-FILLED — pre-registered before the data is read.** The standard predicts, for decisions admitted at age `a`, how often the fill lands outside the window; increment A's fill economics and increment B's per-checkpoint age records give the observed rate. **Verify:** the criterion is committed before the read; the standard is adopted only if the observed rate is within the predicted band; a FAIL goes back to the method, not to a hand-picked number.
4. **COVERAGE — crypto signal birth gets a price that can meet the standard** (absorbs `B-SQE-FEED-FANOUT`). Birth candidates are priced from the trading WebSocket, widened in stages with the message rate measured (the 2026-10-13 storm is the reason for stages; the checksum breaker is live since `725a7f3f5`). **Verify:** the share of crypto birth evaluations priced from a current source, before vs after, per stage; zero `Exceeded msg rate` lines and zero handshake 429s across the widening.
5. **A RECONNECT BRINGS BACK EVERYTHING THAT WAS SUBSCRIBED** — open positions and RTB-queued symbols, on both the reconnect branch and `softResubscribeAll`, with a retry when the handshake is slow and a log on failure. **Verify:** a test drops the socket with a queued symbol subscribed and shows it re-subscribed; a slow-handshake test shows the retry; on staging, the next natural reconnect re-subscribes the queued set (counted).
6. **THE STANDARD IS APPLIED AT EVERY DECIDING CHECKPOINT** — signal birth (both classes), the RTB refresh, promotion (replacing increment B's interim 300,000 ms seed, KD-31), resting-order and taker entry fills, the xStock dispatch and open gates, and exits — **each of the 22 deciding rows in the census either reads the standard or carries a written reason it does not** (e.g. operator closes, ungated by design). The VTS rows are dispositioned explicitly (the VTS is a learning lane, not the trading pipeline). **Verify:** a table in the pre-audit mapping every deciding row to "reads the standard" or "excluded because …"; a compile-forced reader so a new checkpoint cannot bypass it.
7. **A SYMBOL THAT CANNOT MEET THE STANDARD IS REFUSED, COUNTED AND VISIBLE** — in Filter Diagnostics, per class and per checkpoint, never a silent drop. **Verify:** the Filter Diagnostics panel shows the refusal stage with counts on staging, navigated in Claude-in-Chrome.
8. **KYLE'S DECISION, OWED BEFORE OBJ-6 IS BUILT — OFF-HOURS xSTOCK ENTRIES.** A real standard will refuse many quiet-hour xStock entries (the 10-20 audit: xStock refresh off-hours ≤ 15 s only 17 %). His 09-03 ruling says the standard does not loosen; the 11-04 PM sweep found the trade-off itself was never put to him in plain terms. **Verify:** the trade-off (with the measured share of off-hours candidates the standard would refuse) is sent to Kyle and his answer recorded on `#1222` before OBJ-6's xStock half is built.
9. **LEGACY FOUND BY THE CENSUS IS REMOVED** (rule 18): `alert-context-engine.ts` (no importer) and the unreachable `health-monitor.ts:742` branch, each with the full blast-radius check and a `DELETED_COMPONENTS_LOG` entry; the stale `fx5Snapshot` comment corrected. **Verify:** tsc baseline unchanged; zero references at the ref.
10. **STEP 7, PRE-REGISTERED AT STEP 2** — magnitudes per class and checkpoint (refusal shares, birth coverage, the planned-vs-filled rate), and what would count as FAIL.
