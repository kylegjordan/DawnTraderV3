## 1. The defect, in one paragraph
"Fresh" has no single meaning. **46 places** check a price's age; **26 decide something** (census, enumerated by row id: 10 entry-side + B0, 5 exit-side, the rest excluded or demoted with reasons), with bounds of 2, 5, 8, 15, 90 and 300 seconds, 5 and 30 minutes, and a per-symbol formula — chosen batch by batch, mostly hardcoded, none derived from how far prices actually move. **And the one checkpoint that matters most has no bound at all (census B0):** crypto signal birth reads whatever price the cache holds (`signal-orchestrator.ts:2452`, only guard `:2456`, re-derived; the stale Batch-18 comment is at `:2450-2451`), and for ~94 % of births (10-20 audit) that is a 30-60 s REST poll, because only symbols with an open position or a queued RTB row are on the trading WebSocket. A reconnect then drops even the queued ones (`kraken-websocket-adapter.ts:776-782`, re-derived). xStock signal birth prices off the last 15-minute bar's close, whose own age nothing bounds (census #46).

## 4. Objectives — each with its verification
1. **TWO SEPARATE PREDICATES, never one (r2, Coltrane 1): `sourceHealthy` and `priceAdmissible`.** `sourceHealthy` = (a) the transport/shard is alive NOW, proved by recent provider heartbeat or other inbound control traffic that does not depend on any symbol moving; (b) this symbol's subscription is acknowledged in the CURRENT connection generation; (c) this book is trusted (checksum breaker) in the current generation. `priceAdmissible` = OBJ-2's age rule on the price's own observation/receipt age. A quiet symbol on a healthy source is `sourceHealthy` and may still be refused by `priceAdmissible`; ack + trust latched on a half-open connection is NOT `sourceHealthy`. Per feed: crypto WebSocket (all of a-c), crypto REST (the read's own observation time; no generation), xStock WebSocket (receipt of the last changed quote; replay-only = cold). **Verify:** mutation test — acknowledge and trust, then suppress all inbound traffic without a close event ⇒ `sourceHealthy` turns red; control — suppress only the symbol's price changes while the heartbeat continues ⇒ `sourceHealthy` stays green (the age rule may still refuse). Both definitions in the System Manual.
2. **THE MEASURED STANDARD — ONE METHOD, PARAMETERISED BY THE DISTANCE THE PRICE MUST NOT HAVE CROSSED (r3, Langston answer 4) — reviewed by Langston and Coltrane BEFORE any code reads it.** The rule, one for every symbol and both classes: *a price of age `a` may decide only if the one-sided upper confidence bound on the probability that the relevant executable side moved adversely by more than `D` within `a` is at most `p*`.* **Entry** instantiates `D = Δ = 0.25 × |planEntry − planStop|` on the worse side (KD-10). **Exit** instantiates `D` = the stop budget, with the exit's own event (crossing the stop or target — Coltrane 5), derived and reviewed separately; census #18's `clamp(budget/σ, 15 s, 300 s)` is already this in kind, and the crypto 2 s exit bound (#11/#12) is RE-DERIVED under it rather than kept by assertion. Until the exit instantiation is derived and reviewed, the exit rows keep their current measured contracts. **`a` IS OBSERVATION AGE (r3, Langston B2).** Where a checkpoint has only receipt age (the xStock DB-snapshot rows #1, #2, #3, #5, #23 read `NOW() − MAX(captured_at)`), the per-checkpoint capture-pipeline lag floor (~9 s on xStock: 4 s sampling + the 5 s batch flush, `ticker-batch-writer.ts:30`; Langston's F2, `1-system-manual/pm/decisions/2026-11-05_xstock-freshness-condition.md:16`, `:49`) is subtracted or declared — **inside OBJ-6's central reader, never as arithmetic at a call site** — so our own lag is never charged to the market's movement distribution, which would refuse quiet names hardest. **The movement sample is drawn from the same price quantity the decision prices off** (the method states its price basis; a later change in `B-PLAN-PRICE-BASIS` re-derives the standard — §5). **Two numbers, not one (r2, Coltrane 2):** `p*` — the maximum tolerated probability of being outside the window — and `1 − δ` — the statistical confidence required before claiming the true probability is at most `p*`. The METHOD is ours under KD-34 (owner + Langston + Coltrane, recorded by PM Claude); **the LEVEL reaches Kyle as its consequence, not as a probability — merged with OBJ-8 into ONE ask** (r3, Langston answer 2): refusal shares per class and session at each candidate `(p*, 1 − δ)` the group proposes. **Runtime form (r2, Coltrane 3):** per decision, from a VERSIONED, MONOTONE lookup over `(symbol, session, age)` of the upper bound on adverse executable-side displacement — admit only where that bound is within the plan's `Δ`; the envelope is forced monotone so a longer age can never read safer through sample noise. A fixed reference-window table is rejected (it would reintroduce an arbitrary bound when stop distances differ). **Defined exactly BEFORE any data is read:** the side used, the start and decision timestamps, observation vs receipt clock, and the treatment of an absent endpoint quote. **Pre-registered:** the sampling window, the minimum effective sample, the dependence treatment (blocking), the training/holdout split, and pooling — per symbol is Kyle's directive, so pooling thin symbols is unavailable unless the method states and proves an exchangeability rule. **Missing coverage, too few independent observations, or an uncertified archive ⇒ UNASSESSABLE ⇒ refuse — never read as "zero moves."** **The confidence is SIMULTANEOUS, not pointwise (r3b, Coltrane):** a published version serves a whole `(symbol, session, age)` surface and the admissible-age boundary is selected by scanning it, so a per-cell `1 − δ` does not bound the chance that at least one unsafe cell reads safe. The Step-2 pre-registration makes the bound simultaneous / selection-valid over every cell one published version may serve, or explicitly allocates `δ` across that population; monotonicisation, boundary selection, tuning and any pooling test all run INSIDE that corrected procedure. The forward fill check (OBJ-3) cannot repair this — it tests a different outcome. xStock: three strata — regular / extended / overnight — under the SAME `p*`, each with a minimum-sample guard and an explicit refusal where a stratum is thin (never a `?? 0`, `#546`); the quiet-hours rule does not loosen — off-hours admissible ages will often collapse or be unassessable. **Verify:** the design document with all of the above; Langston + Coltrane PROCEED recorded; `p*` and `1 − δ` recorded by PM as a KD-34 decision; the surface reproducible from a committed query.
3. **AN EXTERNAL FALSIFICATION CHECK, NOT A PREDICTION OF THE SAME VARIABLE (r2, Coltrane 4).** Fill displacement also carries spread, depth walk and decision-to-fill latency, so planned-vs-filled is a test the standard can fail, not the quantity it models. **Pre-registered** (before the read), on a forward SHADOW population once increment B records checkpoint ages (**carrier, r3 Langston:** three writers record age at the four KD-48 sites #1/#2/#3/#5 — KD-48's watch, increment B's P10 record, and this batch — so the ONE carrier is settled with CC-C at Step 2; and the KD-48 week-over-week series splits at any bound change, so that window is filed as a KD-56 register entry at this pre-registration): every candidate's plan/window, checkpoint, source, age and the model's verdict; the actual fill for admitted candidates; the contemporaneous executable-side counterfactual for shadow-refused candidates; the coverage state and missingness reason. Read per class / checkpoint / session / age band, never one pooled rate; the shadow-refused cohort reported separately for opportunity cost; a held-out forward window. **A FAIL keeps the standard unapplied and fail-closed** — it cannot send anyone back to choose a friendlier number. **Verify:** the pre-registration committed before the window; the read run as registered.
