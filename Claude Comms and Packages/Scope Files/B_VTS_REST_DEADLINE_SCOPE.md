## Objectives and proof

1. **Recover what can already be measured.** Trace paper maker placement, the price-refusal paths, and fill/drop through the durable trade and restart. Specify which current records can demonstrate any of the required times and which cannot. **Proof:** exact-identity controls distinguish a pending placement from a committed fill and a linked never-filled expiry. A same-symbol attempt, a taker fill, and a missing timestamp cannot count as evidence about a particular maker order.
2. **Keep the missing facts needed to judge a rest.** The proposed observation measure joins the exact paper rest, its placement and deadline, its terminal result and decision time, local receipt time of any winning quote, and sufficient evidence to attribute a pricing refusal that spans its deadline. Step 2 picks the smallest existing durable writer and decides whether current data already supplies any part. **Proof:** the recovered or new observations identify real fills and no-fill outcomes after restart without treating unrecorded timestamps or an incomplete refusal history as zero. Preserve supported legacy records, the C3 clocks and comparator, the existing typed never-filled exclusions, and current outcomes. Change records only for this measurement.
3. **Register and read the paper population before disposition.** Pre-register class, coverage, denominator, outcome and end rule at Step 2, along with any required observation gate. Report separately a genuine price crossing received at or after the deadline that still filled during a refusal span, a valid earlier quote processed late, an expired held rest or drop with no fill, and insufficient evidence. **Proof:** include positive valid fill, late-check and refusal/expiry controls and challenge the clock-order measurement with a post-deadline quote. Apply #1073’s conversion trigger only to evidence that identifies the actual rest, its governing deadline, order of events, and refusal span. A zero only grades the measured population with intact coverage. Any lifecycle change returns to scope review.
