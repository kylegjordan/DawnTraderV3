**COUNTS, NOT RATES (Langston Step-4 gate B condition, binds the Step-8 read):** the VTS size counters (`xsRestSizeShort/Unknown`, `xsExitFiredSizeShort/Unknown`) and the paper `[XS_SIZE]` counters are reported as COUNTS. A rate needs matched denominators added under the same guard first.

## 5. PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY
The VTS already sells on the bid and buys on the ask. Paper xStock still decides stops and targets on the midpoint, because in September the bid trigger fired a false stop on a blown-out book (MDB). Spread-blown, live since yesterday, now holds those books before any trigger runs: since then the bid and the midpoint disagreed only 21 times, all in normal trading hours on books under 0.8% wide — the ordinary half-spread a real seller would pay. So the plan moves paper stops and targets to the bid, but only while spread-blown and the book guard are on (otherwise it falls back to the midpoint). It adds one size check shared by paper and the VTS: exits always fire, but resting fills and VTS entries are refused if the book can't hold our size or doesn't say. It also flags crossed books, and marks a new calibration boundary because exit results change again.
