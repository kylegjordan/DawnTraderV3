### A1. Intent and deliberately preserved behavior

- Merged scope goal and objective 5, Kyle October 9: use a plausible Kraken sell bid on the same tick with enough size. There is no forced exit or time limit, and an expected thin-book hold pages nobody. Read the original bid-trigger work and increment B's `B_XSTOCK_BID_TRIGGER_RELAND_INCB_PRE_AUDIT` §0.r2 call 2 and §0.r2c C2. They deliberately made trigger size trace-only and kept the archived crossed-book walk for normal closes. That protected against indefinite holds and allowed an estimated remainder. The proposal changes paper taker **fill eligibility** when the quote is short, unknown or invalid. It preserves trace-only size evaluation at the trigger. It explicitly retires the archived crossed-book walk for this subset. This needs review because it is a behavior change with a risk of longer holds.
- Increment 4b, `f7ac48c80`, deliberately rejected a latched exit. C-P5's retirement, `8fac86e5e`, left the existing spread rules in charge. Keep fresh exit evaluation on each cycle and preserve guard and spread-switch semantics. An old stop breach grants no permission to exit on a later recovered tick.
- `B-FEED-MISMATCH-FIX` P1 / `51e35b195` made the archive witness and signed divergence drive existing refusal/yield. P2 gave an operator flatten one attempt and a governed observed-price fallback when no book is usable. Preserve the flatten's loss fence and immediate refusal visibility, and verify actual closure rather than a successful route return. Keep VTS, live mode, entries, maker bookings, crypto and time-only TEC/max-hold paths outside the change. KD-36 keeps latent trailing and break-even features off in paper and VTS.
- Original F1 execution-price intent in `bridge/canonical` establishes actual execution prices and auditable fills. That corpus supplies no current xStock raw-frame age or size rule. The System Manual §§3.5.1b and 18.0.1 describes the shared pricing/walk behavior. It leaves the paper same-frame relationship and age semantics unspecified. Step 10 owes those semantics and the relevant SIM dependency update if approved.

## Implementation plan

1. **Carry one immutable quote from the judged frame** (A1, A3). Add raw-only provenance at parse time: producer sequence or identity, source envelope discriminant, monotonic receipt time and connection generation. Require a usable `update` from the current generation. Neither inherited mark freshness, a snapshot arriving after reconnect, nor wall-clock equality with another frame qualifies. No venue timestamp is available. Pass exact frame, symbol, position ID, mode/class, trigger cause and actual basis from the ordinary monitor to the fill boundary. Keep mark audit fields and TEC switch semantics. With a guard/spread override that deliberately chooses a mark decision, allow a fill only if that cycle's own real raw quote qualifies, and record the actual trigger basis and independent fill basis. Clear eligibility on disconnection/reconnect by generation without expiring the shared mark or mutating other consumers' cache.
2. **Subject eligible paper taker closes to one final acceptance check** (A1–A3). Include normal stop/target price exits, genuine price-based stop variants if enabled, stops while resting and policy-permitted maker-to-taker target fallback, including expiry. Classify by actual cause, so time-only timeout never becomes a price stop by its persisted label. After position/config awaits, match the re-read position, grade the original quote's monotonic age, require real plausible uncrossed sides, and require finite positive bid plus `bidQty >= current requested quantity` in units. Recheck eligibility and age immediately before simulated fill acceptance, with no intervening external await. Pass only the accepted bid and size to the existing paper fill path. At storage precision, record that bid with the full requested quantity walked and zero extrapolation. Store accepted-frame identity, basis and receipt age in additive typed close metadata. Existing decision mark, independently read ticker witness and archive-depth age retain their meanings. The archive and a later raw cache entry are ineligible substitutes. Where diagnostic witness is retained, read it outside the accept decision. Exact boundary is simulated fill computation, followed by persistence of that accepted result with no repricing. A database recording delay is not additional quote life. Require a successful matching close record before emitting learning/exit-decision side effects for this subset, using the same writer. The existing order of learning dispatch before the trade update is insufficient for a failed-recording test. No broader change to unrelated close paths.
3. **Retry and report without booking a fictitious exit** (A1–A3). When the selected quote fails, leave the position open. Neither archive walk, `walk_yield`, nor extrapolation may let this subset exit. Separate stale/replay, malformed book, unknown size and short size in existing close/size diagnostics. Take the next independent monitor evaluation. A recovered price that no longer triggers leaves the position open. Retain existing lifecycle changes such as expired maker rest removal, mark/P&L updates and paused TEC rules. Reuse per-position refusal bookkeeping only if it can keep these counters separate from yield and paging. Bound repeated logging with one at-cap marker per episode, candidate 60 eligible refused attempts for comparison, continue rechecking without forced close, and end on an evaluable clear or closure/removal. Expected thin or quiet episodes page nobody. Reuse existing impairment alerts and operator escape. Restarts can end the diagnostic episode and must never create a pending close. Do not land a hard age budget or cap from this draft alone.
4. **Separate mechanism proof from age/size policy and accept deployment only under the existing gates** (A1–A3). Offline, run the real monitor, Paper Order Placer and close persistence using deterministic fixtures. Use the 15,000 ms warmth value as one comparison in the proof. Measure accepted, held, and recovered attempts and hold length on covered historical cases where raw receipt and size are provable. Mark unavailable coverage explicitly. Require review of the actual budget and hold trade-off before behavioral enforcement. If available retained data cannot bound the effect, report the coverage gap for review. Any forward sampling proposal needs its own approval and registration before use. Recommend paper xStock policy epoch 8→9 if the behavior lands, using the existing class-specific versioning, with other modes/classes unchanged. No proposed staging soak in this draft. Later Step 7 is a point-in-time check of exact deployed build/config, paper trade UI and any actual scoped closes/refusals available at that check. If no scoped event occurred, record live-unexercised and use the mandatory offline proofs as such. The active Phase A fence and all protected windows remain in force.
5. **Keep the folded-issue dispositions tied to evidence** (A1, A2). File the #962 closeout at row 2 Step 11 using the 14 frozen maker cases, separate 100/100 maker cohort, direct HOOD log and five X2 controls, including midpoint mutation. Keep #1087, GE row 39 and alert `e473d7da` with their existing parent dependencies. Carry the architecture policy and read coverage into the System Manual/SIM, RUNNING_ISSUES, BATCH_CATALOG, change list and progress/completion record at the applicable boundaries. Review class/doc set before implementation.

**Required discriminating controls.** Run the ordinary monitor and real close/learning writer. With a correct fresh, full bid A and a later agreeing stale archive or divergent bid B, the bookable price remains A. With short/unknown size, malformed or crossed sides, inherited fresh mark on replay, same-millisecond different frame, reconnect or an awaited operation that makes A old, assert no close/learning/delete writes. The next valid tick above the stop stays open, and a later fresh, full breach closes exactly once. Include warm archive control, wrong symbol/position/mode, sub-epsilon shortfall, no book, float/precision bounds, partial failure before trade recording, restart and capped refusal bookkeeping, old metadata readers, maker rest and both maker policies, guard/spread override, cause collisions, operator flatten success/fenced refusal and exclusions for VTS/live/crypto/entries/time-only. Fail the test by swapping in the archive, removing the post-await freshness check, or admitting size just below required. No positive-control `closePosition` mock.

**Review request.** Rule on the same-passed-frame implementation boundary, the strict size policy against increment B's deliberate remainder estimate, the benchmark and evidence gate for a hard age/cap decision, and the class and document set. Review watches specifically for loss of operator escape and silent widening to other modes.

**Plain language.** A paper xStock stop can see one sell quote and record the sale at a different, older one. The plan makes the sale use the same fresh quote, with enough shares offered for the whole trade. If the quote cannot support the sale, the position stays open and gets checked again. The offline proof must show both that behavior and the case where a valid quote later allows the sale.
