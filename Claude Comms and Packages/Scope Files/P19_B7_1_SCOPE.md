## Objective
Replace the friction-blind `finalScore` ranker on the live picker (`getRankedSignals`, `ready_to_buy_service.ts:1671`) with a **risk-normalized, net-of-cost expected-value ranker = the expected R-multiple**, so the system selects the best opportunity **across asset classes** on a common, comparable basis (Kyle's core requirement). Justified on the **structural friction-blindness** (RANK-by-gross-finalScore → EV-GATE-bounces-the-net-negative-pick), NOT the fragile anti-predictive r (which the roadmap mandates re-validating + whose B63 siblings don't replicate). The calibrated win-probability that makes it fully trustworthy is **Phase-25** (data-gated on real outcomes).

## The ranking score — CONFIRMED by code-verify
```
rank by  R = netEV ÷ risk_price        (descending)
  netEV     = evaluateTradeExpectancy's value — VERIFIED in PRICE-delta units
              (net-expectancy-kernel.ts:114-115: pWin·distTarget − pLoss·distStop − frictionPrice;
               friction already ×entry → price units)
  risk_price = (entry − stop), FLOORED at risk_floor
```
- netEV is **price-delta** (verified), so `R = netEV ÷ risk_price` is dimensionally clean (price ÷ price = dimensionless R-multiple). The earlier "double-normalization" worry assumed netEV was ÷entry — code-verify proved it is NOT, so this form is correct.
- R-units make a crypto candidate and a tokenized-stock candidate comparable — the field-standard cross-asset normalization (Van Tharp R-multiples / CTA vol-scaling / Kelly growth-optimal for sequential single-bets).
- **RANK distinct from GATE:** the ranker sets ORDER; the gate still independently decides GO/NO-GO at `netEV > 0`. An all-net-negative pool = clean no-trade under both.

So OBJ-2 = a **~2-line PASS-THROUGH** (add `netRewardToRisk` to the wrapper destructure + the result type) + rank by it — **reuse-over-recompute**, which auto-satisfies the "same friction model, price units" concern (it is literally the kernel's own quantities → zero two-errors-cancel risk; the per-term dimensional unit-test still gets written as a guard). **Side-effect (trap-7) is ALREADY architecturally clean:** the KERNEL is pure (docstring `net-expectancy-kernel.ts:77-80`: "zero side effects… safe for DSS and RTB"); the EV-input-SAMPLE side-effect lives in the WRAPPER (`paper-execution-engine.ts:2094`), NOT the kernel. **For pool-wide rank-time R, call the pure kernel (`computeNetExpectancyKernel`) directly, NOT the sample-recording `evaluateTradeExpectancy` wrapper** — that IS the no-double-EV-sample design, by an architecture deliberately built for RTB/DSS high-throughput. **The no-double-EV-sample test still applies** (Step-4 anchor: prove the EV-input dataset row-count is unchanged vs a gate-only cycle). **Touch points = 3, not 2 (CC-A):** the wrapper destructure (`expectancy.ts:648`) + the returned literal (`:657-668`) + the **`TradeExpectancyResult` interface definition** itself (add the `netRewardToRisk` field or tsc rejects the return). **Dimensional unit-test anchors at the CALLER boundary (Langston FACT-1):** assert the `×entryPrice` friction coercion at `expectancy.ts:627` (where friction becomes a price), NOT just at the kernel — the kernel is a pure subtractor that trusts its injected `totalFriction`, so a future %-friction injection would silently break netEV units and the kernel's own math would never catch it. **Sign/guard ordering (CC-A):** the kernel's `distStop>0 ? **OBJ-3 — degenerate-geometry handling: REJECT primary, floor secondary (CC-A final).** The geometry gate today enforces sign/validity only (verified `signal-target-normalizer.ts:80-84`) — a near-zero `(entry−stop)` PASSES. **Reject the un-executable near-zero-stop trade upstream** (a tiny stop is un-executable; the sizer would size nonsense) — reject KILLS it; the **risk_price floor is ONLY the ranker's arithmetic infinity-guard** and must NOT mask a degenerate executed stop. **Reject + floor basis = MICROSTRUCTURE-derived, CAPITAL-INDEPENDENT (Langston Q1 — corrects his earlier over-unification):** `max(min_ATR_fraction × ATR, min_tick × multiple)` — **min-ATR-fraction PRIMARY** (regime/price-scaling, cross-asset-clean = the whole point of this batch), with **min-tick OR'd in underneath as the absolute-executability floor only**. This is **NOT the same number as the OBJ-5 clamp-bind boundary** (which is capital/heat-dependent — notional + heat caps scale with equity). They share the *concept* ("the sizer can't honor risk-normalization here"), NOT a single value — forcing one numeric threshold to serve both was the over-unification, now dropped. They coincide only at the degenerate extreme where a near-zero stop blows unclamped quantity through the cap.

**OBJ-4 — shadow A/B instrumentation (the Phase-25 proof harness).** Extend reorg-B4 `rtb_shadow_pairings` to capture the **R-multiple** at decision time + whether `pWin` was real-DI-derived or floor-defaulted. **Selection-IC = per-cycle CROSS-SECTIONAL Spearman IC** (within each cycle's candidate set, did predicted-R order realized-R?), then study the **distribution of those per-cycle ICs with window-CLUSTERED standard errors** — the correct Grinold formulation (CC-A, Langston-adopted; supersedes pooling the whole sample). Computed over the **FULL candidate pool (promoted + non-promoted** — isolates ranker skill from the promotion cut), reported **per-regime-family (Simpson's paradox: a positive aggregate can hide a negative IC in one of the 5 regimes, and the go/no-go rides on it)**, with a **min-N gate + CI** (per-cycle IC on a 20-30 candidate set is noise) and **§19.4 sibling-control on CLEAN windows**. This IS the Phase-25 GO/NO-GO that the new ranker actually beats friction (one-pick-per-cycle is low-breadth — Grinold — so genuinely-positive selection-IC is the proof we're not just paying friction).

**OBJ-5 — ranking↔sizing coherence: enforced invariant + clamp telemetry.** Code-verify confirmed the sizer is **fixed-fractional-RISK** (`paper-position-sizing.ts:179` `quantity = riskAmount ÷ stopDistance`) → R-rank is coherent with sizing. **Assert risk-normalized sizing as a documented enforced invariant — as an UPPER BOUND, not equality (CC-A):** every position risks **AT MOST** `riskAmount` (1R), with the clamp/scale flags marking the sub-1R exceptions. Written as strict equality it false-alarms on every clamped position; written as "≤ R + flagged exceptions" it is airtight and still catches a future notional-sizer change (under which R-rank silently decoheres). Its **clamps** (maxPositionPercentPct / maxTotalExposurePct / notional-cap×0.97 / per-symbol-or-portfolio-heat cap) make it piecewise-notional when binding; the `wasClamped` flag is already logged (`:228`). **★ But `wasClamped` alone is INSUFFICIENT (CC-A finding A.2b):** there is a SECOND risk-fraction modulator — `correlationScale = getScalingFactor(mode, symbol)` (`risk-concentration.ts:412`, P19-B4b D5) — applied AFTER risk-sizing (`quantity ×= correlationScale` when `<1`), which scales a covariance-correlated position **below** `riskAmount` and **never flips `wasClamped`**. A watch keyed on the boolean alone is blind to covariance decoherence. **So capture in the shadow layer the single field `effective_risk_fraction_ratio = sized_risk ÷ intended_risk` per candidate** — it absorbs BOTH the notional/heat clamp AND the covariance scale. Bind = ratio materially < 1. **Objective bar (Langston, hard pass/fail):** **>~15-20% bind → the sizer is piecewise-notional in practice, R-rank silently overstates the top of the book → the honest ranker becomes realized-$EV net of friction at the POST-CLAMP executed size; <~5% → noise, keep R; in-between = judgment.** The realized-$EV ranker stays **SHELVED as the contingency** (NOT built now — code-verify confirmed genuine fixed-fractional-risk, so path (a) holds); **IF the measured bind-rate comes back high, the realized-$EV swap gets a NAMED §13 home the moment it's confirmed, not "later."** Bind-rate is a paper-run measurement (needs paper-active data). **★ The OBJ-3 reject/floor and THIS clamp-bind boundary are CONCEPTUALLY linked but NOT one number (Langston Q1 final correction): the reject/floor is microstructure-derived + capital-independent; the clamp-bind is capital/heat-dependent. They share the concept ("the sizer can't honor risk-normalization here"), and coincide only at the degenerate near-zero-stop extreme — do not force a single numeric threshold to serve both.**
