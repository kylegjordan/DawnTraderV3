## 0. What this batch is for
The crypto recorder (the passive archive, `crypto-spot-archiver.ts`) keeps a durable price history for every crypto pair over two Kraken connections. On 2026-10-21 10:19:55Z one connection (shard0, 196 of 408 coins) was cut by the venue, its 9th routine cut that day. It reconnected, took in its opening data, and then received nothing for 14 days while reporting `connected=true`. It recovered only when the 2026-11-04 12:13:26Z deploy restarted it. The recorder reconnects only after a close, sends no ping, never checks that a subscription was accepted, and prints its own silence every minute without acting on it. Nothing else watched it. **After this batch, a silent recorder connection is noticed and replaced within about a minute, "connected" means data is arriving, the feed-health grade sees a half-dead feed, and the 14-day hole is marked in the records it damaged.**

## 1. What is there (read at `origin/migration/aws-supabase` `8b71855f1`; the load-bearing lines re-read by CC-B)
- **Reconnect only on close.** `connectShard` (`crypto-spot-archiver.ts:215-239`): `ws.on('close')` → `scheduleReconnect` (`:241-247`); `ws.on('error')` only logs (`:236-238`). There is exactly ONE call site of `scheduleReconnect` (`:233`).
- **Silence is measured and discarded.** `lastMsgAt` is stamped on every frame (`handleMessage`, `:178`) and read only by the 60 s health logger (`:249-260`), which prints `last_msg_age_ms` and resets `rowsPersistedLastMinute`. `connected=` is `readyState === OPEN` (`:255`).
- **No subscription check.** `subscribe` (`:201-213`) sends two frames (ohlc 1 m, ticker) and logs "subscribed" without waiting for Kraken's reply.
- **Readers of its state:** `getCryptoSpotStats` (`:60`) is read by `drift-dashboard-aggregator.ts:895` (the Passive Archive panel). Census of every reader of `crypto_spot_ticker_snap` / `crypto_spot_ohlc_1m` is a Step-2 item (see §5).
- **The pattern already exists for xStock.** `equity-spot-archiver.ts:534-613` (`B-PRICE-STALENESS-BOUND` P2): a 30 s tick, DB-resolved two-tier threshold (`xstock_fill_safety`), forced reconnect, deduped CRITICAL alert `xstock-equity-feed-stall`, a loud config-missing alert (no silent default), and closure-aware timing. ⚠️ It forces the reconnect with `ws.close()`, which on a half-open socket waits for a close handshake the far side will not answer. The crypto version must `terminate()`. Whether the xStock one has the same weakness is noted for CC-C, not changed here.
- **The feed-health grade cannot see a half-dead feed.** `feed-health-aggregate.ts:36-50` grades a class by its FRESHEST symbol, `:135-150`. A proportion helper already exists (`:51-61`) and is unused by the alarm. The crypto leg reads the trading connection; the crypto recorder is not graded at all (`feed-integrity-monitor.ts:15` imports only the equity recorder).
- **The witness age is lost at persistence.** `getTickerWitness` (`depth-source.ts:201+`) returns `capturedAtMs`; `closed_trades` stores only `exit_ticker_bid`/`exit_ticker_ask` (`shared/schema.ts:1874-1875`).
- **Measured on staging (`error__2026-10-22_00-00-00.log` and later daily logs, read 2026-11-04):** Kraken cuts every recorder connection routinely with code 1006 — shard0 9 and shard1 9 on 10-21. On the 11 later daily logs counted (10-22 to 11-04, excluding 10-30 and 10-31), shard1 was cut 5-13 times a day, with 0 on two. The xStock and futures recorders were cut the same way. **A silent reconnect is therefore a recurring risk, not a one-off.**
- **Recovery confirmed after the deploy:** health line 12:15:24Z shard0 `rows_persisted_60s=274`; 435 distinct coins in `crypto_spot_ticker_snap` for the 12:00Z hour against 181-200 per hour 09:00-11:00Z.

## 3. Objectives (each with how it is verified)
1. **A silence check on every recorder connection.** Each crypto recorder shard is checked on a fixed tick; a connection whose last DATA frame is older than its threshold is `terminate()`d and reconnects through the existing backoff path (one reconnect path, not two). The moment it fires, it logs the socket's state, the age and the last frame types, so the next occurrence can be diagnosed. The Kraken futures recorder (`kraken-futures-archiver.ts`, also cut with 1006 on 10-21 and with no silence check) gets the same check. *Verify:* fake-timer tests — a silent OPEN socket is terminated and reconnected once; a socket receiving data is left alone; a socket already reconnecting is not double-handled; each mutation-checked red.
2. **A subscription check.** After each (re)connect, the recorder counts Kraken's subscribe replies; a rejected or unconfirmed subscription within its timeout is logged with the symbols named and triggers a reconnect. *Verify:* tests for accepted, rejected and missing replies.
3. **The threshold is DB-governed and measured, never invented.** Per-feed silence thresholds live in `module_constants` (`passive_archive`), seeded from the measured inter-arrival of DATA frames per shard (Step 2 measures it on staging), with a loud config-missing alert and no silent default (rule 15). *Verify:* the seed cites its measurement; with the row absent the check is inert and the config-missing alert fires (test).
4. **"Connected" means data arriving.** The health line, `getCryptoSpotStats` and the Passive Archive panel report a connection as live only when data arrived within its threshold; `readyState` is shown beside it, not instead of it. *Verify:* the panel and the health line on staging show both fields; a test proves an OPEN-but-silent shard reads not-live.
5. **One alert, not a storm.** One deduped alert when a connection stays silent through repeated forced reconnects or reconnects more often than its hourly limit (both DB-governed); it clears itself on recovery. *Verify:* tests for raise, dedupe and clear; on staging, no alert across ordinary 1006 cuts.
6. **The feed-health grade counts live coins.** The alarm grades a feed by the share of its symbols fresh, not by its freshest symbol, and the crypto recorder becomes a graded feed. *Verify:* a test where half the symbols go stale grades the feed down (red on the old freshest-symbol rule); Step 2 censuses every consumer of the grade before the change.
7. **The backup price carries its age.** Each closed crypto trade stores the age of its second-opinion price beside `exit_ticker_bid/ask`, read from `getTickerWitness().capturedAtMs`; the witness stays fail-open and never blocks a close. *Verify:* migration + rollback; a close writes the age; the close path is unchanged when the witness is absent.
8. **The 12 stale-witness closes are marked.** The trades Langston enumerated (BEAM, APR, SKY, RSR, LSK ×2, TRX, DASH, GRASS, DOS ×2, plus the 12th, re-enumerated at Step 2 from the dark-set symbols and the 2026-10-21 10:19:55Z → 2026-11-04 12:13:26Z window) are flagged so outcome analysis can exclude them, and the hole is noted on `#596`. *Verify:* the enumeration query and its result are in the pre-audit; the flag is present on exactly those rows.
9. **Step 7 (pre-registered here, the numbers fixed at Step 2):** on staging, over 48 h after the deploy — every recorder shard's data age stays under its threshold except across a reconnect; every 1006 cut is followed by data within the threshold; zero forced reconnects on a healthy connection; the grade reads healthy with both shards live. **FAIL** = any shard OPEN with data older than its threshold plus one check tick, or an alert on a healthy feed.
