## 0. What this batch is for
The crypto recorder (the passive archive, `crypto-spot-archiver.ts`) keeps a durable price history for every crypto pair over two Kraken connections. On 2026-10-21 10:19:55Z one connection (shard0, 196 of 408 coins) was cut by the venue, its 9th routine cut that day. It reconnected, took in its opening data, and then received nothing for 14 days while reporting `connected=true`. It recovered only when the 2026-11-04 12:13:26Z deploy restarted it. The recorder reconnects only after a close, sends no ping, never checks that a subscription was accepted, and prints its own silence every minute without acting on it. Nothing else watched it. **After this batch, a silent recorder connection is noticed and replaced within about a minute, "connected" means data is arriving, the feed-health grade sees a half-dead feed, and the 14-day hole is marked in the records it damaged.**

## 1. What is there (read at `origin/migration/aws-supabase` `8b71855f1`; the load-bearing lines re-read by CC-B)
- **Reconnect only on close.** `connectShard` (`crypto-spot-archiver.ts:215-239`): `ws.on('close')` → `scheduleReconnect` (`:241-247`); `ws.on('error')` only logs (`:236-238`). There is exactly ONE call site of `scheduleReconnect` (`:233`).
- **Silence is measured and discarded.** `lastMsgAt` is stamped on every frame (`handleMessage`, `:178`) and read only by the 60 s health logger (`:249-260`), which prints `last_msg_age_ms` and resets `rowsPersistedLastMinute`. `connected=` is `readyState === OPEN` (`:255`).
- **No subscription check.** `subscribe` (`:201-213`) sends two frames (ohlc 1 m, ticker) and logs "subscribed" without waiting for Kraken's reply.
- **Readers of its state:** `getCryptoSpotStats` (`:60`) is read by `drift-dashboard-aggregator.ts:895` (the Passive Archive panel). Census of every reader of `crypto_spot_ticker_snap` / `crypto_spot_ohlc_1m` is a Step-2 item (see §5).
- **The pattern already exists for xStock.** `equity-spot-archiver.ts:534-613` (`B-PRICE-STALENESS-BOUND` P2): a 30 s tick, DB-resolved two-tier threshold (`xstock_fill_safety`), forced reconnect, deduped CRITICAL alert `xstock-equity-feed-stall`, a loud config-missing alert (no silent default), and closure-aware timing. ⚠️ It forces the reconnect with `ws.close()`, which on a half-open socket waits for a close handshake the far side will not answer. The crypto version must `terminate()`. Whether the xStock one has the same weakness is noted for CC-C, not changed here.
- **The feed-health grade cannot see a half-dead feed.** `feed-health-aggregate.ts:36-50` grades a class by its FRESHEST symbol, `:135-150`. A proportion helper already exists (`:51-61`) and is unused by the alarm. The crypto leg reads the trading connection; the crypto recorder is not graded at all (`feed-integrity-monitor.ts:15` imports only the equity recorder).
- **The witness age is lost at persistence.** `getTickerWitness` (`depth-source.ts:201+`) returns `capturedAtMs`; `closed_trades` stores only `exit_ticker_bid`/`exit_ticker_ask` (`shared/schema.ts:1874-1875`).
- **★ r2 — THE RECORDER DOES REACH A CLOSE DECISION (Langston, re-read by CC-B at `active-execution-engine.ts:4425-4470`).** The engine reads the recorder only THROUGH `getTickerWitness` (no table literal in the engine, which is why a table-name census cannot find it). `:4425` reads the witness; on a NOT-warm book (the `else` of `_fillWarm`, `:4448`) `:4451` takes `_refBid` from it, and the close is refused as `walk_above_reference` when the walked fill sits above that bid by more than `up_tol` — the position is left open and retried until `_countCloseRefusal` yields (`:4462-4471`). That gate is mine (`B-FEED-MISMATCH-FIX`, `3n.u`). **The function is fail-open on ABSENCE (null ⇒ `walk_no_reference`, `:4453`); its consumer is fail-CLOSED on a stale PRESENCE — and a dead recorder produces a frozen presence, the worse input.** `getTickerWitness`'s query has no `captured_at` floor (`depth-source.ts:213-220`). **Sized: a fence, not a live defect** — a warm book never consults the witness, and Langston measured 14 closes on dark-shard symbols since 10-21, all on the warm path. `SYSTEM_MANUAL.md:708-709` is true of the function and false as a description of the system; its content update is required by this batch.
- **★ r2 — the backoff resets on OPEN, not on data** (`crypto-spot-archiver.ts:225`, in the `'open'` handler before `subscribe`), so a socket that opens and then goes silent resets the backoff every cycle, and forced reconnects would run at the 1 s floor forever.
- **★ r2 — there is no reconnect-in-flight state.** `scheduleReconnect` fires a bare `setTimeout` with no stored handle (`:244-246`), and `connectShard` calls `.close()` on the socket it replaces (`:217-220`) without removing that socket's `'close'` listener, so any path that replaces a live socket outside the single `'close'` edge schedules a second reconnect ⇒ two live sockets on one shard.
- **Measured on staging (`error__2026-10-22_00-00-00.log` and later daily logs, read 2026-11-04):** Kraken cuts every recorder connection routinely with code 1006 — shard0 9 and shard1 9 on 10-21. On the 11 later daily logs counted (10-22 to 11-04, excluding 10-30 and 10-31), shard1 was cut 5-13 times a day, with 0 on two. The xStock and futures recorders were cut the same way. **A silent reconnect is therefore a recurring risk, not a one-off.**
- **Recovery confirmed after the deploy:** health line 12:15:24Z shard0 `rows_persisted_60s=274`; 435 distinct coins in `crypto_spot_ticker_snap` for the 12:00Z hour against 181-200 per hour 09:00-11:00Z.

## 3. Objectives (each with how it is verified)
1. **A silence check on every recorder connection.** Each crypto recorder shard is checked on a fixed tick; a connection whose last DATA frame is older than its threshold is `terminate()`d and reconnects through the existing backoff path (one reconnect path, not two). **Required by r2, and fenced: exactly one live `shard.ws` and at most one pending reconnect timer per shard at all times** — the batch ADDS the in-flight state (a stored timer handle; the replaced socket's listeners removed before it is terminated), because none exists today. The moment it fires, it logs the socket's state, the age and the last frame types, so the next occurrence can be diagnosed. The Kraken futures recorder (`kraken-futures-archiver.ts`, also cut with 1006 on 10-21 and with no silence check) gets the same check. *Verify:* fake-timer tests — a silent OPEN socket is terminated and reconnected once; a socket receiving data is left alone; a socket already reconnecting is not double-handled; after any sequence of cut / silence / replace events the shard holds one socket and at most one timer; each mutation-checked red.
2. **A subscription check.** After each (re)connect, the recorder counts Kraken's subscribe replies; a rejected or unconfirmed subscription within its timeout is logged with the symbols named and triggers a reconnect. *Verify:* tests for accepted, rejected and missing replies.
3. **The threshold is DB-governed and measured, never invented — and it survives resharding.** Per-feed silence thresholds live in `module_constants` (`passive_archive`), with a loud config-missing alert and no silent default (rule 15). A shard's data cadence depends on how many symbols sit on it, and membership is computed at startup from the universe size (`assignToShard`), so the seed is NOT a shard-level gap measured today: it is either a per-symbol expected cadence combined with the shard's live size, or a generous absolute whose derivation is on the record against the smallest plausible shard. Step 2 measures both and chooses. *Verify:* the seed cites its measurement and its population; with the row absent the check is inert and the config-missing alert fires (test).
4. **"Connected" means data arriving.** The health line, `getCryptoSpotStats` and the Passive Archive panel report a connection as live only when data arrived within its threshold; `readyState` is shown beside it, not instead of it. *Verify:* the panel and the health line on staging show both fields; a test proves an OPEN-but-silent shard reads not-live.
5. **One alert, not a storm — and the hourly limit is the brake.** One deduped alert when a connection stays silent through repeated forced reconnects or reconnects more often than its hourly limit (both DB-governed); it clears itself on recovery. ⚠️ Until 5a lands, this limit is the ONLY thing stopping 1 s reconnect churn on an open-but-silent socket; it is load-bearing, not cosmetic. *Verify:* tests for raise, dedupe and clear; on staging, no alert across ordinary 1006 cuts.
5a. **The backoff resets on the first DATA frame, not on open** — for the two recorders this batch touches (crypto spot, futures), so a socket that opens and goes silent climbs the backoff instead of churning at 1 s. The xStock recorder has the same reset on open; it is CC-C's (row 34 / `#635`) and is told to them, not changed here. *Verify:* a test where open-then-silent cycles climb the delay (red with the reset back on open).
6. **The feed-health grade counts live coins.** `feed-health-aggregate.ts:9-19` documents FOUR consumers with deliberately different aggregates (a Langston/CC-A Step-2 consensus): ALARM = freshest (`feed-integrity-monitor`) · GO-LIVE GATE = proportion (`parity-gate.ts:5`, `assessWsReadiness`) · STATUS = freshest (`health-monitor.ts:527`, `system-health-monitor.ts:7`, importing `freshestSymbolAgeMs` directly). The freshest rule exists so one legitimately quiet illiquid pair cannot false-CRITICAL a feed — a real concern on crypto. **r2 design:** the ALARM keeps its freshest-symbol term ("is the feed alive at all") and GAINS a second term, the share of the feed's symbols live, against a floor measured at Step 2 so ordinary quiet pairs do not trip it ("is the feed whole"); the crypto recorder becomes a graded feed, per connection. GO-LIVE keeps its proportion; STATUS keeps freshest and SHOWS the live share beside it, so the dashboard and the alarm read the same numbers even where they grade differently — intended, and stated in the header. The header (the governed record of the mapping) and the SIM are rewritten. Step 2 completes the consumer census starting from those imports. *Verify:* a test where half the symbols go stale raises the alarm (red on freshest-only); a test where a handful of quiet pairs go stale does not; the measured floor and its population are in the pre-audit.
7. **The backup price carries its age.** Each closed crypto trade stores the age of its second-opinion price beside `exit_ticker_bid/ask`, read from `getTickerWitness().capturedAtMs`. The FUNCTION stays fail-open (a miss still returns null and the close proceeds); what a stale presence does at its consumer is objective 7a. *Verify:* migration + rollback; a close writes the age; the close path is unchanged when the witness is absent.
7a. **An age bound at the grading consumer** (§13 disposition 1, folded: this batch's own failure produced the stale presence it guards against). In the `walk_above_reference` gate (`active-execution-engine.ts:4448-4471`), a witness older than a DB-governed bound is treated exactly like a missing one — `walk_no_reference`, no grading, the close proceeds, and the arm and age are stamped. The bound lives on the consumer, never in `getTickerWitness`, which must stay fail-open. *Verify:* a test where a stale witness above the walk no longer refuses the close (red without the bound); a fresh witness still refuses as before.
8. **Closed trades whose witness predates its shard's silence are marked — titled by the predicate, the count measured at Step 2.** The published counts differ by predicate and window (Langston: 12 on 11-03; 15 of 29 on 11-02), so none is carried here. Predicate: a crypto close between 2026-10-21 10:19:55Z and 2026-11-04 12:13:26Z, on a symbol of the dark shard, whose stored witness is older than the shard's silence onset. Each flagged row carries the witness AGE, or the literal `underivable` where the snap row cannot be matched — never a bare boolean (that is the `#546` shape this batch exists to fix). The hole is noted on `#596`. *Verify:* the predicate, query and result are in the pre-audit; the flag is present on exactly those rows, with an age or `underivable` on each.
9. **Step 7 (pre-registered here, the numbers fixed at Step 2):** on staging, over 48 h after the deploy — every recorder shard's data age stays under its threshold except across a reconnect; every 1006 cut is followed by data within the threshold; zero forced reconnects on a healthy connection; the grade reads healthy with both shards live. **FAIL** = any shard OPEN with data older than its threshold plus one check tick, or an alert on a healthy feed.
