## 1. Audit

### A1 — why the pages land at the open (mechanism re-read)
`vts-runner.ts:4028-4030`: `_ntXsOffHours = xstock && (isInXstockWeekendClose(now) || getXstockSession(now) !== 'regular')`; `_ntPages` is true only outside off-hours. `getXstockSession` (`asset_classes/xstock_spot/time-of-day.ts:82-94`) returns `regular` from 09:30 to 16:00 New York time. `stepNoTriggerStreak` keeps a streak in every session but measures PAGING time only while `_ntPages` is true, with `pageSinceMs` restarting after any off-hours gap; at `VTS_NO_TRIGGER_ALERT_AFTER_MS` = 10 min (`vts-runner.ts:225`) it pages. ⇒ a VTS xStock trade with no decision at 09:30 crosses the page line at **09:40 ET**.
**The venue's own regular-session flag flips at 09:45 ET**: measured first `is_extended_hours = false` rows 13:45Z on 10-29 and 10-30 (EDT) and 14:45Z on 11-02 and 11-03 (EST), §3d. The system already knows this: `entry-spread-plausibility.ts:25` says the feed's flag "flips at 13:45Z, 15 min after the open" and is not the engine's session. So for 15 minutes our clock calls it regular while the venue does not, and books in that window are not yet the regular book. The pages then clear 15-20 min after the open, once the books turn usable.
**Census (§9.5(a)) at the page decision:** one writer of `_vtsNoTriggerStreak` (`vts-runner.ts`, the real-lane resolve loop); the shadow lane does not page. `_ntPages` has TWO consumers per trade, not one: the crossing page (`stepNoTriggerStreak`) and the member doorbell `onMemberReason` (`vts-runner.ts:4042`), which pages at once when a standing member's refusal turns non-expected. **⚠️ r1 missed a second SITE of the same predicate (Langston C-4):** `vts-rail-reporting.ts:441` defines `isRegularSession` as `!isInXstockWeekendClose(...) && getXstockSession(...) === 'regular'`, which drives the standing record's 30-minute member clock (`stepMemberClock`, `core/trading/vts-rail.ts:67-72`; escalation `vts-rail-reporting.ts:286-297`). Off-session that clock is cleared and it restarts at the first regular pass, so for a member carried over the open it starts at 09:30. Measured by Langston: 10 of the 374 xStock window rows are that family (body "minutes of US regular-session time"), all minted at minute 30-31 after the open. The session function `getXstockSession` has other readers (the VTS instrument's per-session buckets, the paper xStock paths); **this increment does not change `getXstockSession`**.

- **Deployment check (not a success criterion):** zero crossing pages minted in [0,25) and zero doorbell or member-clock pages minted in [0,15). This band is empty by construction on any build containing P1, so it only proves the build is live.
- **PASS:** crossing pages minted in [25,45) total **≤ 10 over the five sessions** and **≤ 5 in any one session**; and member-clock pages land at minute 45 or later (none in [30,45)).
- **FAIL (merely delayed):** crossing pages minted in [25,45) **≥ 15 in any one session**, or **≥ 40 over the five** — roughly half the delay-only prediction; or any non-expected reason that paged in the baseline window stops paging.
- **Between PASS and FAIL:** inconclusive; the per-session table goes to Langston to rule.

Under P1 they cannot fire before minute 15; whether the member's reason is still non-expected at 09:45 is not measured, so **this family has no PASS bar, only a FAIL: ≥ 10 per session on average** (no better than the baseline, only later).

## 4. Risk
A genuinely broken xStock feed at the open pages up to 15 minutes later than today (25 minutes after the open instead of 10). **The settle also blacks out the doorbell fast path for the first 15 minutes** (`onMemberReason`, `vts-runner.ts:4042` — 123 of the baseline's 350 opening pages, minutes 11-13), and **delays the 30-minute member clock by 15 minutes for members carried over the open** (P1's shared predicate). Off-hours behaviour, crypto paging and every exit decision are unchanged; only alert timing moves.

## 5. Plain-language summary
Most VTS xStock alerts fire ten minutes after the US open, because our clock calls the market open at 9:30 while the venue only switches to its regular book at 9:45. They then clear by themselves. The plan waits 15 minutes after the open before that clock starts. It also adds a count of why waiting trades were held back, and gives the one exit type that doesn't say which price it used the same label as the rest.
