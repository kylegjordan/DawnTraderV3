- **PREVIOUSLY STATED (scope r2, objective 6): the ALARM gains a live-share term across the feed. NOW: the live-share term grades the CRYPTO RECORDER only (a new graded source, per connection); the crypto trading feed and the xStock leg keep their freshest-symbol grade unchanged. REASON:** A5 — on a healthy day only 73-96 % of crypto symbols tick in a trailing hour (ticker frames arrive only on change), so a share term on a small, event-driven set is a false-CRITICAL generator; the recorder's universe is where the measured separation exists.
- **PREVIOUSLY STATED (scope r2, objective 7a): a witness older than a DB-governed bound is treated as absent. NOW: a witness is used for grading only if it is VOUCHED — its symbol is in the recorder's universe, its connection is live, AND its age is under a ceiling; otherwise the new arm `walk_witness_unvouched`. REASON:** A6 — a quiet coin's witness is legitimately hours old (healthy per-symbol max gap up to 2.97 h), so an age bound alone would either be too tight (strips grading from healthy quiet coins) or too loose (would not catch a frozen shard for hours). Liveness is the discriminating signal; the ceiling is the backstop.

## 3. PLAN (each item cites its finding)
- **P1 (A4, objectives 1, 5a) — one socket, one timer, per connection.** In both recorders (crypto spot per shard; the futures class per instance): a stored reconnect timer handle and a `reconnectPending` flag; replacing a socket removes its listeners first and `terminate()`s it; `scheduleReconnect` is a no-op while one is pending; the backoff resets on the first DATA frame after open, not on open. Fence: after any sequence of cut / silence / replace, exactly one live socket and ≤ 1 timer.
- **P2 (A1, A4, objective 1) — the silence check.** A 30 s tick per connection: no DATA frame (ticker or ohlc for spot; ticker for futures) for longer than its threshold ⇒ log the socket state (`readyState`, ages of the last data and last any-frame, last frame types, bytes received) and terminate + reconnect through P1. A connection that is `reconnectPending` is not checked.
- **P3 (A1, A4, objective 3) — the thresholds, DB-governed.** `module_constants` `passive_archive`: `crypto_spot_silence_ms` = **120,000** and `futures_silence_ms` = **120,000**, seeded with this derivation: the healthy cross-shard max gap is 19.2 s for a 196-symbol shard; the smallest plausible spot shard is ~150 symbols (`SHARD_SIZE` 300 ⇒ two shards only above 300 symbols), so its expected max is ~25 s; 120 s is ≥ 4.8× that. The 10-symbol xStock-perp instance's healthy daily max is ≤ 40 s ⇒ 3×. Missing row ⇒ the check is inert and a `passive-archive-config-missing` alert fires (rule 15, the equity pattern).
- **P4 (objective 2) — subscribe replies.** Count Kraken's `subscribe` method replies per channel; a `success:false` names its symbols in the log; no reply within 15 s ⇒ reconnect via P1. UNAUDITED: the exact v2 reply shape on this endpoint is read off the wire at Step 3 before the parser is written, and the test fixtures are taken from that capture.
- **P5 (A2, A9, objective 5) — one alert per recorder, self-clearing.** Dedupe key `passive-archive-silent:<recorder>:<connection>`: raised when a connection stays silent through 3 consecutive forced reconnects, or exceeds 12 forced reconnects in an hour (both DB-governed, seeded with the 1006 measurement: 5-13 natural cuts a day, so 12 FORCED in an hour is far outside normal); resolved in-app on 10 minutes of data. When every recorder is silent at once the body says so (A2). A new machine actor `passive-archive-watchdog` in `ALERT_ACTORS`.
- **P6 (A5, objective 6) — the per-symbol clock and the live-share term.** The crypto recorder gains the xStock recorder's shape: a per-symbol `lastTickerUpdateAt` map stamped on `update` frames only (never `snapshot`), a first-update warmth clock, and accessors `getCryptoRecorderSymbolAges()` / `isCryptoRecorderWarm()`. `feed-health-aggregate.ts` stays pure (no I/O): a new pure `liveShare(items, windowMs)` beside `proportionFresh`. `feed-integrity-monitor` grades the recorder as its own source per connection: live share over a **2 h** window against a floor of **0.60** (DB-governed `feed_health` rows for the new source; derivation A5: healthy ≥ 0.80, half-dead ≤ 0.44), warmth-gated. The trading adapter's ALARM, GO-LIVE and STATUS aggregates are unchanged (A10); STATUS shows the recorder's live share beside them. The header (`:1-22`, the governed mapping) and the SIM are rewritten to list the fifth consumer.
- **P7 (A3, A6, objective 7a) — the vouched witness.** At the grading consumer only (`active-execution-engine.ts:4448-4471`), never in `getTickerWitness`: a witness is used if (a) its symbol is in the crypto recorder's universe, (b) that symbol's connection is live per P2, and (c) its age ≤ `close_fill_contract.witness_max_age_ms` (seed **21,600,000** = 6 h, 2× the healthy per-symbol max of 2.97 h). Otherwise the new arm **`walk_witness_unvouched`** (distinct from `walk_no_reference` and from `walk_stale`, Langston record item 2): no grading, the close proceeds, the arm and the witness age are stamped. xStock is untouched (its witness is a consistency record off the same table, A3).
- **P8 (A7, A8, objectives 7, 8) — the age on the record, and the marking.** Two nullable columns on `closed_trades`: `exit_ticker_captured_at` (timestamptz) and `exit_ticker_age_basis` (`at_close` | `backfill_matched` | `underivable`). Written at close from `capturedAtMs`. A one-off backfill script (read-only on the snap table, writes only these two columns, idempotent, dry-run first) over the 255 crypto witness closes since 2026-08-27, matching by symbol + equal bid/ask + `captured_at ≤ closed_at`, run before 2026-11-20; unmatched ⇒ `underivable`. "Flagged" = age > the P7 ceiling or `underivable`; the 14 in A7 are the expected flagged set inside the dark window. Migration + rollback + MANIFEST. The hole and the flag are written onto `#596`.
- **P9 (all) — tests, each mutation-checked red.** Fake-timer archiver tests (P1-P5), a pure test for `liveShare`, monitor tests for the recorder source (half-dead raises, a handful of quiet pairs does not), engine gate tests (vouched refuses as before; unvouched does not refuse and stamps the arm; out-of-universe GLMR shape), the backfill matcher on fixtures.
- **P10 (A3, sources 3-4) — governance content.** `SYSTEM_MANUAL` `:708-709` rewritten (function fail-open on absence; consumer fail-closed on presence; now vouched by P7) + the recorder's liveness; `SYSTEM_IMPACT_MAP` B74 inventory rows 3 and 6 (watchdog, reconnect policy, reset-on-data), the feed-health consumer list, the new alert and actor.

## 4. Step 7 (numbers fixed here)
On staging for 48 h after the deploy: (1) every recorder connection's data age < 120 s plus one 30 s tick, except across a logged reconnect; (2) every natural 1006 cut is followed by data within 120 s; (3) zero forced reconnects on a connection that was receiving data; (4) the recorder's live share ≥ 0.60 once warm, both shards; (5) no `passive-archive-silent` alert unless a pause like A2 occurs, and if one does, it resolves itself; (6) a close on the not-warm path with an unvouched witness, if one occurs, stamps `walk_witness_unvouched` and is not refused. **FAIL** = any connection OPEN with data older than 150 s and no forced reconnect logged, any double socket on a shard, a false alert on a healthy feed, or an unvouched witness refusing a close. Plus the backfill result on the record: the 14 of A7 flagged, the rest of the 255 with an age or `underivable`.

## 5. Plain summary
The audit confirmed the scope and narrowed two things. The recorder reaches exactly one decision, the not-warm close check, so that is where a stale backup price is now kept out — judged by whether the recorder is actually alive, not by age alone, because quiet coins are legitimately hours old. The "share of live coins" alarm term goes on the recorder only, because the trading feed's small set of coins is too quiet for it. The measurements set the numbers: two minutes of silence before replacing a connection (healthy max is under 20 seconds), a two-hour window and 60 % floor for the live share (healthy is over 80 %, half-dead under 45 %), and fourteen affected trades rather than twelve.
