## 1. What happens today (measured 2026-10-06/07)
1. **The rail and its alert:** `_recordPriceSkip` (`active-execution-engine.ts:~810-850`) counts consecutive untrusted ticks per position in an in-memory `Map` (`_priceSkipStreak`, `:755`); at `exit_integrity / max_consecutive_price_skips` it raises a `breakage`/`warning` system alert, dedupe `price-skip-<mode>-<symbol>`, titled *"Exit checks skipped — mark older than ceiling"* for the staleness branch.
2. **It fires on the market closing.** 2026-10-06: 15 of these in the first hour after the 16:00 ET close, with 19 xStock positions held after the 15:53Z reset; per-symbol ticker updates fell 3-4× on mid-size names (ALB ~660 → ~190/h; CAG ~800 → ~200/h) while large names barely thinned (NVDA ~890 → ~775/h). Before that day: 2 since 09-22. All-time (Infra, 09-03): **177 of 178 fired outside US regular hours**.
3. **It never clears — `#638`.** The streak resets on the first venue price (`:2422`, `_priceSkipStreak.delete`) and resolves nothing, so the row stays active until a human resolves it, and an unresolved row blocks the same key's next mint.
4. **There is no machinery to clear it (Langston r1, measured at `d455aa154`):** `resolveAlert` has **zero non-test callers under `server/`** — no production path has ever resolved an alert; it takes an **id** (`system-alerts.ts:583`), not a dedupe key; `assertAlertActor` (`:591`) is total and `ALERT_ACTORS` (`:205-220`) holds no trading-engine actor; `ResolveTransport` (`:143`) is the closed set `cli | dispatcher | api | governance-checker`; `resolution_evidence` must be a reference token (`:600`).
5. **Strict equality (FINDING-1):** the check is `if (streak === threshold)` (`:822`) — once per streak, the counter only climbs. If the resolved threshold moves mid-streak to a value already passed, that streak never escalates. Unreachable today (both rows 40 since 2026-07-15 = the hard-coded value).
6. **The hard-coded fallback and its real reach (FINDING-2):** `let threshold = 40; // fail-safe default if the knob is cold` with a `catch` that swallows the read failure. `module_constants` holds exactly **2** `max_consecutive_price_skips` rows (`crypto_spot`, `xstock_spot`), while `asValidAssetClass` admits `crypto_perp`/`xstock_perp` (`shared/asset-classes.ts:172`, registry `:37`/`:39`) — so a perp position would take the 40 off a row **never seeded**. **Reachability measured zero** (Langston): `active_open_positions` 19 `xstock_spot` + 1 `crypto_spot`; `closed_trades` 978 rows, those two classes only. A fence, not a live defect — and the `catch` is swallowing a class-coverage gap that would otherwise announce itself.
7. **The longest staleness is the least reported (r1a §6.1):** one unbroken streak alerts once; `HUT/USD` ran 2 h 46 m stale on 2026-10-06 with zero rows after its two early mints were resolved.
8. **Provenance (TIER 1):** the rail is `P19-B8.5` (venue-only pricing, Langston condition 1: *"skipping a tick when the venue quotes nothing is correct… must not fail silently"*); the staleness-vs-absence copy split is 2026-07-22 (Analyst + Langston); the per-symbol ceiling is `P19-B8.5e` (`#548`); the actor gate is `B-ALERT-ACTOR-ALLOWLIST` (`#987`, deliberately total). **Disposition (2): relevant, needs updating to today's intent** — the rail stays; its DELIVERY changes (`#994`), and it gains a clear path (`#638`).

## 2. Objectives
1. **Keep the emit, cut the page for a quiet market** (`#994`; Langston's binding constraint — *"what changes is DELIVERY, not emission"*). The per-position skip record and its log line stay. When a position's staleness is the MARKET (its cohort is quiet too), it does **not** raise a `breakage` alert; it is counted into ONE standing per-class *"xStock venue quiet"* record per quiet window, carrying **each skipping position's current staleness** (not only a count), the oldest mark age, and the skip rate beside its budget. It RESOLVES through objective 9 when the venue resumes.
2. **Still page when it is US** — a symbol stale while its cohort keeps ticking (a lost subscription, a stuck book). **The discriminator is the COHORT comparison designed in 3b.f-d**, plus row 3a's feed-level grade for the whole-feed case. **When the cohort is too thin to discriminate, the default is to PAGE** (Langston r1 Q2: never trade a self-announcing failure for a silent one). **Condition (pre-registered, not a knob):** Step 2 measures the off-hours cohort-size distribution against the discrimination rule BEFORE choosing the thinness line, and states **what share of the 2026-10-06 close's 15 rows the chosen rule would still have paged**. That share is the batch's prediction, checked at Step 7.
3. **Escalate on DURATION, not on one crossing** (3b.f-d option 3 + r1a §6.1): a position that stays unpriceable escalates by how long it has been stale, so a streak whose early row was resolved still surfaces; and a position still stale into liquid hours escalates as the genuinely alarming case — the session boundary read from the session table, not the `is_extended_hours` flag.
4. **Clear what it raises** (`#638`): a `price-skip-*` row and the standing record resolve when the condition clears — lifecycle `resolve`, **never `ack`** (`#982`). Implemented by objectives 7-9; this objective is the behaviour, they are the machinery.
5. **Remove the hard-coded threshold fallback, and make the escalation robust to the threshold (FINDINGS 1 + 2).** (a) A cold or unseeded knob raises its own config alert (dedupe per class) and **escalates the position at once** — the cold-path value is "escalate now", never a number the streak may already have passed, and never a `return`/throw that leaves the streak counting silently. (b) The check becomes `streak >= threshold` with a once-per-streak escalated flag (same key and lifecycle as the streak), so a threshold that moves mid-streak cannot lose the escalation. (c) The class-coverage gap (`crypto_perp`/`xstock_perp` unseeded) is surfaced by (a) instead of swallowed; no perp row is seeded here (no perp position exists — Langston's measurement).
6. **The entry leg is decided explicitly, not swept in** (3b.f-d): `xstock-stale-fill-block` (one global key, an ENTRY refused, already self-batching) stays out unless Step 2 shows its trigger and resolve condition match.
7. **NEW — resolve by dedupe key (BLOCKER part 1).** A named function in `system-alerts.ts`, `resolveAlertsByDedupeKey(key, by, evidence, transport)`: reads the file, selects every **non-terminal** row with that key, resolves each through `resolveAlert` (so every existing gate applies), returns the ids resolved. **More than one match** is impossible under the non-resolved dedupe on `addAlert` (`:508`); if it is ever seen, it resolves all of them and logs `[RESOLVE_BY_KEY][MULTI_MATCH] key=… n=…`. Zero matches is a no-op, not an error.
8. **NEW — a canonical engine actor and transport (BLOCKER part 2; a change to the `#987` gate, made as its own objective).** `ALERT_ACTORS` gains `{ value: 'active-exit-monitor', tag: 'machine', why: 'server/services/active-execution-engine.ts — resolves its own price-skip and venue-quiet rows when the re-measured condition clears (B-VENUE-QUIET-ALERTING, #638)' }`; `ResolveTransport` gains `'engine'`. The gate stays total. **Evidence** is the position's uuid (a reference token under `:600`); for a position that has since CLOSED, the closed trade's id.
9. **NEW — resolve off a RE-MEASURED condition, not a remembered streak (BLOCKER parts 3-4).** The streak `Map` does not survive a restart (the `#520`/`#585` class), so the resolve is NOT driven by the streak reset at `:2422`. Instead a sweep — at engine start and then every monitor cycle, off the hot exit loop (scheduled after the cycle, awaited by nothing on the exit path) — lists the non-terminal `price-skip-<mode>-*` and venue-quiet rows, **re-measures** each symbol now (its mark age against its own ceiling; for the standing record, the cohort ticking again), and resolves those whose condition has cleared or whose position has closed. **Failure is visible and never kills the exit cycle:** a resolve that throws is caught, logged `[VENUE_QUIET][RESOLVE_FAILED] key=… err=…`, counted on the cycle summary line, and retried on the next sweep because the row is still non-terminal. A row whose resolve fails on every sweep for an hour raises one `breakage` alert of its own (dedupe `price-skip-resolve-stuck-<mode>`).

*Verify (all):* unit tests for each branch:
- quiet market ⇒ no breakage row, the standing record updates and resolves;
- one stale symbol among a ticking cohort ⇒ breakage row;
- thin cohort ⇒ breakage row;
- condition clears ⇒ the row resolves with the position uuid as evidence;
- **restart between mint and resume** ⇒ a fresh engine's first sweep resolves the row;
- resolve throws ⇒ the exit cycle completes, the failure is logged and counted, and the next sweep retries;
- cold or unseeded knob ⇒ config alert plus immediate escalation;
- **the threshold moves mid-streak to a value already passed ⇒ the streak still escalates** (FINDING-1);
- an unknown actor is still refused (the `#987` gate stays total);
- multi-match ⇒ all resolved and logged.
