The next dispatcher run delivered all 15 first notices. Nine members had both book-state and quiet-market reasons, five had quiet-market reasons only, and one had a book-state reason only. The join, controls and exact IDs are in [the review change list](../Change%20Lists/B_PAPER_EXIT_EPISODE_NOTIFY_CHANGE_LIST.md). The count measures shared escalation and Discord delivery. Its closeout explicitly registered holiday paging as a residual (§3 and the Step 11 disposition). The November sweep is the intended duration escalation. The open scope question is how many Discord notices one positively identified paper exit episode needs.

## 2. Proposed boundary and verification

1. **One Discord warning per confirmed paper xStock duration episode.** Qualify only warnings produced by the existing standing-member duration sweep on a measured `thin` verdict. Have that producer carry the episode identity and standing record/position references explicitly. Reuse the alert store and dispatcher for one durable warning with the affected position count and a stable link or ID to the full evidence. Keep the existing per-position warning IDs, severity, reason families, timestamps, supersession, resolution and System Alerts visibility. Subsequent verified members join the same warning evidence. The existing duration clock may reset without defining a second episode. Step 2 must pin an observable end/re-arm rule and show it holds across a process restart. **Proof:** replay all 15 observed member shapes and timings with the same episode identity. Require one Discord first notice and 15 independently inspectable position records. Exercise a later member, a restart and a genuinely new episode to expose accidental duplicates or suppression.
2. **Keep independent warnings on their existing immediate path.** The producer's duration classification and explicit member linkage govern eligibility. A plain symbol key, similar title, shared timestamp, or co-occurring book refusal is insufficient. Keep immediate exit warnings, individually unpriced positions after recovery, independent book-state/hollow warnings, crypto, live mode, unrelated warnings, and VTS first and repeat notifications on their existing paths. Legacy rows and incomplete or invalid links use existing individual delivery. **Proof:** inject those controls in the very same tick as the valid group, including an independently triggered book-state warning on a group member's symbol and an orphaned or mismatched link. Each must reach the normal sink without waiting for group delivery.
3. **Keep the closure guarantee and make delivery auditable.** Only a durable, valid episode warning with verifiable member coverage can represent those alerts in Discord. Measure first delivery and repeat delivery separately. An episode needs a genuine delivery receipt and the existing reminder/back-off and escalation behavior while covered positions remain unresolved. A promotion timestamp is insufficient proof of delivery. Require a clearly recorded retry or the existing individual path on failed or uncertain coverage. Step 2 must show how partial publication, a failed post, a restart, and a member resolving between dispatch and receipt preserve evidence and a live notification path before implementation is proposed. **Proof:** simulate failed send, unclaimed unresolved state, mixed coverage and stale/closed members. Verify independent notices still go immediately, a failed send advances no delivery state, and real coverage permits one reminder per episode when due. If the existing store and delivery paths cannot support that safely within this seam, return to Coltrane and PM with the specific gap.
