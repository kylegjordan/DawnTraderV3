## 4. Objectives (each with its verification)
- **OBJ-1 — a "money held" balance, one function, defined from ONE anchor event (Langston C-1, C-4).** **Money held = the anchor value + the entry cost of every paper position that was OPEN at the anchor event + realized P&L closed since that event.** That is the account's capital at cost: cash, plus what is committed to open positions, at their entry price.
  - **Which event.** `portfolio_state.anchor_version` → the `portfolio_anchor_events` row with the same `(mode, anchor_version)` (unique index, `shared/schema.ts:1213`). `executeReanchor` writes both in one transaction with one version (`portfolio-anchor-service.ts:148-181`), and it is the only writer of `portfolio_state` (Langston, repo-wide: one update, one insert). So value and time are one event, whatever its reason; `getLastAnchorAt(OPERATOR_REBASE_REASONS)` is not reused, because the kill switch's window answers a different question (`#1171`).
  - **What an anchor value IS — corrected (C-4).** r2 called an `auto_divergence` re-anchor "a measurement of money held". **That was false.** The mirror figure is the real Kraken account's free USD and stablecoin cash (`kraken-mirror-balance.ts:101-104`, `:121`), which cannot see a paper position, and `reanchorToLive` writes exactly that number (`portfolio-anchor-service.ts:203`, `:208`). The common meaning across all four reasons is **cash not committed to paper positions at the event**: `launch_snap` fires at genesis with nothing open; `start_new` stops the engine and then anchors to the mirror figure (`routes.ts:11141-11174`); the operator `measurement_override` of 10-06 refused unless every paper position was flat (`server/scripts/paper-reset-3000.ts:33-37`); `auto_divergence` fires AT an open (`active-execution-engine.ts:6612` → `evaluateDivergenceAtOpen`), so positions are open by construction.
  - **The straddle term.** Positions open at the event hold cash that the anchor value does not contain. Their realized P&L is counted on close, but their committed cash never was. Measured at v4 (2026-08-12 04:19:20.541Z, 2250.00 → 824.11): 5 positions straddled it, **$1,615.42 of entry notional and +$187.32 of P&L**; OOB/USD opened 218 ms before the write and was the triggering open. r2's formula therefore read about $1,615 low from 08-12 to 08-19. **Rejected alternatives:** refusing or flagging on a straddle, because it is structural for `auto_divergence` and would refuse at every such event; asserting a straddle-free invariant, because nothing enforces one for that reason. **Entry cost** = Σ quantity × entry price, the same basis as the exposure numerator (`trade-safety.ts:631-636`), so the exposure ratio compares like with like. At v5 the straddle set is empty (the operator flattened first), so today's figure is unchanged: $820.00 − $210.30 = $609.70.
  - **Labelled as not re-read (Step 2 confirms each at the code):** that `start_new`'s engine stop flattens every paper position before the anchor; that the two historical override scripts (`b8-5-measurement-override.ts`, `b8-5-tune3-anchor.ts`) ran flat (each event's straddle set is measured at Step 2 and the outcome recorded); and the entry-fee treatment between the entry cost and `pnl`.
  - **Fail-closed on a broken pair:** no ledger row for the state row's version, or a ledger `new_balance` that differs from `portfolio_state.balance`, ⇒ the function does not guess; Step 2 sets what the sizing callers do then (this function does not feed the kill switch, so a refusal there is not the catastrophe the null-session comment describes).
  - *Verify:* unit tests over a restart (realized before it still counts); an operator re-anchor (realized before it does not); an `auto_divergence` re-anchor after an operator one (the v3/v4 shape: realized before the auto event does not count); **a straddle (a position open at the anchor event, closed after: its entry cost and its P&L both count, exactly once; still open: its entry cost counts)**; the broken-pair refusal; a fence that both functions use `getRealizedPnlSince`.
