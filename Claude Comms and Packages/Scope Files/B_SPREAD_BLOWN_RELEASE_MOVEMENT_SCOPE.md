# B-SPREAD-BLOWN-RELEASE-MOVEMENT — scope

change-class: architecture

Owner: CC-C · RUNNING_ISSUES #1199 · pre-sprint stage 2, item 3

## Directive and boundary

Determine whether xStock paper exits should require evidence of quote movement when they resume through a fresh seed after a `spread_blown` hold. The candidate can affect only a confirmed fresh seed caused by clearing the comparator during that hold. The completed Bid Trigger Reland objective 6 observation satisfies this batch's input dependency. Kyle's governing exit requirement was “No forced exit and no time limit” when a sale would be unavailable in live mode (overnight-exit design ask r1, Kyle's answer 1, 2026-10-09). Objective 6 subsequently went on under his 2026-10-16 “turn it on and then review and observe” directive. Issue #1199 records an open scope question about the existing fresh-seed behavior. This scope authorizes an evidence comparison and decision. A changed rule would need an approved plan and release gates.

## Original intent and current path

Read at `fe3fcc586fd8b5d6e08cf449829f5cb54f78e17d`: System Manual §3.5.1, System Impact Map §§S25–S25c and book-state/exit-trigger sections, BATCH_CATALOG Bid Trigger Reland, its progress report §§3c, 3d, 3h, objective 6 change list, the overnight-exit design ask, RUNNING_ISSUES #1199, and the introducing commits found with Git pickaxe. The bounded comparison concerns mechanisms introduced in 2026-09 and 2026-10.

- **Absolute hold and its release in the TEC — disposition (1), still relevant and correct as shipped.** The original arm was introduced off by default in `9c82d1e10e41f8b87eb0873ba9b7b7a0f7845608` as “hollow:spread_blown, taken only when cfg.spreadBlownEnabled.” Objective 6 activation `75f6fcc22ac157c495ce7f9e917dbf32de791d53` says “re-seed can no longer release a wide book” and tests “the wide book, re-seed on the first tight frame”. `f7413a96afdd07441115876749f9391e9b8f8075` records “the release after a spread_blown hold is a fresh seed with no movement test”. The revised objective 6 reached staging in `5cab7b820f18606d002f4cf12cd36af131ba32bb`. Current `book-state.ts` checks the absolute spread before selecting a comparator-relative verdict. In `active-execution-engine.ts`, when the hold has cleared the comparator, the first qualifying frame can seed it, the second can validate it even if identical, and the first possible decision comes on the following evaluation because TEC reads validation before advancing. Ordinary cold starts are another source of fresh seeds, so they need their own controls. A retained ring can still refuse an implausible seed.
- **Ring-independent escape — disposition (1), still relevant and correct for its separate population.** The introducing implementation `546cda86e4e66de62aa648efb033459e40450b19` says “median and the frame are under an ABSOLUTE ceiling and both sides moved >= 2 moves in”. In `book-state-tracker.ts` that route operates on an existing seed judged implausible. It checks a full trailing window, the current spread, at least two changed quote pairs, and movement on both sides. The movement conditions protect against a frozen pair and against a stub ask with a moving bid. This supplies a comparison candidate for #1199. Its existing release rule and its callers are protected.
- **Surviving comparator — disposition (1), baseline behavior.** Before the hollow-skip cap, a wide tick increases the per-position streak and keeps the shared comparator. A previously validated comparator can admit the first recovered quote. On a cap-reaching yield the engine clears the comparator if it is free of the implausible-seed condition; the wide tick itself still refuses. Clearing a validated comparator early would create a delay outside the candidate's intended population. Entry behavior, the existing escape routes, unaffected short holds and restart-only fresh seeds are baseline controls.
- **Upstream and downstream read-only touchpoints.** The equity archiver supplies raw quote sides and the receipt clock. The retained ring can judge a fresh seed; its persisted market-data state and sole writer are documented under S25–S25c. The TEC uses its admitted frame and the existing exit selector to reach stop/target and fill checks. The refusal tally and close evidence screen potential episodes for investigation. The selector also serves VTS, which has distinct side and close rules. The batch targets the TEC paper exit only when the wide hold caused the fresh seed.

## Objectives and verification

1. **Reproduce the current sequence with coverage recorded.** Start from the §3d screening counts: 81 of 297 paper xStock closes had a recorded stop-side `spread_blown` marker, and 31 of those 81 had a reported below-stop release value. `exit-refusal-tally.ts` retains the first wide-hold marker and updates release values across later refusal episodes, so the two fields can describe different episodes. Keep the first overnight read's 53 above-stop release log events across 17 symbols as separate context. Separately sample still-open or censored holds where records survive. Before scoring, match the actual wide hold, its comparator history and clearing cause, the seed, validation, first eligible decision and release, position, mode, stop or target, quote clocks and source, and the serving build. Join a close when one occurred. Count unmatched or incomplete episodes in the coverage denominator. Distinguish TEC evaluations from venue updates and report the complete, incomplete and unavailable populations.
2. **Compare safety and delayed exits on the same admissible quote history.** For confirmed post-hold fresh seeds only, compare the current behavior with requiring at least two changed quote pairs and movement on both bid and ask in a consecutive sub-ceiling run. Start at the first eligible frame, count later genuine quote changes and record the last-wide-to-first-eligible transition separately. A passing fixture preserves same-quote first-evaluation recovery after a short hold that kept its validated comparator and reproduces third-evaluation recovery on the baseline after a cap-reaching hold cleared it. Include ordinary cold starts, implausible-chain escapes, missing/crossed quotes, identical replays, alternating wide/narrow, duplicate evaluations, one- and two-sided recovery, restarts, two positions sharing a symbol, entry, VTS and crypto. CRM's reported ask-only recovery is a decision-sequence control with contemporaneous bid evidence unavailable. GIS's briefly narrow book is a displayed-size opportunity witness, with the booked fill checked against its own depth row. If the audit cites CRM's empty hot quote window, include a hit from the same archive query. Require direct linkage for exact quote consumption. Report changes in releases, blocked witnessed opportunities as a cost, added wait, bid-to-stop change, later decision direction, and right-censoring. A historical counterfactual ends where comparable evidence ends. Step 2 will register grading criteria, evidence limits and minimum coverage before the result is used to select policy.
3. **Return one reviewed policy disposition.** Retain the current release if evidence is insufficient or the candidate withholds genuinely executable exits without a demonstrated safety benefit. Recommend an amendment only with measured benefit and an explicit treatment of missed exit opportunities. Coltrane reviews the result and any policy choice before trading behavior changes. Preserve the absolute spread check, current freshness and fill standards, existing ring-independent escape, and Kyle's no-forced-exit instruction. The batch owns neither #1191 VTS alert delivery nor the #961/#1073 staging sequence. No deploy or settings change is part of the evidence comparison.

In plain language, when the TEC has to rebuild its quote reference after a wide spread, compare resuming as soon as the book looks usable with waiting to see both quotes move. The test has to catch a frozen quote without keeping a real exit waiting when enough buyers are already there.
