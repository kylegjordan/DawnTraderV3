## 0. WHAT THIS BATCH IS FOR, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Two geometry gates stand between a strategy's signal and a trade: the **reward-to-risk floor** (`expectancy_gates.min_rr`) and the **reachability ceiling** (`expectancy_gates.reach_atr_max`). Both are per-(strategy × class) since `B-GEOMETRY-REACH-BASELINE`. **Neither has ever been set from realised outcomes** — `min_rr` was derived in June as *a notch below each strategy's own mean RR*, and `reach_atr_max` is one number, `4.0`, in all four seeded rows. This batch replaces both with a **new baseline placeholder** chosen against the evidence we actually hold, and records what it would take to falsify each value. **It is not a calibration; Phase 25 still calibrates.**

### 1.2 THE FOUR FINDINGS THIS TABLE CARRIES — each stated so it can be falsified
**F-1 — `strong_bull_trend` is OFF on BOTH classes, and on xStock for a SECOND reason nobody has recorded.** Crypto: 0 of 366,871, 98.65 % `unreachable` — the known `#1052` result (declared multiplier 6.0 against a 4.0 ceiling). ⛔ **NEW: xStock is 0 of 406, and its second refusal reason is a gate standing exactly on its own structural value.** Crypto carries a seeded floor of **1.95**; xStock has **no row**, so it inherits the class default **2.00** — and `strong_bull_trend`'s RR is `target_exit_atr_multiplier / stop_loss_atr_multiplier` = 6.0 / 3.0 = **2.0**, measured across 406 evaluations as `rrMin 1.9999999999999791 … rrMax 2.0000000000000155`. ⇒ **`rr < minRR` is decided by floating-point noise at the fourteenth decimal place, not by anything about the trade.** ⚠️ **THE MECHANISM IS FROM THE CODE AND THE TWO CONSTANTS, NOT FROM THE 18.23 % DROP SHARE** — that share is era-mixed (§1, pre-2026-06-27 the floor was 2.5 and refused this strategy wholesale), so it may be entirely historical. **The knife edge is real today; its firing rate is unmeasured.** *Falsified by:* an xStock `strong_bull_trend` evaluation whose recorded `rr` is materially below 2.00, which would make the span genuine rather than numerical.

**F-2 — `dhma` is OFF on crypto for a reason that is a DECISION, not a defect.** 0 of 6,781; RR span `0.49…1.00` against the class default `2.00`. `dhma` is one of the three non-ATR strategies that feed reachability a pair-ATR (`SYSTEM_MANUAL`, reorg-B2.1 wiring note) and its structural RR is **below 1**. It got no per-strategy floor in June because it was under the 200-sample threshold **in that window**. ⇒ **bug-taxonomy outcome (2): working-as-designed-but-UNADDRESSED.** The June migration already deferred exactly this question (trade-vs-shelve for `morning_star`/`support_bounce`/`volatility_edge`) to **Phase-25 25-20**; `dhma` belongs in the same place, not in a silent unblock. *Falsified by:* a `dhma` evaluation with RR ≥ 2.0.

**F-3 — THE JUNE `min_rr` DERIVATION IS SELF-SUPPRESSING BY CONSTRUCTION, AND THAT IS WHY THE DROP RATES LOOK LIKE THIS.** The migration header states the rule verbatim: *"set a notch below that strategy's own measured mean RR — spread strategy (rrMin != rrMax) → floor = max(1.0, round(mean × 0.90, 2)); fixed-RR strategy (rrMin ≈ rrMax) → floor = max(1.0, round(mean − 0.05, 2))"*. ⛔ **A floor placed at 90 % of the mean of the population it gates refuses approximately the lower half of that population by construction** — and for a right-skewed quantity (RR is bounded below by 0 and unbounded above; crypto `morning_star` runs to 31.72) **0.9 × mean sits ABOVE the median, so it refuses MORE than half.** Observed: `support_bounce` 57.45 %, crypto `morning_star` 60.50 %, `reverse_impulse` 69.54 %, `volatility_edge` 74.79 %. ⛔⛔ **And three floors now sit ABOVE their strategy's current mean** — crypto `vwap_pullback` 2.44 vs 2.12 (88.35 % refused), crypto `reverse_impulse` 2.40 vs 2.11, xStock `morning_star` 1.00 vs 0.92 — so the June window's mean and the cumulative mean are **not the same number**, and nothing re-reads it. *Falsified by:* a re-derivation on the current window returning the same floors. ⚠️ **The load-bearing half of this finding is the FLOOR-vs-MEAN comparison, which is era-free; the refusal SHARES beside it are era-mixed per §1 and are cited as upper bounds.**

**F-4 — FOR FIVE (strategy × class) CELLS THE RR GATE IS A KNIFE EDGE, EXACTLY AS `#1052` FOUND FOR REACHABILITY.** `RR span = 2.00…2.00` on crypto `strong_bull_trend`, crypto `vwap_bounce`, xStock `sma_trend_ride`, xStock `vwap_bounce`, xStock `strong_bull_trend`. For a multiplier-derived strategy **RR is `target_exit_atr_multiplier / stop_loss_atr_multiplier` — a constant** (`strong_bull_trend`: 6.0 / 3.0 = 2.00). ⇒ **`#1052`'s standing rule ("a threshold is only a dial if the quantity it gates is distributed") applies to `min_rr` and NOT ONLY to `reach_atr_max`, and the `ADJUSTMENT_FRAMEWORK` entry does not say so.** Four of those five cells are alive by **0.05**, and the fifth (xStock `strong_bull_trend`) is not alive at all because its floor IS the spike. **Raw extremes, all five: `rrMin` between 1.9999999999999463 and 1.9999999999999922, `rrMax` between 2.0000000000000107 and 2.0000000000000484 — a spread of ~1e-13.** *Falsified by:* a distributed `rr` histogram in any of those five cells.

1. **Is the guard-eval tracker an admissible pre-gate instrument for OBJ-3?** It counts refusals at the gate, so the `atrsToTarget` tail is measured over signals the gate then rejected — pre-gate in the cross-section. **But is the SIGNAL SET itself post-gate over time?** I believe not (the gate refuses a signal; it does not alter price history or the scanner), and I would rather have that checked than assumed.
2. **`#1052`'s spread rule applied to `min_rr` (F-4)** — do you agree it generalises, and should the `ADJUSTMENT_FRAMEWORK` entry be widened from reachability to *any* geometry threshold in the same commit as OBJ-4?

That makes the ceiling the knob **for this strategy**, while `2.4g-5` holds the multiplier as Kyle's. 4. **Does `signal_eval_provenance` carry enough to recompute both gated quantities per row?** It has the stop, the target and the ATR but **no explicit entry price** — I intend to test `forming_close` as the entry proxy against a known cohort before any value is derived from it, and I would rather you rule on the proxy than have me assume it.

My inclination is the **VTS counterfactual cohort** — the tagged-and-simulated `rr_below_min` trades — the one population whose outcome is observed *after* refusal. If you agree, the derivation belongs in the Step-2 audit and not here.
