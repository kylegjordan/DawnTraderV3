The published research supports testing the hour of entry, executable costs and holding time. The closest 15-minute study uses an opening-range breakout in the underlying US equities, with long and short entries and a mandatory same-session exit. DawnTrader's Strong Bull Trend paper xStock path uses a rolling six-bar high, long entries and a different stop and target. ## What a matched comparison would have to show

Freeze the Strong Bull Trend opportunity set before either baseline or candidate geometry gate applies. Admit the same pre-gate set into the comparison and carry geometry refusals, marketable executions, maker waits, never fills and displaced openings in the denominator. Require exact signal bars and ATR, quote time/side/depth, decision and booked entry, original price levels, and position/slot accounting. Match or stratify by session segment, liquidity and fill arm. Compare net results after the applicable fees on executed prices and time capital remained committed. A sample with unresolved quote, volatility, slot or holding dependencies gives an insufficient result. On a pre-registered held-out opportunity set, failure to improve net outcome per unit of capital time over the existing baseline falsifies a proposed improvement. This defines what could change the published-practice assessment. The exact empirical verdict, deployment and trading settings remain governed by the existing KD-73 and KD-76 record.
