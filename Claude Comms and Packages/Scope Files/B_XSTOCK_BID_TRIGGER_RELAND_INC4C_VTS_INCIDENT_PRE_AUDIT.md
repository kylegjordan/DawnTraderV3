## Implementation plan for review

**P1 — Classify at the current VTS rail boundary (A1–A2, A5).** Construct a bounded evidence view for the actual producer, channel, active subscription identity and per-symbol session-eligibility group. Retain the exact symbols and denominators. Unknown eligibility can form its own group with positive source evidence. Explicitly known exceptions are kept in their own group. Require two complete adjacent 60-second windows of positive observation coverage on the same subscription, with at least 10 distinct affected monitored VTS symbols and at least 80% of that group's monitored symbols repeatedly stale and lacking genuine updates in both windows. There has to be evidence of the actual subscription and completed observation windows independent of the stale reasons, such as an observed warm producer's update gap. A cold counter, market label, common `too_old` reason or fresh replay receipt never substitutes. Maintain the original warning when sufficient evidence has not arrived by the existing crossing. A proven closed cohort can keep one informational episode. After a proven reopening it can issue its first warning if it is still positively correlated and the existing paging gate permits. Existing input signals of affirmative closure only. Adapters and fixtures can supply explicitly proven holiday, early-close and exception inputs for tests. Changes to the venue calendar, assumed entitlement, threshold for usable prices, 4c clocks and price selection are outside this amendment.

**P2 — Commit trade membership and outcome as one grouped persistence operation (A1, A3–A4).** Keep the existing store and writer lock. Add a scoped grouped mutation that reads the latest state under lock and atomically commits the stable episode identity, member records and their classification/transition effects. Collect first refusals, age clocks, reason changes, accepted VTS decisions, exact closes and independent fallback dispositions before a bounded flush past the exit work. Key by episode generation and exact trade/streak identity. Multiple trades on one symbol each keep a record. Represent the 30-minute exposure per member once with its original clock. Until a successful join or fallback is confirmed, keep the pending crossing eligible for the next flush, including after the threshold. Track first exposure as durable provisional evidence, in the same batch mechanism, so restart after a failed crossing can reconstruct it. On process loss before the first durable observation, use positive retained refusal evidence only and record unknown exposure if it cannot be reconstructed. Do not mark it handled. A completed/closed member carries an exact witness, including one that completes while its join is being retried. A member that no longer qualifies, or could not durably join ahead of dispatch, goes straight to its individual route from the original paging start. Keep a serialized lane with at most one bounded retry batch per sweep, coalesce repeat observations by exact identity, preserve independent fallback, and surface writer failure without consuming the event. Apply write effects in observed order and verify latest membership under the lock. Old incident-free rows, ACK/resolve semantics and ordinary ownership remain compatible. Retain member evidence after an episode ends under the existing alert retention rules.

**P3 — Deliver one confirmed incident warning and retain recovery (A1–A5).** Route the typed #1191 episode to ANALYST through the existing owner mapping and dispatcher. Leave each episode and member readable with no user action required. Keep a durable first-delivery state and a bounded claim per episode, recover abandoned claims for next dispatcher tick, and retry that same alert ID when delivery fails. Skip ordinary Discord resurfaces only after a confirmed first delivery for this typed warning. An independent problem, including a PDD-style unpriced close, follows its existing route and resurface contract. A short warning that recovers before dispatch still has one due first delivery with its terminal state attached. Verified-closure informational entries have no warning owed until their first valid escalation. Known Discord success followed by persisted receipt cannot send again. An ambiguous success may retry with the same visible incident ID. Tests must expose that limit explicitly rather than claiming exactly-once delivery.

Require two adjacent complete 60-second recovery windows. In each, at least 80% of distinct incident-cohort symbols with remaining open members (rounded up, minimum one) need genuine updates that reached the sink and matching fresh and guard-accepted VTS looks. Record both denominators and the healthy control. Count exact decisions/closes as disposition witnesses for exited members, rather than silently shrinking a denominator due to missing data. If the remaining denominator is zero with positive closure/decision witnesses for all members, the record can end by member disposition without declaring feed recovery. If the group recovers, hand still-refusing trades straight back to their independent route, with original exposure and reason. Informational closure, replay snapshots, a build restart and partial recovery each leave the prior generation in place. A later positively correlated stale event following measured recovery establishes a new generation.

**P4 — Verify using recorded and fault-injected controls (A1–A5).** In an isolated real temporary alert store and mock Discord, replay all 450 member crossings with explicitly homogeneous fixture provenance. Require one first warning delivered, all exact unique members and evidence, and no second ordinary resurface. Run a mixed eligibility/independent-source test and a recent normal-session isolated-stale test whose independent notices must still deliver, plus controls for quiet/wide, crypto, paper and the PDD unpriced path with existing reason/routing. Exercise closed, reopening still stale, partial recovery, full recovery with an outlier, repeated episode, cold/replay-only, failed join/rename on both sides of commit, member decision/close or reason change while a join is pending, and dispatcher failure, ambiguity, concurrency and short-event retry. Assert actual locked reads and atomic rewrites for join, exposure/decision updates, reason exits, recovery and no-op/restart: at most two full reads and one rewrite per nonempty flush, no rewrite for a no-op, no alert-store or Discord I/O awaited while the pass has exit work remaining. Compare measured stage and pass time on the same 450-member seed with normal and no-alert controls. Assert separate business output (exact VTS decision/close IDs and selected prices), so a fast run cannot pass by skipping VTS. At a separately authorized future deployment, do an immediate read-only sanity check of exact build, normal exit decisions, owner routes and alert-state compatibility. No delayed rollout proof or observation is proposed for this implementation. The existing November 27 14:45–15:00Z or earlier named genuine-update read with NEW is the recovery read for the already serving incident, not evidence that this proposed implementation works.
