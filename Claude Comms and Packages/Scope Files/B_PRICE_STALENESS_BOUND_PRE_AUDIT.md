### A2. The stall watchdog is a route-B amplifier on Sundays (Langston condition 2) — MEASURED
**Object:** archiver `runStallWatchdogTick` (every 30 s): returns inside the weekend close; otherwise, if the socket is OPEN and `Date.now() − state.lastDataMsgAt` exceeds the off-RTH (or RTH) threshold, it raises the critical `xstock-equity-feed-stall` alert and CLOSES the socket ⇒ reconnect ⇒ subscribe ⇒ one `snapshot` per symbol.
**Mechanism at the Sunday reopen:** `lastDataMsgAt` was last stamped Friday ~20:00 ET; the watchdog skips the weekend; at Sunday 20:00:00 ET the age is the whole weekend ⇒ it fires at once. The venue does not trade until 20:15, so 13 minutes later the age again exceeds the 750 s off-RTH threshold ⇒ it fires again.
**Evidence:** 14 `xstock-equity-feed-stall` alert rows since 2026-07-07, **12 of them at Monday 00:00-00:30Z (Sunday 20:00 ET)**, every week from 08-03 to 10-05; retained stderr shows the pair exactly — `2026-09-28 00:00:16 … silent 17455827ms > 750000ms (inLiquid=false)` and `00:13:16 … silent 778334ms`; `2026-10-05 00:00:28 … 94439984ms` and `00:13:28 … 778240ms`. ⇒ **the Sunday 20:00 and 20:13 ET whole-universe re-sends are OUR forced reconnects**, and the watchdog pages a CRITICAL alert twice every Sunday for a market that is simply not open yet.
**Not explained by it:** the 14 Saturday bursts of 2026-10-10 (Langston's Finding 1) — the watchdog does not run inside the weekend close; those were socket-close reconnects.

### A3. Route B at the code
archiver `parseTickerSnap(data, frameType)`: `lastTickerUpdateAt` is set only for `frameType === 'update'` (3a1 r4), but the MARK write `latestEquityTick.set(_sym, { price, tsMs: _now, kind, raw })` runs for ANY frame with a finite mark — so a `snapshot` stamps `tsMs = now`. With no prior entry (just after a restart) the mark is born fresh; with a prior entry its age is reset. The staleness gate (eng:2142) then reads the age it is given.
