### A2. The stall watchdog is a route-B amplifier on Sundays (Langston condition 2) — MEASURED, count corrected
**Object:** archiver `runStallWatchdogTick` (every 30 s): returns inside the weekend close; otherwise, if the socket is OPEN and `Date.now() − state.lastDataMsgAt` exceeds the off-RTH (or RTH) threshold, it raises the critical `xstock-equity-feed-stall` alert and CLOSES the socket ⇒ reconnect ⇒ subscribe ⇒ one `snapshot` per symbol.
**Mechanism:** at Sunday 20:00:00 ET the watchdog resumes with `lastDataMsgAt` hours old (decisive quantity: age > 750 s at 20:00 ET; 4.85 h on 09-28, not "the whole weekend") ⇒ it fires at once. The venue does not trade until 20:15, so ~13 minutes later the age again exceeds 750 s ⇒ it fires again (~778 s both weeks).
**Evidence — two instruments, two conventions, stated:** (i) **FIRES, from stderr** (the only instrument that can see both): `2026-09-28 00:00:16 … silent 17455827ms > 750000ms (inLiquid=false)` and `00:13:16 … silent 778334ms`; 10-05: `94439984ms` and `778240ms` (pm2 names a rotation by its CLOSE time, so 10-05's pair is in `error__2026-10-06_00-00-00.log`). (ii) **ROWS, from the alerts file:** 14 `xstock-equity-feed-stall` rows since 2026-07-07 (a genuine origin — the file reaches back to 05-17), **10 at Monday 00:00-00:30Z**, one per Monday 08-03 → 10-05; the 20:13 fire is dedupe-suppressed, so rows can never show the pair.
**Not explained by it:** the 14 Saturday subscribe bursts of 2026-10-10 (Langston's Finding 1) — the watchdog does not run inside the weekend close.

### A3. Route B at the code — and the live case
archiver `:254` sets `lastTickerUpdateAt` only for `frameType === 'update'` (3a1 r4); `:260` writes the MARK `{ price, tsMs: _now, kind, raw }` for ANY frame with a finite mark ⇒ a `snapshot` stamps `tsMs = now`.
**THE LIVE CASE (measured by Langston, 2026-10-10):** PLTR/USD's book was ONE frozen pair from 01:11:20Z — bid 191.00 / ask 213.80, `bid_qty` NULL, 13 occurrences across 20.79 h. It was re-sent at 21:58:26.092Z byte-identical to the 21:21:07Z frame (the inc-C deploy's subscribe), and at 21:58:40Z the engine logged `frame=ok … bidWouldFire=stop` — **the mark read FRESH on a 20.8-hour-old quote with the bid 3.12 below the stop; only the midpoint (202.4) not crossing the stop prevented a decision.** It was a `snapshot`: `T=0` in the 21:22:01Z standing record, whose 60 s window covers that frame. **P1 catches exactly this.**
**RESIDUAL, STATED (Langston): route B has NO observation clock.** The archiver reads 15 `data.*` fields and none is a time; `xstock_spot_ticker_snap.captured_at` is our clock. P1 makes `tsMs` the receipt time of the last CHANGED quote instead of the last REPLAY — a real improvement, still receipt age. **Route A gets genuine observation age (P3, it has `observedAt`); route B cannot from this feed. They are two kinds of fix and the SIM entry says so.**

### A4. The cold window P1 creates, and why r1's P6 was false (Langston, sent back)
**Cost of refusing until the first `update`, measured by Langston over the last two Sunday reopens:** 16 of 16 held symbols got a changed quote within 13.1 s (09-29: min 0.18 s, max 13.12, mean 4.66; 10-06: max 9.7). Proxy stated: "first changed quote" stands in for the first `update` (the snap table has no frame type) — an upper bound, since a replay cannot change a quote.
**r1's defect:** a cold mark refused as `equity_tick_stale_*` is family `quiet_market`; at an in-hours restart the class verdict is `not_quiet` ⇒ `familyJoinsStandingRecord` (`venue-quiet-alerting.ts:78`) returns false ⇒ it PAGES instead of joining. And `Date.now() − 0` prints a 56-year-old mark.
**What actually bounds it, read at the ref:** a page needs the position's streak to reach `exit_integrity.max_consecutive_price_skips` — **40 for `xstock_spot`** (staging `module_constants`, read 2026-10-11) — at `active_execution.monitoring_interval_ms` **1500** ⇒ ~60 s of consecutive refusals (eng:935). The measured cold window (≤13.1 s ≈ 9 ticks) does not reach it. **But r1 would still have filed cold ticks in the streak under a staleness reason, mislabelled, and uncountable — so P1's own acceptance test could not be written.** The fix is a distinct state, P1b.
