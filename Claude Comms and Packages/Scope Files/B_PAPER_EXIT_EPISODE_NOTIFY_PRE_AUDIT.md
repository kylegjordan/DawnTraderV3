### A3. What counts as a receipt and when a reminder is due

- `server/services/system-alerts.ts:606–627` promotes due rows and sets `fired_at`. `scripts/system-alerts.ts:85–159,216–242` formats and posts individual notifications. A success return with the `Discord alert posted` log is the current first-send receipt. The first loop does not use the failure return to preserve a pending first send. Existing repeat handling (`system-alerts.ts:761–862`, `scripts/system-alerts.ts` resurface path) fresh-reads state and advances back-off after successful delivery. A failed first post and a successful repeat are different facts.
- **Observed positive control.** Run the actual CLI twice with two due rows in a scratch `SYSTEM_ALERTS_FILE`, using a loopback webhook: a paper price-skip warning and an independent hollow warning for the same symbol. In run A the sink returned 500 for the paper warning and 204 for hollow. The store promoted 2 of 2, the sink received two attempts and the dispatcher logged one receipt, hollow. On the next run with the sink accepting all posts there were zero first attempts. In run B the sink returned 204 for both and two receipts appeared. The two resulting stores agreed in row state, presence of `fired_at` and metadata. The pre-audit used no real Discord sends. Step 3 must include this control against the changed path.
- **Same-key after promotion.** In a fresh scratch store, promoting one ordinary PGR warning set `fired_at` without any transport call. An attempted duration add on that same key returned the exact active ordinary UUID and its unchanged metadata. The same add on a different key minted a fresh UUID. This confirms that the first-delivery recovery must operate on the earlier exact UUID, independent of the grouping operation.
- **Reminder control.** Actual `computeResurfaceStale` at a fixed 22:00 UTC timestamp returned one unresolved active warning at its 6-hour first repeat, excluded the same row when acknowledged at that age, and returned a warning for Kyle escalation at a second repeat after its 12-hour successive interval. The active/acknowledged warning bases are 6h/12h, with authored 1×, 2×, 4× back-off, using `fired_at` first and the most recent successful repeat thereafter. `resolved`, `scheduled` and `info` are excluded. Acknowledgement affects the row's tier. It supplies no first-send receipt and resets no clock. A group timestamp alone cannot recover a child's due time. #340 and its completion report require back-off to advance on successful delivery only.
- **Acknowledgement during a post.** In the actual `processResurface`, a callback acknowledged the selected warning and returned delivery success. The store credited one repeat on that acknowledged UUID, and the 12-hour tick from first fire found zero due for that row. An acknowledgement before dispatch can change eligibility. An acknowledgement during a successful post cannot erase that completed repeat. Resolution during delivery remains terminal.

## Implementation plan for review

1. **Qualify and publish exact members (A1, A2, A4).** Only the thin paper xStock duration writer can qualify a member. Preserve the warning's own row, timestamps, severity, position and reason evidence. Publish exact child UUIDs and their link to one real aggregate UUID under the store's shared write protection, linked to a stable episode generation on the standing row. Re-read under the lock. Incomplete links, stale reads, competing row IDs and metadata mismatch cannot convey group ownership. For an exact ordinary paper warning on a key the thin writer can supersede:

   - **Known pending:** record first-delivery debt alongside ordinary promotion so an unsuccessful or interrupted post leaves the exact UUID pending. Immediately preserve the normal first-send slot, and have the dispatcher explicitly read the exact pending UUID on the next normal tick even if already active or acknowledged. Use its ordinary warning path and original clock. An actual first receipt or normal terminal evidence allows the sweep to re-evaluate that one symbol; other members may publish meanwhile.
   - **Legacy unknown:** when that exact still-unresolved same-position predecessor first blocks duration supersession, queue one labelled ordinary first-delivery catch-up on its existing UUID. This can repeat a historically delivered notice once in the success case. Its durable obligation uses the known-pending path on an unsuccessful or ambiguous post. Restrict the transition to positively matched same-key, same-position conflicts. Promotion, a prior reminder and acknowledgement supply no first receipt. Legacy rows that never collide keep their usual path.
   - **Known delivered or normally terminal:** use the existing position-specific supersession evidence and mint or join only after its exact predecessor permits it. An unrelated position or warning class cannot be silently superseded or claimed. Use the same ordinary receipt and identity test for a concurrent immediate writer as for a legacy row. The sweep never cancels an unserved normal first to make room for a group.

2. **Seal and re-arm the same position (A1, A2).** Freeze a generation for new joins only on a warmed `not_quiet` sample with genuine-update breadth at the existing threshold, from the same sweep observation. Commit the seal before releasing the scoped paper thin-duration one-shot. Mirror or replace that lane's ephemeral latch with membership by generation and exact position, keyed to the persisted seal. This allows the same still-unpriced position to qualify on renewed thin in the same regular session once the existing duration predicate is met. A store failure cannot release the latch. A restart rebuilds the scoped guard from committed generation data and keeps its stable ID. The generic duration clock and the one-shot behavior of the other routes keep their existing rules. Release the sealed generation's same-key predecessor by exact position supersession after actual first delivery or ordinary terminal evidence. Unserved first obligations move back to their ordinary UUID and keep the key until reconciled. Retain the prior generation, original clocks and historical receipt evidence. Recovery gives the independent post-recovery warning its ordinary route.

3. **Keep one owner for each delivery obligation (A2–A4).** Treat first and repeat separately. For each exact child UUID and obligation, an atomic state transition gives ownership to ordinary pending, one sealed group attempt, individual fallback, or a receipt/terminal record. The normal dispatcher consumes eligible ordinary pending, including promoted ordinary or fallback debt, before grouped work. It skips group-owned obligations only while the linkage and ownership are valid. It still delivers independent warnings on the same invocation. Before posting, claim the exact UUID set and attempt token under the store lock with process identity, birth and boot identity, then release the store lock. A deadline marks the attempt eligible for inspection. Another process cannot take over or transfer a still-live claim, even after the deadline. Transfer requires the original attempt to settle and release its claim, or positive proof the exact process is gone. Unknown owner liveness keeps the claim and raises a diagnostic on that obligation. Use a bounded post and verify the actual timer and direct CLI recovery path. Reconcile with a token comparison so a late result can never update a later owner.

   The first digest freezes its exact member set and includes count and aggregate UUID. Allow one further normal invocation to retry the same labelled group after a failed or interrupted send and lawful claim recovery. An acknowledgement from Discord lost before the store write can yield one duplicated labelled digest. After exhaustion, release under store lock to the already-existing child UUIDs and explicitly consume that individually owned debt on the next normal invocation, using the original trigger and repeat clocks. A settled receipt or exact terminal evidence ends the obligation. A late join owns its own first and appears in an explicitly labelled follow-up on the next eligible tick, combined with a due repeat only where both are owed. It inherits no earlier receipt. Aggregate acknowledgement or resolution cannot close a child's obligation.
