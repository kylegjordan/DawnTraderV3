### A3. What counts as a receipt and when a reminder is due

## Implementation plan for review

1. **Qualify and publish exact members (A1, A2, A4).** The thin paper xStock duration writer is the only source of eligibility. Preserve the existing warning's row, timestamps, severity, position and reason evidence. Commit member UUIDs and their linkage to one real aggregate UUID in a single locked store rewrite that merges with current rows. Use a stable episode generation linked to the standing row. An incomplete link, a stale snapshot, a return of a different same-key row or a metadata mismatch stays on the ordinary path. Record successful first delivery on the exact ordinary warning UUID where it can collide with the paper duration writer, without moving its existing dispatch slot or reminder clock. When a same-key independent row may still owe its first delivery, keep it deliverable and skip group ownership for that symbol until exact evidence permits ordinary supersession. Older rows with unknown receipt use the same conservative branch. A conflicting symbol cannot hold up eligible siblings. Keep pricing and exit decisions out of this write. Re-read after interrupted publication. Any new warning outside a complete durable group commitment remains individually deliverable.
2. **Persist re-arm evidence (A1, A2).** Open an episode on the first proved eligible duration cohort and reuse it for later proved members. Only a warmed `not_quiet` sample from the same measurement, with genuine updates satisfying the existing breadth gate, seals the generation for new joins. Persist that seal before the next thin duration episode can open another generation. Missing breadth, quiet, closed, restart and a clock reset preserve the last committed generation. Recovery gives current independent per-position warning rules their normal reach. Any unsettled obligation from the sealed generation goes back to its original row and original clock. Keep the old generation and delivered membership inspectable.
3. **Track delivery debt and bounded retry (A2–A4).** The first digest freezes the exact eligible member set at a normal dispatcher invocation and carries the aggregate UUID and count. Claim the attempt under the store lock with a durable token and expiry, release the lock before sending, and reconcile only that token and its exact covered UUIDs on success. Use the existing Discord transport and give ordinary warnings their existing first and repeat paths before group work. A late join retains its own first obligation and appears on the next natural tick's explicitly labelled follow-up for the same aggregate, combined with a due reminder only where those obligations coexist. It never inherits an old receipt. On failure keep pending with its exact set, allow one further normal invocation to retry the same labelled attempt, and hand any remaining or uncertain debt back to the ordinary path with its original trigger and repeat clocks. An already-promoted child's durable fallback debt must be read explicitly by the dispatcher and sent through the ordinary individual formatter/transport. Removing a link alone cannot requeue that first post. Keep this debt until actual receipt or exact terminal evidence. If Discord accepted a post just before a process loss, a retry can duplicate that labelled digest once. The bounded retry and labelled identity are deliberate review choices. Prove concurrent CLI and timer claims cannot both send a fresh attempt, and a live claim and expired claim recover without dropping a row. Aggregate acknowledgement/resolution must not settle individual obligations.
4. **Choose the earliest eligible reminder (A3, A4).** Reuse `computeResurfaceStale` on original child clocks and current states. At the first natural tick when any child is due, one grouped reminder may cover the exact due unresolved subset; apply Kyle escalation if any included child requires it. Recheck mutable state at send and at receipt. Advance counters/time only for the exact successfully covered children still eligible, never for resolved rows, later due children or first-send-only joins. If an aggregate closes or fails its validity checks, ordinary per-alert delivery keeps the original clocks. Keep first-attempt, first receipt, failed attempt and repeat receipt as distinct evidence. An operator can fetch the exact covered set using the aggregate ID included in Discord.
5. **Verify before asking for a runtime change (A1–A4).** Use the app's producer/sweep and real dispatcher entry points with a scratch store, virtual clock and loopback receiver. Prove the historical 15-shape acceptance, exact actual receipt count, safety negatives and deterministic retry below. Measure the number of store writes per sweep and dispatcher batch with a concurrent independent write. Bound publication to one group write per sweep and reconciliation to one batch write per attempt, beyond existing per-position work. Confirm no extra timers, database schema or routing changes. Step 7 would inspect served artifacts, ordinary System Alerts and exact post evidence through a fresh approved route. This plan carries no staging observation period and requests no staging window. A later live observation dependency would return to Step 2 to register a window before scheduling it.
