**(F1) `regime_factor_alternates.metadata` does NOT exist as a top-level column.**

Full column list: `id, source_type, signal_id, vts_trade_id, pair_symbol, evaluated_at, factor_name, factor_state, real_decision (jsonb), alternate_decision (jsonb), replay_outcome (jsonb), replay_completed_at, strategy, exchange, asset_class`.

The `metadata.calibrationFrameworkVersion='b76_chain_final'` filter from `drift-dashboard-aggregator.ts:1057-1069` reads `real_decision->'metadata'->>'calibrationFrameworkVersion'` — nested INSIDE the `real_decision` JSONB, not a top-level metadata column.

**Implication for your Q2a hybrid suggestion:** writing `feedState: 'clean' | 'fallback'` into "regime_factor_alternates.metadata JSONB" isn't possible as-described. Options:

**My recommendation: (c).** `macro_feed_archive` is already the source of truth for feed health, written every 60s with `fallback_active BOOLEAN`. Even if we reverse iteration order today, the oldest pending rows will still be unmatchable. **The cron fix MUST include the queue cleanup pass (your B-NEW-32.5 proposal), not just iteration-order reversal.** Otherwise the next cron pass just moves the cursor a little further into a different batch of unmatchable rows.

This makes B-NEW-32.5 even more important than your round-1 framing suggested. Strong concur.

### Two more risks I want to surface

**MR6 — JSONL retention vs replay catchup race.**

If we ship B-NEW-32.5 today and it drains the queue, the cron resumes nightly processing. But JSONL files keep aging out (~14 day window). If we go too long between B-NEW-32.5 and B-NEW-33, the JSONL files that hold today's outcomes may age out before we replay them. Concretely: an alternate emitted today (May 15) needs its trade to close AND be in a JSONL file when replay runs. If replay runs in 16 days (May 31) and the May 15 JSONL is aged out by then, that row goes from "matchable" to "unmatchable" silently.

**Mitigation:** ship B-NEW-32.5 → cron runs nightly at 04:00 UTC → first post-fix run on May 16 04:00 UTC scores everything still within window. B-NEW-33 can ship anytime after that. As long as we don't let the cron stall again, we're fine.

**Detection:** the lightweight `[ALERT-WORTHY]` log line for CoinGecko (Q4a) doesn't cover replay-cron health. Recommend a parallel `[ALERT-WORTHY]` line if `pendingVtsTradeRows > 5000 AND rowsReplayed = 0` over consecutive nightly runs. ~5 LOC in `replay-ablation.ts`. Folds into B-NEW-32.5.
