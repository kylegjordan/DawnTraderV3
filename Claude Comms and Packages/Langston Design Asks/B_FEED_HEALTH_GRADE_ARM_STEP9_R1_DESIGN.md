## r4 (2026-10-17) — Langston's r3 BLOCKER-3 folded, and a FINDING it led to
### 🟨 FINDING F-3 — the monitor cannot see a reconnect (rule 24 outcome 1, real defect; §9.4 disposition 1, folded — the grade this batch arms depends on it)
**Mechanism:** `recordSnapshot` derives the interval's reconnects as `Math.max(0, wsStatus.reconnectAttempts − this.lastReconnectAttempts)` (`feed-integrity-monitor.ts:466-467`), and the comments at `:135` / `:464` call `reconnectAttempts` CUMULATIVE. **It is not:** the adapter adds 1 inside the reconnect timer (`kraken-websocket-adapter.ts:1513-1515`, delay 1 s doubling to 30 s) and **resets it to 0 the moment a connection opens** (`handleOpen`, `:597`; also `stop()`, `:546`). So a drop that recovers inside one 5-minute interval reads 0 → 1 → 0 between snapshots and the delta is 0; a fall between two reads (2 → reset → 1) is also 0 by the `max`; the counter is non-zero only when a snapshot lands MID-reconnect (a 1-30 s window in 300 s) or while it is failing repeatedly. A second path, `reconnectWithBackoff` (`:3330-3357`), can cancel the timer before it adds 1 (`connect()` → `cleanup()`, `:568`, `:555-557`). The adapter's only monotonic counter, `reconnectRetries` (`:390`, `:3352`), covers that one path and is not exposed.
**Measured, with a control:** retained `out.log` 2026-10-16 ~07:23Z → 10-17 ~11:00Z holds **7** `[KrakenWS] Scheduling reconnect` lines (reconnects happen) against Langston's 49-of-49 daily reports reading `reconnectCount 0` — consistent with a counter that cannot see them. *(7 is attempts scheduled, not a census of drops.)*
**History:** P19-B6.7 (`#301`) switched the source when *"the prior 2nd-WS exposed a reset-on-read counter"* (`:464-465`) and assumed the primary's counter was cumulative; it never was. The P19-B6.9 parity gate (`getRollingWindowReadiness`) reads the same deltas, so its ring has been near-blind too — it reads ~100 % by construction.
**Fix (folded):** the adapter keeps a monotonic `reconnectsCompleted` — +1 in `handleOpen` when the open is a reconnect (`wasReconnect`, `:592`), never reset — exposed on `getStatus()`; the monitor's delta reads it. Counting COMPLETED reconnects (one per drop) rather than attempts means a drop that takes three tries counts once. The stale "CUMULATIVE" comments are corrected. **Test:** an adapter that drops and reconnects inside one interval yields an interval delta of 1 (it yields 0 today — mutation-proving); three failed attempts then a success yield 1.
REVIEWER: claim-only · "under what states is the monitor's reconnect count non-zero vs zero after a real reconnect" · listed 9 states, consistent with the mechanism above · re-derived y (`:597`, `:466-467`, `:1513-1515` read at the ref; the 7-vs-0 control).
