The **dispatch** gate reads the tick age alone, as planned (FINDING-3's loosening applies there only).

Moved to the helper's default parameter — age arithmetic only, never a time-of-day term — and the fence is green.
3. **A cold tick is refused as `cold` even if its receipt age would also exceed the limit** (cold is tested before age) — it is the more specific reason.
