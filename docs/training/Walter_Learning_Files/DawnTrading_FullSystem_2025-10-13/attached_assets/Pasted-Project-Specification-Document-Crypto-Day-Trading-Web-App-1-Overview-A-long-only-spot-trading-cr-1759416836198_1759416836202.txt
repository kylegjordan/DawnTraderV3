Project Specification Document: Crypto Day Trading Web App
1) Overview

A long-only, spot-trading crypto day-trading web app that:

Scans Kraken markets hourly, monitors shortlisted pairs live, executes VWAP Pullback, ABCD Long, and SMA Trend Ride strategies.

Enforces disciplined risk: risk-per-trade (R), max concurrent exposure %, max open trades, slippage tolerance, order-book depth checks.

Supports Start/Stop master control, Live vs Test (paper) mode.

Tracks every trade, fees, P/L and R (also in $), exports tax-ready CSV for chosen periods.

Integrates ChatGPT as both AI Analyst (reports/suggestions) and AI Assistant (always-on help + voice/text).

Optional AI Capital Allocation Mode to split capital across simultaneous signals within guardrails.

Responsive web app (Android + Windows priority) with a dashboard-first UX.

2) Trading Strategies (long-only, intraday)
2.1 VWAP Pullback Long

Rules (fixed): Price above VWAP; pullback to VWAP; bullish reversal candle.

Entry: Buy near VWAP after confirmation.

Stop: Below VWAP or pullback low (whichever farther).

Exits: Partial at prior swing high; remainder at +2R or on VWAP loss.

2.2 ABCD Long

Rules (fixed): A spike → B pullback → C higher low (above VWAP) → D breakout.

Entry: Buy stop above C-range high.

Stop: Below C low.

Exits: Measured move (A–B added to C-break) or +2R.

2.3 SMA Trend Ride

Rules (fixed): Uptrend above SMA; pullback to SMA; bounce confirmation.

Entry: Buy on SMA bounce.

Stop: Below prior swing low or SMA break.

Exits: Close below SMA or fixed +R target.

Spot-only: No margin, no leverage, no shorting, no derivatives.

3) Screener Criteria (hourly)

Market cap ≥ $50M; 24h volume ≥ $20M; daily range ≥ 5%; price ≥ $0.001; listed on Kraken.

Liquidity safety: project slippage vs. position size at stop using current order-book depth. Skip if projected slippage > tolerance.

Tiered slippage tolerance defaults (user-adjustable):

Majors (BTC/ETH/top-20): 0.5%

Mid-caps: 2%

Small/meme: 3–5% (use caution)

Eligible coins go to Watchlist; live monitoring (WebSocket) checks strategy triggers.

4) Capital Allocation & Risk (non-AI vs AI)
Non-AI Mode (default)

Risk per trade (R) fixed (e.g., $10–$500). Position size = R ÷ stop distance.

Checks before entry: (1) available balance, (2) per-trade risk = R, (3) max concurrent exposure % (e.g., 10–20% of account), (4) max open trades (e.g., 1–5), (5) slippage tolerance & order-book depth.

Continuous screening even with open trades; opens additional trades if within limits.

AI Capital Allocation Mode (optional)

ChatGPT Analyst+Allocator can recommend or instruct capital splits across simultaneous signals based on recent performance/expectancy.

Guardrails still enforced: per-trade R cap, max exposure %, max trades, slippage/OB checks.

User toggle: AI Capital Allocation ON/OFF.

5) Execution Workflow

Scan (hourly) → REST pulls tickers/stats.

Filter → Screener criteria + liquidity/OB checks.

Monitor (live) → WebSocket for shortlisted pairs; compute VWAP/SMA locally; detect patterns.

Go/No-Go → Risk/balance/exposure/slippage checks.

Trade → Place buy; auto-place stop & targets (OCO if supported).

Manage → Scale/exit per strategy rules; time-outs if needed.

Log → Persist full trade + fees.

Report → AI Analyst summaries; notifications to user devices.

6) Start/Stop + Modes

Start/Stop Button on Dashboard.

Stop: continue managing open trades to their rules; do not open new ones.

Start: resume full workflow.

Mode Toggle: Live vs Test (paper). Paper trades simulate orders/fills/prices (Kraken sandbox or local sim).

7) Trade Tracking & Fees

For each execution (buy/sell/partial):

Timestamps, pair, strategy, mode (Live/Test), entry/exit price, qty, fee per leg (maker/taker), total trade value (gross), fees (abs + %), net P/L ($ and %), R and R in $.

Store stop/target(s), slippage realized %, order IDs, exchange response.

Aggregations: per-day/week/month, per strategy, per coin, fee totals, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, equity curve.

8) CSV Export & Tax Reporting

CSV Export (user-selected period: custom dates, YTD, last FY, etc.) with all accountant-friendly fields:

Trade ID, date/time (UTC), pair, side, qty, entry/exit, gross proceeds, fees, net proceeds, cost basis method (FIFO/LIFO configurable), realized P/L, mode (Live/Test), strategy, exchange, tx hashes if on-chain (N/A for Kraken spot).

Download from History page; also email export link if desired.

9) Notifications (mobile-friendly)

Push/email/Telegram (configurable):

Filled BUY: pair, qty, entry, stop, targets, slippage %, fees.

Filled SELL/Exit: pair, exit price, gain/loss ($, %, R and $R), net after fees.

AI Analyst alerts: e.g., “VWAP outperforming this week; consider wider stop buffer from 0.2% → 0.3%.”

Quiet hours setting.

10) AI: Analyst + Assistant (ChatGPT API)

Analyst (reports): Daily/weekly/monthly summaries (win rate, profit factor, expectancy, best/worst assets & strategies, parameter suggestions). Explains R in $ for clarity.

Assistant (always-on): Floating button (text & voice). Context-aware help (features, terms), quick setting changes (“Set R to $50”), “why” explanations for trades/exits, and on-demand asset analysis.

Searchable Analysis Tab:

Push view: Trend insights by day/week/month/year, recommendations, anomalies.

Search: Enter symbol (e.g., BTC, ETH, PEPE) to get on-demand technical snapshot (VWAP/SMA context, recent performance, typical slippage, historical strategy outcomes).

Goals Tracking:

User sets goals (e.g., “+5R/month”, “max drawdown < 8%”, “win rate > 55%”).

AI tracks progress vs goals, surfaces gaps and specific adjustments.

11) Strategy Variable Customization (with education)

Adjustable (with tooltips and impact notes):

Risk per trade (R $); max exposure %; max open trades; stop buffer %; profit targets (R multiples); SMA length; min 24h volume; min daily range; slippage tolerance (tiered or global).

Fixed (not user-editable): strategy DNA conditions (e.g., VWAP must hold for VWAP Pullback).

12) Data & Calculations

VWAP calculated locally from Kraken trades or 1-min OHLCV.

SMA/EMA computed locally on selected timeframe.

Projected slippage pre-trade: simulate market order of position size at stop against current order-book depth; compare average fill vs stop; skip if > tolerance.

Realized slippage logged per exit.

13) System Tools & Architecture

Exchange: Kraken REST (scan/ohlc/account) + WebSocket (live prices/order events).

Backend: Python/Node service for strategy engine, risk checks, order routing, fee/slippage calc.

DB: Postgres (or Supabase/Firebase) for trades, parameters, reports, goals, notifications.

Frontend: Responsive React/Vue (Android/Windows friendly).

Auth/Secrets: Secure API key vault, role-based access.

AI: ChatGPT (GPT-4o/GPT-5) via API; optional LangChain/LlamaIndex for structured data access.

Notifications: Email (SMTP), Push (FCM/WebPush), optional Telegram/Discord.

14) Dashboard (landing page)

Portfolio Overview: Current value; realized/unrealized P/L.

Start/Stop Toggle (status badge).

Mode: Live / Test indicator & toggle.

Active Trades: pair, entry, stop, targets, unrealized P/L, fees to date.

Recent Trades (last 10): coin/symbol, qty, buy/sell price, values, fees, net P/L ($, %, R, $R), timestamps.

AI Analyst Preview: latest notes; See more → Analysis page.

Quick actions: CSV Export, Settings, AI Assistant (floating).

15) Settings

Risk & Exposure: R ($), max exposure %, max open trades.

Strategy Params: stop buffer, targets, SMA length, min vol/range, slippage tolerance (by tier or global).

AI: Allocation mode ON/OFF; report cadence; goals.

Notifications: channels, quiet hours.

Mode: Live/Test; Kraken keys; sandbox toggle.

16) Privacy & Safety

Spot-only; no margin; guardrails prevent negative balances.

Rate-limiters; order retry logic; idempotent order IDs.

Failsafes: network/API outage handling; Emergency Kill (optional setting): immediate market exit of all positions.

17) Future Extensions

Multi-exchange universe (Binance, Bybit) with unified symbol map.

Sentiment/news signals; on-chain flows (for listed assets).

Paper “A/B testing” of parameter variants.

18) Text-Based Architecture Flow (for Replit)

Kraken API (REST/WebSocket) → market data, orders, fills.

Screener Module (hourly REST) → filters by volume/range/cap/price; checks order-book depth vs projected position; enforces slippage tolerance.

Strategy Engine (live WebSocket) → computes VWAP/SMA; detects VWAP Pullback / ABCD / SMA setups.

Risk & Allocation Layer

Non-AI: R sizing, available balance, max exposure %, max open trades, slippage check.

AI Allocation (optional): ChatGPT recommends/distributes capital; guardrails enforced.

Trade Engine → submits buy; places stop/targets (OCO); manages exits; logs realized slippage & fees from fills.

Database → stores trades, fees, params, goals, reports, notifications.

AI Analyst (ChatGPT) → pulls stats from DB; generates daily/weekly/monthly insights; tracks goals; writes reports back to DB.

AI Assistant (ChatGPT) → floating button; text/voice help; quick setting changes; on-demand asset analysis; explains actions.

Notifications Service → sends buy/sell and P/L alerts to user devices; respects quiet hours.

Web Frontend (React/Vue) → Dashboard (portfolio, active trades, last 10, AI preview), History (filters + CSV Export), Analysis (push insights + search by symbol), Settings (risk/AI/notifications), Start/Stop & Live/Test toggles.
