Task: Create a Dedicated “Reports” Panel

Remove the “Export CSV” button from the main dashboard (top bar).

The export functionality will be moved to the new Reports panel.

Add a new “Reports” panel accessible from the left navigation menu (same hierarchy level as Dashboard, Strategies, etc.).

Inside the Reports panel, implement the following structure:

Tabs or sections for:

Canned Reports (predefined templates)

Custom/Ad Hoc Reports (user-defined filters)

Exports (CSV/PDF downloads)

Canned Reports to include at launch:

a. Tax Report (Accountant-ready)

Contains all data required for tax filing:

Trade ID, Date/Time (UTC & Local), Symbol

Buy/Sell indicator, Entry Price, Exit Price, Quantity

Entry Fee, Exit Fee, Total Fees

Gross P/L, Net P/L, P/L %

Holding Duration

Realized vs. Unrealized Gains

Cost Basis

Short-term vs. Long-term classification (based on 1-year cutoff)

Export formats: CSV and PDF summary

b. Performance Summary Report

Aggregates by symbol, strategy, or account

KPIs: Win rate, Avg. R Multiple, Max Drawdown, Total P/L, Sharpe Ratio

c. Trade Journal Report

Chronological trade-by-trade log with notes, outcomes, and optional screenshots

d. Fees & Costs Report

Summarizes total fees by month, exchange, and symbol

Useful for expense tracking and tax deductions

e. P&L Reports (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual)

Aggregates totals for Gross P/L, Net P/L, Fees, Win/Loss count, and Avg. hold time

Custom Reports Section:

Allows users to filter by date range, symbol, strategy, and account type

Option to save and re-run filters

Output exportable as CSV and/or PDF

User Experience Notes:

The Reports panel should have a clean, minimal layout with a date picker, filter inputs, and “Generate Report” / “Export CSV/PDF” buttons.

No live trading data should be fetched or modified during report generation. Reports should query internal or cached data only.
