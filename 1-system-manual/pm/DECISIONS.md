# PM Claude — decision ledger and how decisions are tracked to done

> **Why this exists (Kyle, 2026-11-04):** *"it's very important that when we make decisions together and then you relay those decisions to the others, that you're recording those decisions and then you're making sure that those decisions are being implemented by the other sessions … it's up to you to keep them honest."* Also: nothing that has been started is left unfinished unless we made a deliberate decision to cancel it.
> **Owner:** PM Claude. **This file is the record of what Kyle decided.** The plan files remain the authority for ORDER; a decision's "where" column points at the plan line that carries it.

## How the system works

1. **Record.** Every decision Kyle makes with PM Claude gets a `KD-` number here (**K**yle **D**ecision; the plain `D-1`, `D-2` were already used for other things in the issue ledger) **in the same turn**, with his words where he gave them, the date and the source.
2. **Place, and tag.** If the decision changes the work, PM Claude writes it into the plan the same turn, at a named position, **and the plan line itself carries the `KD-` number** (in its "agreed" cell or its note). The issue ledger's `HOME:` line carries it too. **That tag is what ties the log to the plans.**
3. **Relay.** PM Claude posts the decision on Discord to each session it binds, naming the session at the start and quoting the number. **Each session replies `ACK KD-n` and updates its own task list.** *(The first relays on 2026-11-04 said "D-2"; it is the same decision as KD-2.)*
4. **Verify, against the object, not the reply.** Status moves `RELAYED` → `ACKED` → `IN PLAN` → `IN WORK` → `DONE` only on evidence: the plan line at the review branch, the session's task-list line, the `B-NAME Step-N` commits, and for DONE the completion report or the agreed close.
5. **Reconcile the log with the governed plans (Kyle, 2026-11-04, KD-6).** `node scripts/pm/reconcile-decisions.mjs` reads this log and the governed plans (the sprint and pre-sprint plan, the after-live list, the roadmap, the issue ledger, the PM rules) **at the review branch**, and reports: (a) a decision whose anchor is missing from the plan it names; (b) a `KD-` tag in a plan with no decision here; (c) a decision marked DONE whose plan line still reads open. **It runs at every crew check and before PM Claude reports status to Kyle; a mismatch is fixed, or brought to Kyle, the same day.** Each decision's **anchors** cell names `file: token` pairs the check looks for (or `none — <why>` when it changes no plan).
6. **Check on a rhythm.** At every crew check (at least daily while sessions are working) PM Claude re-reads every decision that is not DONE. **No ACK within about two hours of an awake session, or no movement once its turn has come → a nudge (at most one an hour per stall); after two nudges with no movement → Kyle hears in one line.**
7. **Started-but-unfinished watch.** The same check lists every batch with a Step commit and no close. One untouched for 7 days with no recorded decision to park or cancel it → PM Claude asks the owner where it stands and brings Kyle a decision: resume at a named place, park with a reason, or cancel. **Parking or cancelling is itself a decision and is recorded here.**
8. **Changes.** A decision is never edited silently. Reversed or replaced → its status becomes `REVERSED by KD-m` and the new decision gets its own number.
9. **Slot check and the `kind` column (KD-28, P1d).** A decision that slots, assigns an owner to, or orders work has `kind` = `placement` and cites `slot-check: <record path>` (or `no-slot-check: <reason>`); every other new row is `other`; `pre-guard` is only the backfill of KD-1 to KD-28. The slot-check record (`node scripts/pm/slot-check.mjs`) is committed in the same commit as its decision row. `binds` for new rows: session aliases separated by `;` (e.g. `OLD Claude; NEW Claude`), `(released)` after a session that no longer holds it, `all four` for every coding session. The status filter convention is "not DONE": the slot check lists every decision whose status is anything but DONE, never a whitelist of status words.

**Status words:** `RELAYED` · `ACKED` · `IN PLAN` · `IN WORK` · `DONE` · `WAITING` · `REVERSED by KD-n`
